## 🌿 Coriander–Basil–Green Chilli Chutney (Fresh, Aromatic, Sharp)

### 🧾 Ingredients (Serves ~4)
- Fresh **coriander leaves** – 1 cup (packed)
- Fresh **basil leaves** – ½ cup (adds depth + slight sweetness)
- **Green chillies** – 1–2 (adjust heat)
- **Garlic** – 1–2 cloves *(optional but powerful)*
- **Ginger** – ½ inch
- **Roasted peanuts** or **cashews** – 2 tbsp *(for body + balance)*
- **Lemon juice** – 1–2 tbsp *(or tamarind small piece)*
- **Salt** – to taste
- **Water** – little (for grinding)

---

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step (5–7 mins total)

1. **Prep**
   - Wash coriander & basil well  
   - Remove thick stems (keep tender ones)

2. **Blend Base**
   - Add coriander, basil, green chilli, ginger, garlic into mixer

3. **Add Body**
   - Add peanuts/cashews → gives creamy texture (no watery chutney)

4. **Balance**
   - Add salt + lemon juice (or tamarind)

5. **Grind**
   - Add very little water → grind to smooth / slightly coarse

6. **Taste Adjust**
   - Too sharp? → add more nuts  
   - Too bland? → add salt + lemon  
   - Too spicy? → add more coriander/basil  

---

### 🧠 Flavor Logic (why this works)
- **Coriander** → freshness + base  
- **Basil** → aromatic sweetness (slightly Italian touch)  
- **Green chilli** → heat  
- **Nuts** → thickness + mouthfeel  
- **Lemon/Tamarind** → brightness (cuts bitterness)

---

### 🔁 Variations (based on your need)

| Style | Change |
|------|--------|
| **South Indian style** | Add coconut (2 tbsp) |
| **Street sandwich chutney** | Add mint + little sugar |
| **Protein boost** | Add roasted chana dal |
| **No garlic version** | Skip garlic, increase ginger |

---

### 🍽️ Best Uses
- Idli / dosa / paniyaram  
- Sandwich spread  
- Rice mix (add little oil → instant coriander rice feel)  
- Side for grilled chicken / paneer  

---

### ⏱️ Time Estimate
- Prep: 3 mins  
- Grinding: 2 mins  
- Adjusting: 1–2 mins

# 🧊 3. STORAGE STRATEGY (3 OPTIONS)

---

## 🥇 Option A: **Fridge (5–7 Days) – Daily Use System**

### Steps:
1. Transfer to **glass container**
2. Pour **thin oil layer (1–2 tsp)** on top
3. Close airtight

### Daily Usage Protocol:
- ❌ Don’t mix entire jar daily  
- ✅ Take small portion → dilute with water when needed  

### Why it works:
- Oil blocks air → prevents oxidation (like sealing a dataset)

---

## 🥈 Option B: **Freeze Cubes (15–30 Days)**

### Steps:
1. Fill ice tray with thick chutney
2. Freeze overnight
3. Transfer cubes to zip bag

### Usage:
- Take 1–2 cubes  
- Thaw + add water/lemon → ready

### Advantage:
- No spoilage  
- Portion control  
- Zero daily contamination
