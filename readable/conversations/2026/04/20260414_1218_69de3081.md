# 🧩 1. Types of Credit Card Offers

## 💳 Balance Transfer (BT)

**What it is:**
- Move existing credit card debt → new card with **0% interest for X months**

**Typical UK terms:**
- 0% for **12–30 months**
- Fee: **2%–4%** of transferred amount

**Example:**
- Transfer £5,000  
- Fee = 3% → £150  
- No interest for 24 months

**Use case:**
✔ Reduce interest on existing debt  
✔ Structured payoff plan

# ⚙️ 2. How People “Make Money” Using Credit Cards

This is where it becomes interesting—but also dangerous if misunderstood.

---

## 🧠 Strategy 1: Stoozing (Classic UK method)

**Flow:**
1. Get 0% purchase card  
2. Spend using card instead of cash  
3. Keep your cash in **high-interest savings**

**Example:**
- Spend £5,000 on card  
- Put £5,000 in savings @ 5%  
- Earn ~£250 interest  
- Pay card before 0% ends  

✔ **Profit = interest earned – fees (usually zero here)**

---

## 🧠 Strategy 2: Money Transfer Arbitrage

**Flow:**
1. Take MT £5,000 (fee ~4% = £200)  
2. Invest or save at > fee return  

**Reality check:**
- Savings rates (~4–5%) barely beat fees  
- Profit margin = very thin or zero  

👉 Only works if:
- You have **higher return investment**
- OR special low-fee promo

---

## 🧠 Strategy 3: Balance Transfer Ladder

**Flow:**
1. Move debt → 0% card  
2. Before expiry → move again to new 0% card  

✔ Keeps debt interest-free for years  

⚠️ But:
- Fees accumulate  
- Needs excellent credit score  

---

## 🧠 Strategy 4: Cashback + Rewards Optimization

- Use cards with cashback (0.5%–5%)  
- Pay in full monthly  

✔ Risk-free gain  
✔ No interest involved  

---

# ⚠️ 3. Reality Check (Important)

This is where most people go wrong.

### ❌ Hidden Risks
- Missing 1 payment → **0% cancelled immediately**
- After promo → **20%–30% APR**
- High utilization → credit score drop
- Fees eat profits fast
