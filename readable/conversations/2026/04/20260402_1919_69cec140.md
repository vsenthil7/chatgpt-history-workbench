## 🧄 Garlic Chutney (South Indian Style)

image_group{"aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["garlic chutney south indian red chutney","poondu chutney tamil style","garlic chutney dosa idli side","spicy garlic chutney texture close up"],"num_per_query":1}

### 🔹 What this is
A bold, spicy, slightly tangy chutney — perfect for **idli, dosa, chapati, or even rice**. In Tamil homes, this is often called *Poondu Chutney*.

---

## 🧾 Ingredients
- Garlic – 10–15 cloves  
- Dry red chillies – 4–6 (adjust spice)  
- Tamarind – small marble size  
- Onion – 1 small (optional but adds body)  
- Tomato – 1 medium (optional for tanginess)  
- Salt – to taste  
- Oil – 1–2 tbsp  

---

## 👨‍🍳 Method (15–20 mins)

### 1. Roast base
- Heat oil  
- Add garlic + red chillies  
- Sauté till garlic turns slightly golden  
- Add onion + tomato → cook till soft  

### 2. Grind
- Cool slightly  
- Add tamarind + salt  
- Grind to **coarse or smooth paste** (your preference)  
- Add little water only if needed  

### 3. Optional tempering (recommended)
- Heat 1 tsp oil  
- Add mustard seeds + curry leaves  
- Pour over chutney  

---

## ⚖️ Taste Profile
- 🔥 Spicy  
- 🧄 Strong garlic punch  
- ⚡ Slight tang (if tomato/tamarind used)  

---

## 🔁 Variations
- **No tomato version** → more shelf life, stronger garlic taste  
- **Add coconut** → milder, thicker chutney  
- **Add jaggery pinch** → balances spice (hotel-style)  

---

## 🧊 Storage (your style – batch prep ready)
- **Fridge:** 3–5 days (without coconut lasts longer)  
- **Oil layer on top:** extends life  
- **Freeze:** grind thick → freeze in cubes → use as needed  

---

## ⏱️ Quick Workflow (Efficient Prep)
- Peel garlic in bulk → store in fridge  
- Roast chilli + garlic → cool → freeze base  
- Grind fresh when needed → 2 min chutney

## 🧄🌶️ Garlic Green Chilli Chutney (Fresh, Spicy Version)

### 🔹 What this is
A **fresh, sharp, green chutney** — more *raw heat + aroma* compared to red chutney. Perfect for **idli, dosa, curd rice, or even as sandwich spread**.

---

## 🧾 Ingredients
- Garlic – 8–12 cloves  
- Green chillies – 4–8 (adjust heat)  
- Coconut – 2–3 tbsp *(optional but recommended for balance)*  
- Tamarind – small piece OR 1 tsp lemon juice  
- Roasted chana dal (pottukadalai) – 1–2 tbsp *(for thickness)*  
- Salt – to taste  
- Oil – 1 tsp  

---

## 👨‍🍳 Method (10–15 mins)

### 1. Light sauté (key step)
- Heat 1 tsp oil  
- Add garlic + green chillies  
- Sauté **just 1–2 mins** (don’t brown → keeps green color & fresh taste)

### 2. Grind
- Add coconut + roasted chana dal + tamarind/lemon + salt  
- Grind to smooth or slightly coarse  
- Add little water for consistency  

### 3. Optional tempering
- Mustard seeds + curry leaves in hot oil  
- Pour on top  

---

## ⚖️ Taste Profile
- 🌶️ Sharp, fresh spice (different from dry chilli heat)  
- 🧄 Strong garlic aroma  
- 🥥 Mild creaminess (if coconut used)  
- ⚡ Slight tang  

---

## 🔁 Variations
- **Raw version (no sauté):** stronger, more pungent (good for immunity style)  
- **Mint/coriander add-on:** turns into hybrid green chutney  
- **No coconut:** more shelf life, but sharper taste  
- **Add curd:** creamy dip version  

---

## 🧊 Storage + Batch System (your style)

### 🔹 Fridge
- With coconut → 2–3 days  
- Without coconut → 4–5 days  

### 🔹 Freeze (best method)
- Grind **thick paste (no water)**  
- Freeze in cube tray  
- Use 1–2 cubes → dilute fresh  

### 🔹 Oil layer trick
- Add thin oil layer on top → slows spoilage  

---

## ⏱️ Efficient Workflow (like system thinking)

- Pre-sauté garlic + chilli → refrigerate base  
- Grind fresh in 2 mins when needed
