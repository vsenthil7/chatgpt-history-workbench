## 🥔 Classic Tamil-Style Potato Fry (Urulai Roast)

image_group{"layout":"carousel","aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["South Indian potato fry crispy","Tamil style urulai roast","spicy potato fry in kadai","crispy potato roast curry leaves mustard seeds"],"num_per_query":1}

### 🔹 Ingredients (Serves 2–3)

- Potato – 3 medium (≈ 350–400 g)
- Oil or ghee – 2 tbsp  
- Mustard seeds – ½ tsp  
- Urad dal – 1 tsp  
- Curry leaves – few  
- Turmeric powder – ¼ tsp  
- Red chilli powder – ¾ to 1 tsp  
- Salt – as needed  
- Optional:  
  - Crushed pepper – ¼ tsp (for heat depth)  
  - Garlic – 3 cloves crushed  
  - Sambar powder – ½ tsp (hotel touch)

---

## 🔥 Step-by-Step Method (No Guesswork)

### 1️⃣ Parboil First (Important)
- Cut potatoes into medium cubes.
- Boil in salted water **7–8 mins only**.
- They should be **70% cooked**, not fully soft.
- Drain completely. Let steam dry 5 minutes.

👉 This prevents mushy fry.

---

### 2️⃣ Tempering
Heat kadai on medium.

Add:
- Oil/ghee  
- Mustard seeds (let splutter)  
- Urad dal (light golden)  
- Curry leaves  
- Garlic (if using)

---

### 3️⃣ Spice Activation
Lower flame.

Add:
- Turmeric  
- Chilli powder  
- (Optional pepper / sambar powder)

Immediately add potatoes and mix well.

---

### 4️⃣ Slow Roast Phase (Critical Stage)

- Keep flame **low-medium**.
- Do NOT stir continuously.
- Let one side crisp 3–4 minutes.
- Flip gently.
- Total roasting: **12–15 minutes**.

Finish when:
✔ Edges brown  
✔ Slight crisp crust  
✔ Inside soft  

---

## 🧠 Why This Works (Science Touch)

- Parboiling gelatinizes starch.
- Slow roasting evaporates surface moisture.
- Minimal stirring allows caramelization.
- Pepper gives sharp heat; chilli gives body heat.

---

## 🌶 Variations Based on Mood

**Temple style (no onion, no garlic)**  
→ Use only chilli + turmeric + curry leaf.

**Hotel style**  
→ Add ½ tsp sambar powder + pinch garam masala at end.

**Extra crispy version**  
→ Add 1 tsp rice flour while roasting.

---

## 🍽 Best With

- Lemon rice  
- Rasam rice  
- Curd rice  
- Sambar sadham  

---
