## Ingredients
- **Milk** – any (cow / buffalo / full-fat gives thicker curd)
- **Starter yogurt** – ½ to 1 tsp (plain, unsweetened, live culture)

---

## Step-by-step (science-first, kitchen-simple)

### 1️⃣ Boil the milk  
Bring milk to a **full boil** (rising foam), then simmer **2–3 minutes**.  
**Why:**  
- Kills unwanted microbes  
- Denatures proteins → thicker yogurt

⏱️ *5–7 minutes*

---

### 2️⃣ Cool to lukewarm  
Let milk cool until **warm but not hot** (≈ **40–45 °C**).  
Finger test: you can hold your finger **comfortably for 5 seconds**.

**Too hot = bacteria die**  
**Too cold = fermentation slows**

⏱️ *10–15 minutes*

---

### 3️⃣ Add starter (less is more)  
- Take **½–1 tsp yogurt**
- Mix with a little warm milk first
- Pour back and stir gently (once or twice only)

**Why:**  
Over-starter = sour, thin, grainy curd.

---

### 4️⃣ Ferment (don’t disturb)  
Cover loosely. Keep warm.

- Ideal temp: **25–35 °C**
- Time:
  - **6–8 hrs** → mild
  - **8–12 hrs** → medium
  - **12+ hrs** → sour & probiotic-heavy

📍 Warm spots:
- Oven (light ON)
- Wrapped in cloth
- Near stove / warm shelf

⏱️ *Hands-off time*

---

### 5️⃣ Refrigerate  
Once set, **refrigerate immediately**.  
This **stops fermentation** and stabilizes texture.

---

## Quick troubleshooting
- **Watery curd** → milk not boiled enough / starter too much / shaken  
- **Slimy or bitter** → over-fermented or contaminated starter  
- **Didn’t set** → milk too hot or too cold when starter added  

---

## Pro tips (from fermentation logic)
- Always use **plain curd as starter**, not flavored
- Use a **fresh batch starter every 5–7 days**
- For **thick curd**: boil longer + don’t move while setting
- For **drinking curd/buttermilk**: ferment longer, then whisk
