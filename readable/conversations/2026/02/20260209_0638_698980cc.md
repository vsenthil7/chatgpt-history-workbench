That stored state is the **KV-cache**.

## Why KV-cache exists (the core reason)

Transformers are expensive because:
- Every new token attends to **all previous tokens**
- Recomputing attention is costly

KV-cache allows:
- Past tokens’ attention representations to be **reused**
- Only the *new token* needs fresh computation

This gives:
- ⚡ Much faster generation
- 💸 Much cheaper inference
- 🧠 Longer practical conversations

That’s why IBM and OpenAI highlight it.
