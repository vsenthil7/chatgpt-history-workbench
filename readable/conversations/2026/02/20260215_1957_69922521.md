## 🍋 Lemon Rice (Elumichai Sadam) – Balanced, Non-Sticky Version

Since you usually prefer **clean structure + exact ratios**, here is a precise version.

---

## 🧂 Ingredients (2 servings)

- Cooked rice – **2 cups** (cooled, grains separate)
- Lemon juice – **2 tbsp** (fresh)
- Turmeric powder – **¼ tsp**
- Salt – **¾ tsp** (adjust)
- Ghee or oil – **2 tbsp**

### Tempering
- Mustard seeds – **½ tsp**
- Urad dal – **1 tsp**
- Channa dal – **1 tsp**
- Peanuts – **2 tbsp**
- Green chilli – **1–2 slit**
- Dry red chilli – **1**
- Curry leaves – **1 sprig**
- Hing (asafoetida) – **1 pinch**

Optional:
- Ginger finely chopped – **½ tsp**
- Cashew – 6 pieces

---

## 🔥 Step-by-Step Method

### 1️⃣ Rice Prep (Most Important)
- Rice must be **completely cooled**.
- Spread on plate.
- Add **¼ tsp turmeric + little salt** and gently mix.
  
👉 This prevents yellow patches later.

---

### 2️⃣ Tempering (Flavor Core)
Heat ghee.

Add in order:
1. Mustard (let crackle)
2. Urad + channa dal (light golden)
3. Peanuts (roast well)
4. Green chilli + red chilli
5. Curry leaves
6. Hing
7. Ginger (optional)

Turn OFF flame.

---

### 3️⃣ Lemon Activation
Let tempering cool for **30–40 seconds** (important).
Now add lemon juice.

👉 If added on high heat → lemon becomes slightly bitter.

---

### 4️⃣ Combine
Pour tempering over rice.
Mix gently (do not mash).

Rest 10 minutes before serving.

---

## 🌿 Why Lemon Rice Tastes Better After 30 Minutes?

Acid (lemon) slowly penetrates starch surface.
Oil layer coats grains.
Turmeric stabilizes colour + mild antimicrobial action.

It becomes more unified — not sharp, not raw.

---

## 🧠 Digestive Logic (You like this part)

- Lemon → increases salivary enzymes.
- Turmeric → anti-inflammatory.
- Tempered fats → slow glucose spike.
- Peanuts → add protein + reduce glycemic load.

Compared to plain rice:
More stable energy release.

# 🍚 1️⃣ If Rice is **Freshly Cooked (Hot Now)**

⚠️ Most sensitive stage.

### What to do:
- Spread rice on wide plate.
- Add **½ tsp oil or ghee**.
- Let cool **15–20 mins minimum**.
- Do NOT add lemon to hot rice.

### Lemon quantity:
➡️ Use **1.5 tbsp only first**  
After mixing, taste. Add remaining ½ tbsp if needed.

Why?
Hot rice absorbs acid aggressively → can become sharp later.

Papad or mild potato fry.

# 🔥 Method (Important Difference from Rice)

### 1️⃣ Temper First
Do full tempering in ghee.

### 2️⃣ Add Water Directly to Tempering
Pour **1¾ cups water** into same pan.
Add salt + turmeric.
Bring to boil.

### 3️⃣ Add Semiya
Lower flame.
Add roasted semiya gradually.
Stir gently.

Cover and cook **4–5 minutes**.

Turn OFF when just cooked (not mushy).

---

### 4️⃣ Lemon Stage (Critical)
Let it rest **2 minutes**.
Then add lemon juice.
Mix gently.

👉 Never add lemon while boiling — semiya becomes slightly sticky + dull.

---

# 🧠 Rice vs Semiya – Structural Difference

| Factor | Rice | Semiya |
|--------|------|--------|
| Starch density | High | Medium |
| Absorption speed | Slow | Fast |
| Overcook risk | Low | High |
| Lemon penetration | Surface | Full strand |

Semiya absorbs faster → so slightly **less lemon than rice**.

---

# 🌿 Texture Control Tips

If dry → sprinkle 1 tsp hot water + cover 2 min.  
If sticky → add 1 tsp sesame oil + fluff with fork.  
If sharp → add 1 tsp ghee to mellow acidity.
