That fiery, crunchy South-Indian classic—here’s a **reliable home version** that hits the restaurant vibe without mystery powders.

---

## Ingredients (serves 2–3)

**Paneer**
- Paneer cubes – 200 g (medium, not tiny)

**Marinade**
- Cornflour – 2 tbsp  
- Rice flour – 1 tbsp (for extra crisp)
- Ginger-garlic paste – 1 tsp  
- Red chilli powder – 1 to 1½ tsp (adjust heat)
- Kashmiri chilli powder – ½ tsp (color, optional)
- Turmeric – a pinch  
- Lemon juice – 1 tsp  
- Salt – to taste  
- Water – just enough to coat thickly

**Tempering / Toss**
- Oil – for shallow/deep fry  
- Garlic – 6–8 cloves, sliced  
- Green chillies – 2–3, slit  
- Curry leaves – 1 sprig  
- Optional: crushed fennel – a pinch (Tamil Nadu touch)

---

## Method

1. **Marinate (5 min)**  
   Mix all marinade ingredients to a *thick batter*. Coat paneer gently. Rest 5 minutes.

2. **Fry (4–5 min)**  
   Fry in medium-hot oil until **golden and crisp outside**, soft inside. Remove and drain.

3. **Temper & Toss (2 min)**  
   In 1 tbsp hot oil, add garlic → green chillies → curry leaves (crackle!).  
   Add fried paneer, toss fast. Finish with a squeeze of lemon if you like brightness.

4. **Serve hot**  
   Onion rings + lemon wedges on the side. 🔥

---

## Chef Notes (quick fixes)
- **Paneer turning hard?** Fry quickly; don’t overcook.  
- **Too spicy?** Add a pinch of sugar or more lemon.  
- **No cornflour?** Use all rice flour (still crispy, lighter crunch).  
- **Air-fryer option:** Spray oil, 180 °C for 8–10 min, toss halfway; then do the tempering.
