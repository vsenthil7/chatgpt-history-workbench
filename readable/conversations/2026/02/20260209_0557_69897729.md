### 🥣 Simple Morning Mor Rasam (1 serving)

**Ingredients**
- Buttermilk (mor) – **250 ml** (thin, not thick)
- Water – **100 ml**
- Cumin (jeera) – **½ tsp** (crushed)
- Green chilli – **½** (crushed) *(optional morning)*
- Ginger – **½ tsp** (crushed)
- Curry leaves – **6–8**
- Turmeric – **1 pinch**
- Salt – to taste

**Tempering (optional but nice)**
- Ghee – **½ tsp**
- Mustard seeds – **¼ tsp**
- Cumin – **¼ tsp**
- Dry red chilli – **½**

### 👩‍🍳 Method
1. Mix **buttermilk + water + salt + turmeric**.
2. Add **crushed jeera, ginger, green chilli, curry leaves**.
3. Heat on **very low flame** → **DO NOT BOIL**.  
   Stop when steam comes & aroma rises.
4. Optional: do quick tempering in ghee, pour on top.
5. Rest **2 minutes**, then drink.

### 🌿 Morning Logic (why mor rasam)
- Cooling (unlike pepper/tomato rasam)
- Rehydrates gut after night fast
- Gentle probiotics
- No acidity spike

### ❌ Morning avoid
- Tomato
- Pepper
- Garlic (save for lunch rasam)
- Heavy chilli

### 🕖 Best time
- **6:00–9:00 AM**, empty stomach or with kanji.

## 🌅 MORNING RASAM (5–9 AM)
**Purpose:** Wake digestion gently, cool gut

**Base**
- Buttermilk (mor) + water

**Use**
- Jeera (whole or powder)
- Ginger
- *Tiny* green chilli (optional)
- Curry leaves
- Turmeric, salt

**Avoid**
- ❌ Tomato  
- ❌ Pepper  
- ❌ Garlic  
- ❌ Heavy chilli  
- ❌ Boiling

**Heat**
- Very low flame → steam only

**Tempering**
- ❌ Not needed  
- Optional: ¼ tsp ghee *after* switching off

👉 This is **drinkable rasam**, not rice rasam.

---

## ☀️ LUNCH RASAM (12–2 PM)
**Purpose:** Proper digestion for heavy meals

**Base**
- Tomato + water (or tamarind-light)

**Use**
- Jeera
- Pepper
- Garlic (2–3 cloves)
- Red chilli
- Curry leaves, coriander

**Heat**
- Proper boil (2–3 mins simmer)

**Tempering**
- ✅ Must (ghee/oil)
  - Mustard + jeera + dry chilli

👉 This is **rice rasam**. Strong is good.

---

## 🌙 NIGHT RASAM (7–9 PM)
**Purpose:** Gas relief, light cleanse, sleep-friendly

**Base**
- Water or very thin tomato (optional)

**Use**
- Jeera
- Pepper (less)
- Ginger
- Garlic (1 small clove optional)

**Avoid**
- ❌ Too much chilli  
- ❌ Very sour tomato/tamarind

**Heat**
- Gentle simmer, not aggressive boil

**Tempering**
- Optional, very light

👉 Sip slowly. Small quantity only.
