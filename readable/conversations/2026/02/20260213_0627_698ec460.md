## 🌾 1) Instant Wheat Dosai (Soft Morning Version)

### 🧂 Ingredients (1–2 persons)
- Whole wheat flour – 1 cup (120 g)
- Water – 1.5 to 2 cups (thin batter)
- Salt – ½ tsp
- Jeera – ½ tsp
- Green chilli – 1 (finely chopped)
- Curry leaves – few (optional)
- Onion – 2 tbsp finely chopped (optional)
- Ghee – for cooking

⏱️ Prep Time: 5 mins  
⏱️ Cooking Time: 10 mins  

---

### 🔥 Method (Important Texture Logic)

1. **Mix batter**
   - Add water gradually.
   - Batter must be **thin like rava dosa batter** — slightly watery, not thick.
   - Rest 5 minutes.

2. **Heat tawa properly**
   - Medium-high.
   - Lightly smear ghee.

3. **Pour like rava dosa**
   - Do NOT spread thick.
   - Pour from outside → inside in circular motion.
   - Small holes are normal.

4. **Drizzle ghee**
   - Cook till golden edges.
   - Flip only if needed (thin version usually one-side enough).

---

### 🧠 Texture Science
- Thin batter → lace texture  
- Thick batter → soft chapati-like dosa  
- High heat first → crisp edges  
- Medium finish → inside cooks without raw wheat smell  

---

## 🌾 2) Soft Dinner Version (Slightly Thick)

If you want soft, chapati-style dosa:

- Reduce water (1.25 cup)
- Spread gently like normal dosa
- Cook both sides
- Add little extra ghee

Good for:
- With curd
- With mild chutney
- For night digestion

---

## 🌿 Optional Upgrade (Hotel Touch)
Add:
- 1 tbsp rice flour → crispy
- 1 tbsp curd → softness
- Pinch sugar → colour

|------|---------|------|
| Morning | Thin crispy | Light on stomach |
| Lunch | Medium | Filling |
| Night | Soft | Easy digestion |

## 🌾 Where each ingredient goes (and why)

### 🟤 1️⃣ Jeera – ½ tsp  
👉 **Add directly into batter. Raw.**  
- Do NOT temper separately.  
- It releases aroma while dosa cooks.  
- Helps digestion (wheat can feel heavy otherwise).

---

### 🌶️ 2️⃣ Green chilli – finely chopped  
👉 Add into batter.  
- If you want mild → remove seeds.  
- If you want visible flavour pockets → don’t grind.

---

### 🌿 3️⃣ Curry leaves  
👉 Finely chop and add into batter.  
- If whole leaves → they may burn.  
- Chopped gives aroma without bitterness.

---

### 🧅 4️⃣ Onion  
👉 Add into batter **after mixing water**.  
- Don’t grind.  
- Spread slightly when pouring dosa so onion cooks evenly.  
- If batter too thick, onion won’t cook properly.

---

### 🧈 5️⃣ Ghee  
👉 ONLY while cooking on tawa.  
- ½ tsp around edges.  
- Little on top after pouring.  
- Don’t mix ghee into batter.

---

## 🔥 Exact Order (Important)

1. Wheat flour  
2. Salt  
3. Jeera  
4. Green chilli  
5. Curry leaves  
6. Add water → make thin batter  
7. Add onion last  
8. Rest 5 mins  
9. Cook with ghee

---

## ⚠️ Common Mistake You Usually Do 😄
- Batter too thick → raw wheat smell  
- Onion too big → stays uncooked  
- Low flame → becomes rubbery  

Start medium-high → then reduce slightly.

### ✅ Can add — but VERY little
- Just a **small pinch (not ½ tsp!)**
- Too much → bitter + strange colour in wheat dosa

### Why add?
- Mild earth flavour
- Slight golden colour
- Anti-inflammatory benefit (minor effect at this quantity)

👉 Mix directly into batter.

### ✅ Good addition — especially with ginger
- Add **¼ tsp freshly crushed**
- Not too much — wheat + excess pepper = harsh taste

### Why good?
- Improves digestion  
- Adds warm heat (not chilli heat)  
- Balances onion sweetness  

👉 Add directly into batter.
