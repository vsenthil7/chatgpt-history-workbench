- **Activity 1 - Icebreaker - Mood Charades (5 mins)**
    
    *This activity makes you and the student comfortable with each other.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. Ask the student:
        1. Pick an emotion. (Happy, Sad, Angry, etc.)
        2. Share a situation when they felt this emotion.
            1. **Example:** Happy → I was very happy when I got good marks in my exam.
    2. Share a situation in which you feel an emotion that was chosen by the student.
    3. Now, you pick an emotion and repeat the above.
    4. Help the student wherever required.
    
- **Activity 2 - Active Listening (20 mins)**
    
    *This activity helps the student to practice their listening skills.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. Take a minute to recollect a story or a topic you are interested in.
    2. Tell a story or talk about the topic for 2 to 3 minutes.
    3. Once done, ask the student to summarise what you have said.
    4. Next, ask the student a few questions about what you told them to test their listening.
        1. **Example Questions:**
            1. How did I start the story or topic?
            2. It was about whom?
            3. Did I mention my name?
    5. Repeat the same with another story or topic, if time permits.
    6. Help the student wherever required.

# Step-by-Step Event Flow

## 1. Session Start
- Mentor greets the student.
- Mentor briefly introduces the topic of the session: **Inter-Personal Skills**.
- Mentor explains that the session will contain **multiple activities to practice communication and listening**.

---

# 2. Activity 1 – Icebreaker: Mood Charades (5 minutes)

### Mentor instructions to student
- Mentor asks the student to **pick an emotion**.  
  Examples:
  - Happy
  - Sad
  - Angry

### Student response
- Student chooses an emotion.

### Student explanation
- Mentor asks the student to **share a situation when they felt that emotion**.

Example structure:
- Emotion → Situation  
- Example:  
  - Happy → I was very happy when I got good marks in my exam.

### Mentor participation
- Mentor then **shares their own situation** where they felt the same emotion chosen by the student.

### Role reversal
- Mentor now **picks an emotion**.
- Student shares a situation related to that emotion.

### Mentor support
- Mentor helps the student wherever required.

---

# 3. Activity 2 – Active Listening (20 minutes)

### Mentor preparation
- Mentor takes **a minute to recollect a story or topic of interest**.

### Story narration
- Mentor **tells a story or explains a topic for 2–3 minutes**.

### Listening test
- After the story is finished:

Mentor asks the student to **summarise what was said**.

### Follow-up listening questions
Mentor asks questions such as:

1. **How did I start the story or topic?**
2. **It was about whom?**
3. **Did I mention my name?**

### Repeat if time permits
- Mentor may **tell another story/topic**.
- Student again summarises and answers questions.

### Mentor support
- Mentor helps the student wherever required.

---

## Activity Reflection Questions

Mentor asks the student the following questions:

### Question 1
**Did you find it easy to listen to and understand what I was saying?**

Possible answer:  
- Yes, you explained it very well.

---

### Question 2
**What emotions or feelings did you sense during the story or topic?**

Example response format:  
- I felt a sense of **[emotion]** when you mentioned **[specific part]**.

---

### Question 3
**Which part of the story or topic stood out to you the most?**

- Student shares their response.

---

### Question 4
**Did you enjoy the activity?**

- Student shares their experience.

---

# 4. Activity 3 – Teamwork Art (15 minutes)

### Observation
- Mentor observes the **given picture**.

### Important rule
- Mentor **must not show or describe the picture directly to the student**.

### Instruction phase
Mentor gives **step-by-step verbal drawing instructions**.

Example:
- Draw a half circle.
- Draw **7 half-circles below the first one**.

### Drawing phase
- Student follows the instructions and draws.

### Guessing phase
- Student guesses the **object they drew**.

### Role reversal
- Student now gives instructions.
- Mentor draws.

### Guessing again
- Mentor guesses the object.

### Mentor support
- Mentor helps the student wherever required.

*(The reference picture used in the activity is a **rainbow**.)*

---

# 5. Activity 4 – Homework of the Day (5 minutes)

### Homework discussion
- Mentor discusses **previous session homework** with the student.

### New homework explanation
Mentor explains the new homework task.

### Homework task

**Title:**  
_When I was not listening properly_

Student must:

1. Write **two scenarios** where they made a mistake because they **did not listen properly**.

2. For each scenario:
   - Explain **what happened**.
   - Explain **what they learned**.
   - Provide **a solution for the future**.

### Encouragement
- Mentor encourages the student to complete the homework.
- Mentor provides examples or tips if required.

### Homework reminder
- Mentor informs the student that the homework **will be discussed in the next session**.

---

# 6. Session Closing Reminders

After the session:

1. Mentor asks the student to **mark today's attendance**.
2. Mentor **marks their own attendance**.
