# 5. Best way to reconnect after exams

## Reconnection principle

Do **not** start with:

- tasks
- discipline
- “what next?”
- earning ideas
- improvement plans
- too many questions
- “show properly”

Start with **shared lightness + non-demanding attention**.

She likely needs:
1. **presence**
2. **ease**
3. **choice**
4. only later **structure**

---

## Phase 1 – First reconnection: “presence before purpose”
For the first reconnect, the goal is **not productivity**.  
The goal is: **make interaction feel emotionally safe again**.

Best style:

- light tone
- short duration
- no over-analysis
- no correction
- no extraction of information
- no hidden agenda

Good topics:

- last day of school feeling
- funniest thing in class
- what food she wants
- who signed shirts / memories / photos
- best and worst part of the school year
- art only if she brings it or shows it voluntarily

Best emotional message:
> “I’m here, I’m interested, and I’m not trying to control the moment.”

---

## Phase 2 – Gentle bridge through her world
After that, reconnect through **her interests**, not your plans.

Use what she already signalled:

- art
- outings
- friends
- food
- holiday mood

Examples of good bridge style:

- “What was the best part of the last day?”
- “Which friend made you laugh the most?”
- “What food do you want in holidays?”
- “Do you want to do something fun with your art later?”

Notice: these are invitations, not demands.

---

## Phase 3 – Small shared ritual
A small ritual is better than a big “program.”

Examples:

- one photo a day of something funny / pretty / creative
- one small art share whenever she feels like it
- one snack-rating idea
- one “holiday mood” check
- one casual walk / shop / outing memory collection

This gives continuity without pressure.
