    3. Give the laptop to your friend at the weekend.

   **“A customer sees a pen but refuses to buy it. How will you convince them to buy the pen?”**

4. Student thinks and gives their answer.  
5. Mentor listens and encourages the student to explain their reasoning.  
6. Mentor shares their own answer or example strategy.

Possible discussion points:

- Explain the usefulness of the pen.  
- Offer a discount or bundle offer.  
- Highlight quality or special features.  
- Build value so the customer changes their decision.

7. Mentor appreciates the student’s creativity and participation.

# 4. Summary Discussion – Negotiation Process

Mentor asks the student:

**“What steps did you notice in all these questions?”**

Student tries to recall the negotiation flow.

Mentor explains the **4 Steps of Negotiation**:

1. **Prepare** – Understand the situation and people’s perspectives.  
2. **Discuss** – Talk and share viewpoints.  
3. **Propose and Bargain** – Suggest solutions and adjust.  
4. **Close the Deal** – Finalise agreement.

# 5. Activity 3 – Plan a Trip (15 mins)

Mentor introduces the negotiation scenario.

Scenario explanation:

- Mentor prefers **outdoor activities** (trekking, hiking, sightseeing).  
- Student prefers **indoor activities** (reading, relaxing, eating good food).

---

## Brainstorm Phase (5 minutes)

1. Mentor and student think of ideas.  
2. Both suggest activities they enjoy.  

Examples discussed:

- Visiting a scenic place with cafes.  
- Hiking to a viewpoint then relaxing.  
- Exploring a city with food stops.

---

## Negotiation Phase (8 minutes)

1. Mentor and student negotiate ideas.  
2. Both suggest compromises.  
3. Mentor encourages the student to explain their arguments.  
4. Student tries to balance both interests.

Example outcome:

- Morning sightseeing or light trekking.  
- Afternoon relaxing at a café or scenic place.

---

## Reflection

Mentor asks:

- Would this trip be enjoyable for both?  
- What negotiation strategies worked?

Mentor gives feedback on the student’s negotiation approach.

- Bargaining in a market.  
- Convincing parents about something.

Student must write:

- What worked well.  
- Why the negotiation succeeded.

---

### Scenario 2 – Negotiation that failed or required compromise

Student must write:

- What went wrong.  
- Why agreement was difficult.

Mentor explains that these reflections help **improve real-life negotiation skills**.
