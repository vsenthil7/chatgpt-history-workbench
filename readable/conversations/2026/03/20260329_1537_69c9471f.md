Should I Learn Blockchain in 2025? Is It Still Worth It After 4 Years of Watching the Space? 😵‍💫

Hey all 😁😁😁
I’ve been a blockchain enthusiast since 2022. There was a lot of hype back then, and like many others, I jumped into it full of excitement. But in 2024, I paused my learning journey — mainly because I got caught up with startup work.

Today, I decided to revisit the space and ask myself:
"Should I continue learning blockchain? Or is it just another overhyped buzzword?"
After 4 years of observing this domain closely, here’s my take:

Let’s start with the good stuff:
Blockchain isn’t dead — it’s evolving.
In the early days (especially during the lockdown crypto boom), it was all "blockchain this, blockchain that"... but no one really knew what to do with it. It was all hype, with very few real-world apps.

That’s one reason I paused learning — I couldn’t find any blockchain application that I was using daily (besides a crypto exchange app sitting idle on my phone). Maybe you were using one, but I wasn’t.


But now? 😎 Things are changing: 

Asset tokenization (like real estate, stocks, art) is actually being used, not just discussed.

CBDCs (Central Bank Digital Currencies) are rolling out in multiple countries.

Enterprise adoption is real – big companies are using private chains for logistics, finance, and supply chain.

AI + Blockchain is picking up — projects like Bittensor (TAO) and Fetch.ai are building AI-native blockchains.

Layer 2s (zkSync, Optimism, Base) are way more dev-friendly now — cheaper, faster, scalable.

Basically, the space is shifting from "talking about ideas" to actually building useful stuff.



But yeah, 💀 some issues are still there:

Scams and rugpulls? Still exist, but they’re smarter and harder to detect.
User experience? Still nowhere near Web2 levels, though it’s getting better.
Regulations? A mixed bag — some countries are clearer now, some are worse.


So… is it worth learning blockchain now?
Absolutely. There’s no better time than now.

But here’s the real talk:

Only learn blockchain if you’re okay with:

Playing the long game — adoption is happening, but it’s slow.

Staying adaptable — this space moves insanely fast, and dev tools evolve constantly.

If that sounds exciting to you, then jump back in. If you're only here for quick money or trends, maybe wait.

That’s it — just wanted to share my 4-year perspective. Hope it helps someone thinking the same 😇😊

Even though things got really bad, we still submitted our project within the deadline and started working on the presentation. Sadly, some of our friends couldn’t submit their projects on time. The presentation round felt like a mini-interview, and after that, Rishi was basically half-dead with his fever.

This wasn’t just an event for me—it was a test of my obsession with blockchain. I’m proud I pushed through despite the pain. We didn’t win, which is a bit disappointing, but I’m glad we didn’t give up.
