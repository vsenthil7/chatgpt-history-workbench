# 🎯 Purpose

With this layer:

---

# 🏗️ Core Components

## 1) 📌 Obligation Engine (OE)
Turns signals into **Action Units (AU)**

**Action Unit =**
- `action_id`
- `trigger_signal_id`
- `responsible_actor_id` (single owner, no ambiguity)
- `action_type` (audit / reimburse / correct / disclose)
- `deadline`
- `severity` (S1–S4)
- `status` (open / in-progress / resolved / overdue)
- `evidence_required` (list)

> Rule: **Exactly one accountable owner per AU**

---

## 2) 🧾 Accountability Ledger (AL)
Append-only log of:

- Who was assigned  
- What they did (or didn’t)  
- When they acted  
- Evidence submitted  

> ❗ No delete. Only “superseded”.

---

## 3) ⏱️ Escalation Engine (EE)
Time + severity driven escalation.

**Example:**
- S3 (medium): T+48h → supervisor  
- S2 (high): T+24h → director  
- S1 (critical): T+6h → board / public flag  

> If no action → **automatic escalation**, not manual.

---

## 4) 🏅 Incentive Engine (IE)
Applies **rewards + penalties** tied to AU outcomes.

### Rewards (for timely, verified action)
- Reputation score ↑  
- Public “resolved-by” credit  
- Priority access to future funds / roles  

### Penalties (for failure or delay)
- Reputation score ↓  
- Visible overdue flags  
- Funding/role restrictions  
- Mandatory audit assignment  

> ❗ Incentives must be **visible and cumulative**

---

## 5) 🧭 Role Registry (RR)
Defines:

- Actor identities  
- Authority scope  
- Escalation chain  

> Prevents “no one is responsible” failure.
