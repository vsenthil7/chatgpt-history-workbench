- **Activity 1 - Icebreaker - 3 Stars and 1 Wish (5 mins)**
    
    *This activity makes you and the student comfortable with each other.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. Share the given questions with the student.
    2. Ask the student to share their answer, followed by your answer.
    3. Help the student wherever required.
    
    <aside>
    😱 **Questions:** Share 3 stars and 1 wish.
    
    1. **Star:** What are you good at?
        
        **Example:** 
        
        🌟 I am good at drawing.
        
        🌟 I am a great painter.
        
        🌟 I am good at communicating.
        
    2. **Wish:** What do you wish to do/learn?
        
        **Example:**
        
        💭 I wish to learn the French language.
        
    </aside>
    
- **Activity 2 - Be a good listener and speaker (20 mins)**
    
    *This activity is to teach how to be a good listener and speaker.*
    
    **Activity Name: Grocery Shop Adventure**
    
    <aside>
    ♻️ **During the activity:**
    
    1. Explain the given scenario to the student.
    2. Ask the given questions to the student.
        1. Connect the questions with the concept.
    3. Feel free to add your questions to make the activity interesting.
    4. Help the student wherever required.
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    🚧 **Scenario:** **Imagine you are a 7-year-old boy, John, and your mom gives you some money to buy groceries for the week. You are all set to go to the grocery store. But when you get there, you suddenly forget what your mom wanted you to buy.**
    
    **Questions:**
    
    1. Why do you think you have forgotten the things to buy?
        
        **Possible answer:** I might have been excited about going to the store and forgot because I did not pay full attention when my mom told me the list.
        
    2. What would have made you remember the things?
        
        **Possible answer:** I could have listened carefully when my mom told me the list or I could have written it down. Also, if I repeated the list to myself, I might have remembered better.
        
    3. Do you think things would have changed if you have listened carefully?
        
        **Possible answer:**
        
        - Yes, if I listened carefully and paid full attention when my mom told me the list, I would likely remember what to buy.
        - If I listened, I could have communicated well with the shopkeeper and brought the things.
    4. What are all the questions you would ask a shopkeeper to buy the things your mom wanted to buy?
        
        **Possible answer:** 
        
        - I need to get some items from the list. Can you please help me find them?
        - I need to get apples, whole wheat bread, eggs, and some cheese for sandwiches. Where can I find each of them?
        - Do you have any information on any ongoing promotions or discounts on cereals?
        - Are there different varieties of tomatoes available?
        - Do you have an online payment facility?
    5. What are all the questions you would ask a customer if you are a shopkeeper?
        
        **Possible Answer:** 
        
        - Hello! How may I help you today?
        - Is there anything specific you are looking for today?
        - Are you looking for any particular brand or type of product?
        - How much of [product] are you looking to purchase?
        - Are you aware of any ongoing promotions or discounts that might interest you?
        - Is there anything you would like a recommendation for? We have some new arrivals.
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    ♻️  **Summary of  the activity:**

    5. **What did you learn from the activity?**
        
        **Possible Answer:** I learned that it's important to ask questions, listen carefully, suggest solutions, and cooperate with others to solve problems. Good communication skills make a big difference in understanding and being understood.

    <aside>
    👮🏻 **Share the given points with the student:**
    
    1. Give full attention to the speaker.
    2. Maintain eye contact with the speaker.
    3. Use words like “I see," "Go on," etc.
    4. Use open-ended questions often.
        
        **Example:** when, why, where, or how type of questions.
        
    5. Use body language, such as nodding, smiling which conveys your understanding and empathy.
    6. Avoid interruptions.
    7. Don't give decisions rather help them in making decisions.

# Session 5 – Mentor Execution Plan (Clean, Practical Flow)

This is your **actual call-ready flow** (not just curriculum).  
Designed for **real student interaction + time control + engagement**.

---

# ⏱️ Total Session Flow (Approx: 45–50 mins)

| Section | Activity | Time |
|--------|--------|------|
| 1 | Icebreaker | 5 mins |
| 2 | Core Activity (Grocery) | 20 mins |
| 3 | Story Activity | 15 mins |
| 4 | Homework + Wrap | 5–10 mins |

---

# 1. Icebreaker – *3 Stars & 1 Wish* (5 mins)

### 🎯 Objective
- Make student comfortable  
- Shift them into **speaking mode**

### 🧠 How to run (Mentor Script Style)

Start simple:

> “Today we’ll start with something fun — 3 stars and 1 wish 😊”

Then guide:

- “Tell me 3 things you are good at”
- “Now tell me 1 thing you want to learn”

### 💡 If student struggles:
Prompt gently:
- “Are you good at drawing? speaking? helping others?”
- “Do you want to learn English? skills? anything?”

### 🔍 What to observe (Important for you)
- Confidence level  
- Sentence formation  
- Hesitation vs flow  

---

# 2. Core Activity – Grocery Shop Adventure (20 mins)

### 🎯 Core Concept
> **Listening → Understanding → Speaking → Problem solving**

---

## Step 1 – Story Setup (2 mins)

Tell slowly:

> “Imagine you are a small boy… you go to shop… but you forgot the list…”

Pause. Let it sink.

---

## Step 2 – Guided Thinking (10–12 mins)

Ask **one question at a time** (IMPORTANT — don’t rush)

### Q1: Why forgot?
👉 Push toward:
- Not listening
- Distraction
- Excitement

---

### Q2: How to remember?
👉 Guide:
- Listen carefully  
- Write it down  
- Repeat in mind  

---

### Q3: If listened properly?
👉 Key learning:
- Listening = clarity  
- Clarity = confidence  

---

## Step 3 – Speaking Practice (5–6 mins)

Now shift to **real communication**

### Roleplay 1: Student = Customer

Ask:
> “What will you ask shopkeeper?”

If stuck, scaffold:
- “Where can I find…?”
- “Do you have…?”
- “Can you help me?”

---

### Roleplay 2: Student = Shopkeeper

Ask:
> “Now you are shopkeeper — what will you ask?”

Push:
- “How can I help you?”
- “What do you need?”

---

## 🧠 Mentor Insight (Important)

This activity is NOT about answers.

It is about:
- Listening awareness  
- Asking questions  
- Speaking confidence  

---

# 3. Reflection Questions (5 mins)

Ask calmly:

- Did you like the activity?
- What skills did you use?
- What was the problem?
- What did you learn?

### 🎯 Expected shift
Student should say:
> “Listening is important”

If not → guide them there.

---

# 4. Key Teaching Points (2–3 mins)

Deliver clearly (slow + emphasis):

> “To be a good listener and speaker…”

### 📌 Say these as simple rules:

- Listen fully  
- Don’t interrupt  
- Ask questions  
- Use small words (“okay”, “I see”)  
- Use body language  

💡 Keep it **human, not lecture**

---

# 5. Story Activity – *Little Robot’s Big Dream* (15 mins)

### 🎯 Objective
- Test listening  
- Trigger imagination  
- Encourage speaking  

---

## Step 1 – Narrate (5 mins)

Tell story with **emotion + pauses**

Stop at:
> “They found a special power source…”

---

## Step 2 – Student continues (10 mins)

Ask:

> “What happens next?”

---

### 💡 If student is stuck:
Use prompts:
- “Is it dangerous?”
- “Do they save someone?”
- “Do they become heroes?”

---

### 🔍 Observe
- Creativity  
- Sentence structure  
- Confidence  

---

# 6. Homework (5 mins)

### 🎯 Objective
Move learning → real life

---

## 📝 Explain clearly:

> “Talk to one family member and ask questions about their life”

---

### Give 2–3 simple starter questions:

- “What was your childhood like?”
- “What is one lesson you learned?”
- “What problems did you face?”

---

### 💡 Important Instruction:
> “Next session, you will tell me what you learned”

---

# 7. Closing (2 mins)

Say:

- “Good job today 👍”
- Highlight ONE strength:
  - “You asked good questions”
  - OR “You spoke confidently”
