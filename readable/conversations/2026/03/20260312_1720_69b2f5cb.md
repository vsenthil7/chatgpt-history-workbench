# Activity 1 – Icebreaker: I Spy

### Objective
To make the student comfortable and engaged at the start of the session.

### How the Activity Works

1. The mentor asks the student one question at a time.
2. Both mentor and student try to guess the answer.
3. After the guess, the correct answer is revealed.
4. Whoever answers correctly first gets one point.
5. The mentor keeps the activity interactive and encouraging.

### Questions and Answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I spy something soft, round, and can be thrown. | Ball |
| I spy something that grows, is smooth, and is found on plants. | Flowers |
| I spy something round and found it in the face. | Eyeballs |
| I spy something small and fluffy that sounds like meows. | Kitten |
| I spy something round, juicy and tangy that you must peel before eating it. | Orange |

# Activity 2 – You Said, I Heard

### Objective
Help the student understand how misunderstandings happen and how empathy helps prevent conflict.

### Core Concept

When someone says something, there are two ways it may be interpreted:

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Real Takeaway** | The actual meaning of what the person intended to say |
| **Reel Takeaway** | The misunderstood interpretation that may lead to conflict |

---

## Example Explanation

**Boss:** “I love to hear your voice during team meetings.”

| Interpretation Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Real Takeaway | I should participate more in meetings. |
| Reel Takeaway | The boss thinks I talk too much and it’s becoming a problem. |

---

## Statements for Discussion

### 1. Boss Statement

**Boss:** Your colleagues have good things to say about you.

| Interpretation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Real Takeaway | My colleagues appreciate my work. |
| Reel Takeaway | Maybe my colleagues are talking negatively about me. |

---

### 2. Teacher Statement

**Teacher:** Your class participation has been outstanding recently.

| Interpretation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Real Takeaway | The teacher appreciates my active participation. |
| Reel Takeaway | The teacher might be sarcastic and classmates may not like it. |

---

### 3. Parent Statement

**Parent:** Your room needs to be more organised.

| Interpretation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Real Takeaway | My parent wants me to keep my room neat. |
| Reel Takeaway | My parent is nagging again about my room. |

---

### 4. Friend Statement

**Friend:** I like how you always listen when I need to talk.

| Interpretation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Real Takeaway | My friend values my listening and support. |
| Reel Takeaway | My friend thinks I never respond or give opinions. |

---

### 5. Sibling Statement

**Sibling:** You never help with chores in our house.

| Interpretation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Real Takeaway | My siblings feel I do not contribute enough. |
| Reel Takeaway | They are exaggerating because I sometimes help. |

# Activity 3 – Conflict Resolution Practice

### Objective
Allow the student to practice resolving conflicts using fairness and empathy.

### Instructions

1. Mentor explains the scenario.
2. Student gets **3 minutes to think**.
3. Student writes what **both sides are thinking**.
4. Student shares their solution.
5. Mentor discusses and guides the student.

---

## Scenario 1 – Football Conflict

Two groups want to play football at lunch but only one ball is available.

**Conflict Points**

| Group A | Group B |
|---|---|
| They are older and get fewer chances to play. | They brought the ball first. |

**Sample Resolution**

Divide lunchtime into two halves.

| Time | Who Plays |
|---|---|
| First Half | Group B (they brought the ball) |
| Second Half | Group A |

This solution ensures fairness.

---

## Scenario 2 – Parking Dispute

Two neighbours want the last guest parking space.

| Person | Argument |
|---|---|
| Priya | She has lived there longer |
| Gowri | First come, first served |

**Sample Resolution**

- Gowri gets the parking today because she came first.
- Next time, Priya receives priority.

This balances fairness and respect.

2. **Why did it happen?**  
3. **What could change to avoid the disagreement?**

This homework helps the student reflect on real-life conflict situations.
