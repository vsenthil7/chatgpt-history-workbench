# 🧾 1. Can UK client pay via BACS?
### ❌ Not directly useful for India

- **BACS** = UK domestic bank transfer only  
- Works **only within UK bank accounts**
- So unless:
  - You have a **UK bank account**, or  
  - A **UK receiving account (via Wise/Revolut)**  

👉 Otherwise → **BACS alone won’t reach India**
