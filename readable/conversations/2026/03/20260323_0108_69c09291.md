# 🧾 2. Is every charity eligible to issue 80G certificate?

👉 **NO. Not every charity can issue 80G.**

## ❌ A charity **CANNOT** issue 80G if:
- Not registered under Income Tax Act
- 80G approval expired
- Registration cancelled/suspended
- Trust is informal / unregistered

---

## ✅ A charity **CAN issue 80G ONLY IF:**

1. Registered as:
   - Trust / Society / Section 8 Company

2. Has valid:
   - **12AB Registration** (mandatory)
   - **80G Registration (Form 10AC / 10AD approval)**

3. Has:
   - **Unique Registration Number (URN)**
   - Validity period (check expiry!)

---

- Mode of payment
- Donor Name + PAN
- Date of donation
- Receipt number
- Signature / seal
