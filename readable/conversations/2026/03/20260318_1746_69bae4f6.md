- **Activity 1 - Icebreaker - Rose, Thorn, and Bud** **(5 mins)**
    
    *This activity makes you and the student comfortable with each other.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. You and the student will share your Rose, Thorn, and Bud with each other.
    2. Share the given explanation with the student.
        1. **Rose**: Happiness that happened recently.
        2. **Thorn**: A challenge that I recently faced.
        3. **Bud**: An idea or dream for the future.
    3. Ask the student to share their answer, followed by your answer.
    4. Help the student wherever required.
    
- **Activity 2 - Self-motivation (20 mins)**
    
    *This activity is to teach the student about strategies to stay motivated during difficult times.*
    
    <aside>
    📋 **During the activity:**
    
    1. Explain the given scenario to the student.
    2. Encourage the student to share their thoughts and ideas for each question.
    3. Provide suggestions and additional questions if needed.
    4. Emphasise that there are no right or wrong answers, and their ideas are important.
    </aside>
    
    [](data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIAAAP///wAAACH5BAEAAAAALAAAAAABAAEAAAICRAEAOw==)
    
    **Activity Name: Motivation Quest**
    
    <aside>
    😎 **Scenario:** Imagine a student, Seema, who is preparing for a crucial exam. However, she faces challenges such as feeling overwhelmed, self-doubt, and external distractions.
    
    </aside>
    
    **Questions:**
    
    <aside>
    1️⃣ When you are feeling overwhelmed, what is the strategy you could use to break down your study goals into more manageable tasks?
    
    **Possible Answer:** One strategy is to create a study plan where I break down my larger study goals into smaller, achievable tasks. This way, I can focus on one step at a time, making it less overwhelming.
    
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    2️⃣ How can positive self-talk help you fight self-doubt and boost your motivation?
    
    **Possible Answer:** Positive self-talk can be a powerful tool to change negative thoughts into positive ones. When I doubt my abilities, telling myself that "I am capable" or "I can learn and succeed" can help shift my mindset.
    
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    3️⃣ How might visualizing success in the exam positively impact your motivation and confidence?
    
    **Possible Answer:** Visualizing success involves creating a mental image of myself successfully completing the exam. This positive imagery can boost my confidence, reduce anxiety, and motivate me to work towards that success.
    
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    4️⃣ When you are facing a challenging task, how can breaking it into smaller steps help you overcome delaying it?
    
    **Possible Answer:** Breaking a task into smaller steps makes it less overwhelming. It becomes easier to start, and as I complete each step, I gain a sense of accomplishment, motivating me to continue.
    
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    5️⃣ What is a specific, achievable goal you can set for your study session to avoid delay?
    
    **Possible Answer:** Instead of a vague goal like "study for the test," set a specific goal like "complete two pages of notes" or "review one chapter." Clear goals make it easier to start.
    
    </aside>
    
    <aside>
    🧙 **Summary of the Activity:**
    
    > **Note:**
    > 
    > 1. Ask the given questions one at a time.
    > 2. Help the student wherever required.
    
    **Questions:**
    
    1. **Did you like the activity?**
        
        **Possible Answer:** Yes, I enjoyed the activity. It was engaging and provided a creative way to explore the concept.
        
    2. **From the activity, what problem did you identify?**
        
        **Possible Answer:** The activity highlighted the challenge of managing time effectively and staying motivated during difficult times.
        
    3. **What did you understand from the above activity?**
        
        **Possible Answer:** From the activity, I understood the importance of staying motivated during difficult times and how to avoid delaying a work.
        
    4. **What did you learn from the activity?**
        
        **Possible Answer:** I learned that positive self-talk and visualization techniques can significantly impact one's mindset and motivation, especially during challenging tasks like exam preparation.
        
    </aside>
    
- **Activity 3 - Motivation Brainstorm (15 mins)**
    
    *In this activity, the student will practice the self-motivation concept.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. Tell the given scenario to the student.
    2. Ask the student to share 5 ways to stay motivated along with the explanation.
    3. Ask the student to share their answer, followed by yours.
    4. Guide and help the student wherever required.
    
    <aside>
    🏋🏽 **Scenario:** Gokul used to enjoy working out, but recently, he lost motivation to exercise. He finds it challenging to stick to a fitness routine.
    
    **Sample Answer:**
    
    1. **Reward Yourself:** Gokul can reward him for achieving fitness milestones. It creates positive reinforcement and encourages continued effort.
    2. **Join a Fitness Community:** Being part of a fitness community provides a support system. Motivates through sharing goals and experiences.
    3. **Set Specific Goals:** Having clear fitness goals gives Gokul a target that can be completed. It increases his motivation as he sees his progress.
    4. **Find an Accountability Partner:** Partnering with someone provides accountability, making exercise more enjoyable and ensuring consistency.
    5. **Switch up the Routine:** Trying new activities or classes adds variety, preventing boredom, and renewing motivation to stay active.
    </aside>
    
- **Activity 4 - Homework of the day (5 mins)**
    
    *This activity encourages the student to learn and practice.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. Discuss the previous session's homework.
    2. Then, explain the given homework to the student.
    3. Encourage the student to complete homework.
    4. Give some examples or tips to the student.
    5. Inform the student that homework will be discussed in the next session.
        
        <aside>
        📝 **Homework:**
        
        1. Think of a task that you have been postponing due to low motivation. It could be related to homework, chores, or any personal project.
        2. List down the ways you will motivate you to complete the task.
        </aside>
