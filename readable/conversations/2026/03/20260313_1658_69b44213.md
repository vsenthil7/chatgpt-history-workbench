    *This activity helps the student to understand self-awareness.*
    
    **Activity Name: What do you think?**
    
    <aside>
    📖 **During the activity:**
    
    1. Encourage the student to share their thoughts and ideas for each question.
    2. Provide suggestions and encourage them with additional questions if needed.
    3. Emphasise that there are no right or wrong answers, and their ideas are important.

    **Instructions:**

    2. Ask the student to share their answer, followed by your answer.
    3. Make it fun and engaging.
    4. Help the student wherever required.
    
    **Questions:**
    
    1. Describe yourself in three words.
        
        **Possible Answer:** Creative, kind, determined.
        
    2. Has your personality changed since childhood?
        
        **Possible Answer:** Yes, I have become more confident and outgoing since I was a kid.
        
    3. Is your personality like either of your parents?
        
        **Possible Answer:** I am curious like my mom and enjoy making people laugh like my dad.
        
    4. What qualities do you admire most in yourself?
        
        **Possible Answer:** I like that I am strong when facing issues and that I stay positive.
        
    5. What are the 5 things you are good at?
        
        **Possible Answer:** I am good at solving problems, playing the guitar, speaking in front of people, making cool pictures, and cooking yummy food.
        
    6. What things scare you?
        
        **Possible Answer:** I am scared of spiders and tall places.
        
    7. If you ate a monkey’s banana, what would be the emotion of the monkey?
        1. Angry
        2. Sad
        3. Happy
        
        **Possible Answer:** The monkey would probably be angry or sad.

        **Possible Answer:** I would feel upset and talk to my friend about it to find a fair solution.

        1. I made a mistake, and I can learn from this.
        2. I was correct, and I will do the same.
        
        **Possible Answer:** I made a mistake and I can learn from it. I should have found a better solution for the puppy's safety.
        
    10. What are your goals?
        1. What do you want to learn?
        2. What do you want to be better at?
        3. What do you want to achieve?
        
        **Possible Answer:**
        
        1. I want to learn a new language.
        2. I want to improve how I manage my time.

# Session 2 – Introduction to Self-Awareness  
*(Mentor Practical Flow Guide – so you can run the call smoothly)*

Estimated total session time: **45 minutes**

---

# 1. Session Start (2–3 min)

**Mentor greeting**

Example:

> Hi Durga, how are you today?  
> Did you have a good day?

Small talk for comfort.

Then say:

> Today we are going to talk about **Self-Awareness** — understanding ourselves, our feelings, strengths, and goals.

---

# 2. Activity 1 – Icebreaker  
**Listen, Remember, Speak (5 min)**

Purpose: Improve **attention and memory** and make the student comfortable.

### Step 1 — Choose a theme

Example theme: **Kitchen**

### Step 2 — Say 10 words slowly (once)

Example:

- Kettle  
- Cooker  
- Refrigerator  
- Spoon  
- Knife  
- Plate  
- Microwave  
- Mixer  
- Cup  
- Toaster  

### Step 3 — Give 30 seconds

Tell the student:

> Take 30 seconds and try to remember the words.

### Step 4 — Ask them to recall

> Can you tell me the words you remember?

Encourage even if they remember only 3–5.

### Step 5 — Reverse round

Now ask the student to give **10 words in a theme**.

Example themes they may choose:

- School
- Classroom
- Office
- Animals

---

# 3. Activity 2 – Self Awareness Discussion  
**“What do you think?” (20 min)**

Purpose: Help the student **reflect about themselves**.

Important mentor rule:

- No right or wrong answers
- Encourage thinking
- Ask follow-up questions

---

## Question Flow

Ask one question at a time.

---

### Q1 — Describe yourself in three words

Example:

> How would you describe yourself in three words?

Example answer:

- Kind  
- Hard-working  
- Friendly

---

### Q2 — Has your personality changed since childhood?

Example:

> Do you think you have changed from when you were a child?

Example answer:

> Yes, I became more confident.

---

### Q3 — Are you like your parents?

> Do you have any qualities similar to your parents?

Example:

> I am patient like my mother.

---

### Q4 — What qualities do you admire in yourself?

Example:

- Helpful
- Honest
- Hardworking

Encourage self-confidence.

---

### Q5 — Five things you are good at

Examples:

- Drawing
- Speaking
- Helping people
- Studying
- Sports

---

### Q6 — What things scare you?

Examples:

- Darkness
- Insects
- Public speaking

Explain:

> Everyone has fears. Understanding them helps us improve.

---

### Q7 — Monkey banana question

Scenario:

> If you eat a monkey’s banana, what will the monkey feel?

Choices:

- Angry  
- Sad  
- Happy  

Discussion: **understanding others’ emotions**

---

### Q8 — Friend takes credit for your work

Scenario discussion.

Ask:

> How would you feel?

Encourage answers like:

- Upset
- Angry
- Disappointed

Then ask:

> What would you do?

Goal: **conflict handling and emotional awareness**

---

### Q9 — Puppy in the rain

Scenario to teach **learning from mistakes**.

Expected reflection:

> I should find a better solution.

---

### Q10 — Goals

Ask three sub-questions:

1. What do you want to **learn**?
2. What do you want to **improve**?
3. What do you want to **achieve in life**?

Examples:

- Learn a new skill
- Improve communication
- Get a good career

---

# 4. Activity Summary (3–4 min)

Ask these three questions.

### Question 1

> What was this activity about?

Expected answer:

> Thinking about ourselves.

---

### Question 2

> What did we do in the activity?

Expected answer:

> We talked about our personality, feelings, strengths, and fears.

---

### Question 3

> What did you learn?

Expected answer:

- Understanding ourselves
- Recognising strengths and weaknesses
- Managing emotions

---

# 5. Explain Self-Awareness in Real Life (2 min)

Explain simply:

Self-awareness means:

1. Understanding your **feelings**
2. Knowing your **strengths**
3. Improving your **weaknesses**
4. Understanding **how you react in situations**

Example:

> If we know we get angry quickly, we can learn to control it.

---

# 6. Activity 3 – Self Awareness Journal  
**(15 min)**

Ask the student to take **pen and notebook**.

Both mentor and student can write answers.

---

### Journal prompts

Write answers for:

1️⃣ Two biggest **strengths**

2️⃣ One **weakness**

3️⃣ Top **three positive qualities**

4️⃣ Activities that make you **happy**

5️⃣ **Dreams for the future**

6️⃣ A time you **succeeded**

7️⃣ **Positive thoughts** that motivate you

8️⃣ **Self compliments**

Example:

> I am proud that I work hard.

---

Discuss answers briefly.

---

# 7. Homework (5 min)

Explain clearly.

Homework:

> Create a **Self-Awareness Journal Table**

Daily write:

- Experience
- Emotion
- Thought

Example table:

| Day | Experience | Emotion | Thought |
|----|----|----|----|
| Monday | Helped friend | Happy | Helping others feels good |

Explain:

> We will discuss this in the next session.

---

# 8. Session Closing (1–2 min)

Tell the student:

> Please mark your **attendance for today**.

Then say:

> Thank you for the session today. See you in the next session.
