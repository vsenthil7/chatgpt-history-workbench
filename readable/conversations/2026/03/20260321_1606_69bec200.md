- **Activity 1 - Icebreaker - Bring me something (5 mins)**
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. **Ask the student:** Prompt -"Bring me something…”
        1. **Example:** Bring me something to eat.
    2. The student should tell five things related to the prompt.
        1. **Examples:** Biscuits, bread, cookies, Idlis, and Vada.
    3. Now, ask the student to tell a prompt and you tell five things.
    4. Take turns to continue the game within the time limit.
    5. Help the student wherever required.
    
- **Activity 2 - Practicing Gratitude (20 mins)**
    
    *This activity teaches the student about practicing gratitude.*
    
    <aside>
    💁‍♀️ **Activity Name: Expressing Gratitude**
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. Ask the given questions one at a time to the student.
    2. Give 1 minute time to think.
    3. Ask the student to give possible answers.
    4. Ask the student to say thank you at the end of the discussion.
    5. Help the student wherever required.
        
        **Questions:**
        
        1. How will you thank your friends for being there when you need them?
            
            **Possible answer:** I will thank my friends for always being there, and making my days brighter. Grateful for their friendship!
            
        2. In what ways can you express gratitude to your siblings for their support?
            
            **Possible answer:** I might say thanks to my siblings and do something nice, like helping or spending time together.
            
        3. What are some creative ways to express thanks?
            
            **Possible answer:** Handwritten Notes, Personalized Gifts, Acts of Kindness, Quality Time, Creative Art.
            
        4. How will you express appreciation to yourselves for your achievements and efforts?
            
            **Possible answer:** I will treat myself to something special, and say, 'Great job, me!
            
        5. How will you say thank you to your mother for contributing a happy home environment? 
            
            **Possible answer:** "I will give my mother a big hug and say, 'Thank you for making our home so happy. I love you!'”
            
        6. How will you say thank you to the stranger for helping you to cross the road?
            
            **Possible answer:** I will smile and say, 'Thank you so much for helping me to cross the road. I really appreciate your kindness.
            
    </aside>
