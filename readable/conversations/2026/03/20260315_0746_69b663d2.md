## SWOT Analysis → Situation Analysis

**S – Strengths** → What is good about the situation?  
**W – Weaknesses** → What is bad about the situation?  
**O – Opportunities** → What can you gain from the situation?  
**T – Threats** → What can you lose from the situation?
