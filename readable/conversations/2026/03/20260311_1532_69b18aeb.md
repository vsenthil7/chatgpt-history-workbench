- **Activity 1 - Icebreaker - Two Truths and a Lie (5 mins)**
    
    *This activity makes you and the student comfortable with each other.*
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. You can start by saying 3 things about yourself.
        1. 2 things need to be truth and 1 thing needs to be lie.
        2. **Example:**
            1. I am left-handed. (Truth)
            2. I eat 3 times a day. (Truth)
            3. I saw a dinosaur near my home. (Lie)
    2. Ask the student to identify the lie.
    3. Tell the student if the answer is correct or not.
    4. Now, the student should tell 2 truths and 1 lie.
    5. You should try to guess the lie.
    6. Play as many rounds as possible.
    7. Help the student wherever required.
    
- **Activity 2 - 20 Questions (20 mins)**
    
    **Instructions:**
    
    1. You think of a famous person.
    2. Tell the student to ask “yes” or “no” questions to find the mystery person.
        1. Only 20 questions are allowed to be asked.
        2. The answer can be guessed at any question number.
    3. You should only respond with “yes” or “no” answers.
    4. Any type of question counts.
    5. If the student guesses the answer:
        1. Correct - Appreciate their effort.
        2. Wrong - Reveal the answer.
    6. Now, ask the student to think of a person.
    7. Then, you can ask questions to identify the person.
    8. Play as many rounds as possible within the given time.
    9. Help the student wherever required.
    
    <aside>
    ❓ **Example:** MS Dhoni
    
    1. Are they a man? Yes.
    2. Is he famous? Yes.
    3. Is he an actor? No.
    4. Is he a singer? No.
    5. Is he a sportsperson? Yes.
    6. Is he a football player? No.
    7. Is he a cricketer? Yes.
    8. Has he played for India? Yes.
    9. Has he played for Chennai? Yes.
    10. Has he been the Chennai team’s captain? Yes.
    11. Is he a wicketkeeper? Yes.
    12. Guess: MS Dhoni? Correct (12/20 questions).
    </aside>
    
- **Activity 3 - Who am I? (15 mins)**
    1. Tell the student 3 facts about a person, place, animal, or thing.
    2. Ask the student to guess what it is.
    3. Now the student tells 3 facts.
    4. You guess and answer.
    5. Examples are given for your reference.
    6. Play as many rounds as possible in the given time.
    7. Help the student wherever required.
    
    <aside>
    💡 **Examples:**
    
    1. I am in South India; I am a capital; Marina Beach is here.
        
        **Answer:** Chennai
        
    2. I am round; I bounce a lot; I am thrown in a hoop.

        **Answer:** Moon
        
    </aside>
    
- **Activity 4 - Homework of the Day (5 mins)**

    2. Then, explain the given homework to the student.
    3. Encourage the student to complete homework.
    4. Give some examples or tips to the student.
    5. Inform the student that homework will be discussed in the next session.
