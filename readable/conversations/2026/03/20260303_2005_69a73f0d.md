## 🥣 South Indian Sambar (Balanced Home Version)

## 🧂 Ingredients (Serves 3–4)

### Dal Base
- Toor dal – ½ cup (100 g)
- Turmeric – ¼ tsp
- Water – 1½ cups (for cooking)

### Vegetables (choose 2–3)
- Drumstick – 1
- Carrot – 1 small
- Small onion / shallots – 6–8 (optional)
- Tomato – 1 medium
- Brinjal or pumpkin – optional

### Tamarind
- Small lemon-sized tamarind  
  (Soak in ½ cup warm water, extract juice)

### Sambar Powder
- Sambar powder – 1½ tbsp  
- Red chilli powder – optional ¼ tsp (if needed heat)
- Salt – as required

### Tempering
- Ghee or oil – 2 tsp  
- Mustard – ½ tsp  
- Jeera – ½ tsp  
- Curry leaves – few  
- Hing – pinch  
- Dry red chilli – 1

---

## 🔥 Step-by-Step Method

### 1️⃣ Cook Dal
Pressure cook toor dal + turmeric + water  
3–4 whistles until soft.  
Mash smoothly. (No grainy texture.)

---

### 2️⃣ Cook Vegetables
In a kadai:
- Add vegetables
- Add tamarind extract
- Add salt
- Add 1 cup water  

Boil 8–10 mins until vegetables soften and raw tamarind smell disappears.

---

### 3️⃣ Add Spice Layer
Add:
- Sambar powder  
Simmer 3–4 minutes.  
(Important: Don’t add sambar powder too early — it turns bitter.)

---

### 4️⃣ Combine Dal
Add mashed dal + extra water if needed.  
Consistency: Slightly flowing, not thick like chutney.  

Simmer 5 minutes only.  
Switch off.

---

### 5️⃣ Tempering (Flavor Lock)
Heat ghee:
Add mustard → crackle  
Add jeera  
Add red chilli  
Add curry leaves + hing  

Pour over sambar.  
Cover immediately for 2 minutes (aroma trapping technique).

---

## 🎯 Texture Rules (Very Important)

- Too thick → Add hot water, not cold.
- Too sour → Add pinch jaggery.
- Too flat → Add ½ tsp ghee at end.
- Too spicy → Add little mashed dal.

---

# 🔄 Variations Based on Eating

### 🍚 For Rice (Lunch Sambar)
- Slightly thicker
- More tamarind
- More vegetables

### 🥞 For Idli/Dosa (Tiffin Sambar)
- Slightly watery
- Add small onions
- Add pinch jaggery
- Add 1 tbsp cooked moong dal extra for silkiness

### 🌿 Temple Style (No onion, mild)
- More turmeric
- No chilli powder
- Add small jaggery
- More ghee tempering

# 🏨 1️⃣ Hotel-Style Thick Sambar (No Toor Dal)

This mimics the thickness restaurants get.

## Step A – Build a Dal Paste (Thickness Engine)

Heat 1 tsp coconut oil.

Add:
- 1½ tsp channa dal  
- 1 tsp urad dal  

Roast on medium until golden (not dark).

Cool slightly.

Add 3–4 tbsp hot water and grind (or crush very finely with spoon).

👉 This is your “mock toor dal paste”.

---

## Step B – Cook Base

In kadai:

1. 2 tsp coconut oil  
2. ½ tsp mustard  
3. ½ tsp jeera  

Add frozen veg (2 cups).  
Add ¼ tsp turmeric + salt.

Add:
- 1 tsp tamarind paste  
- 2 cups hot water  

Boil 8–10 minutes.

---

## Step C – Add Spice + Thickness

Add:
- 1½ tbsp MTR sambar powder  
Simmer 3 minutes.

Now add your roasted dal paste.

Simmer 5–7 minutes.

It will thicken gradually and coat the spoon like hotel sambar.

Finish with 1 tsp ghee.

---

# 🥞 2️⃣ Convert to Idli Sambar Style

Idli sambar is:
- Slightly thinner
- Slightly sweeter
- Very smooth

Modify above recipe:

✔ Reduce tamarind to ¾ tsp  
✔ Add ½ tsp extra ghee at end  
✔ Add ½ cup extra hot water  
✔ Simmer gently (don’t reduce too much)

Optional trick:
Mash 2–3 soft vegetables (pumpkin/carrot) into the sambar.
This makes it silky.

Final texture:
Flowing but not watery.

That gives natural cream body.
