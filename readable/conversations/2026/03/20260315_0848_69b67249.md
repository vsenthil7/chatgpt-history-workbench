# Activity 1 – Icebreaker  
## Twisted Tongues (5 mins)

### Objective
To create comfort and engagement between mentor and student.

### Instructions
1. Share the given tongue twisters with the student.
2. Mentor and student take turns.
3. Each person tries to say the chosen tongue twister **continuously for 1 minute**.
4. Whoever says it correctly and continuously gets the point.
5. Keep the activity fun and interesting.
6. Help the student if required.

### Tongue Twisters
1. She sells seashells by the seashore.  
2. I scream you scream, we all scream for ice cream.  
3. Light the night light tonight.  
4. The blue bluebird blinks.

---

# Activity 2 – The Role of Self-Awareness in Personal Development  
## Activity Name: Discover Goals (20 mins)

### Purpose
To help the student understand **strengths, weaknesses, and goals** through everyday objects.

### During the Activity
Mentor should:

- Encourage the student to share thoughts and ideas.
- Ask additional questions if needed.
- Reinforce that **there are no right or wrong answers**.

---

## Main Questions to Ask the Student

### 1️⃣ Strength Representation
Ask the student to:

Find an object in their room that represents **one of their strengths**.

Example  
“Books represent my knowledge and intellectual strength.”

---

### 2️⃣ Weakness Representation
Ask the student to:

Find an object in their room that represents **one of their weaknesses**.

Example  
“Television remote represents my weakness in time management.”

---

### 3️⃣ Explanation
Ask the student to explain:

Why they chose that object.

---

### 4️⃣ Goal Connection
Ask the student to connect the object to **a future goal**.

Example  
“I want to become a teacher.”

---

### 5️⃣ Goal Discussion
Encourage the student to talk about:

- Their goal
- How they plan to achieve it

---
