**HubSpot → QuoteFlow**  
**Xero → FlowCast**

You don't necessarily need **100,000 customers**.

# 💰 Are these "money-run" projects?

**Potentially yes — but I would call them money-run candidates, not money-running businesses yet.**

I would separate the stages:

**Product idea → engineered product → marketplace-ready → installed → activated → retained → paid → profitable.**

Claude + ChatGPT can push extremely hard on the first three.

We cannot manufacture the last four through engineering.

That's why I don't want us doing another **30–40 engineering sprints per product** indefinitely without getting market evidence.

### The next milestone should NOT be "perfect code"

It should be:

> **Can an unknown company install it, obtain value quickly, trust it, and pay without us manually rescuing them?**

That is the real test.

# 🥇 EngageOS WhatsApp

This is the one I would currently push hardest commercially.

The underlying problem is enormous: businesses use WhatsApp for leads, customer support, follow-ups, appointments, sales and service.

But don't make it merely another WhatsApp inbox.

The defensibility needs to become:

**conversation → intent → workflow → SLA → automation → human handoff → outcome → analytics**

If EngageOS can demonstrate measurable outcomes such as:

> 437 conversations  
> 82 leads identified  
> 39 follow-ups automatically created  
> 17 recovered opportunities  
> £X/₹X attributable revenue

then it becomes much easier to sell.

**My score: 9/10 opportunity.**

# 🥈 FlowCast Xero

This may actually become the **cleanest SaaS business** of the eight.

Why?

Finance pain has direct monetary consequences.

Businesses understand:

**cash flow / overdue invoices / collections / forecasting / payment risk.**

Therefore ROI can be explained without educating the buyer about a completely new category.

Something like:

> "FlowCast identified £38,000 of collections at risk and prioritised the five invoices most likely to affect next month's cash position."

That's commercially powerful.

**My score: 8.5–9/10.**

# OnCall Copilot Slack

Potentially valuable because engineering downtime is extremely expensive.

The interesting product isn't:

> "AI summarises the Slack incident."

It is:

**incident detection → coordination → timeline → ownership → evidence → remediation → postmortem → learning**

That becomes operational infrastructure rather than another AI assistant.

Enterprise engineering teams can justify meaningful pricing if it genuinely reduces MTTR.

**8/10.**

# RetainKit Shopify

Huge demand.

Shopify currently shows **403 email-marketing apps with win-back functionality**, while third-party marketplace analysis suggests win-back demand is heavily concentrated among incumbents such as Klaviyo. citeturn0search2turn0search3

That's substantially more interesting.

**Market: 9/10.

**release-risk intelligence + coverage gaps + flaky-test intelligence + requirement/test traceability + AI-generated evidence.**
