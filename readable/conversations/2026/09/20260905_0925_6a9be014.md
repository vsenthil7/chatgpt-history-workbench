## 05/09/2026 10:25 — Tamil-Style Tomato Rice 🍅🍚

Partner, if you want a **simple Tamil home-style thakkali sadam**, this is a reliable version for **2 people**.

### Ingredients

- Cooked rice — **2 cups** (preferably cooled and separate grains)
- Tomatoes — **3 medium**, finely chopped
- Onion — **1 medium**, sliced
- Green chilli — **1–2**, slit
- Ginger — **1 tsp**, finely chopped/grated
- Curry leaves — **8–10**
- Coriander leaves — **2 tbsp**, chopped
- Turmeric — **¼ tsp**
- Chilli powder — **½ tsp**
- Sambar powder — **½–1 tsp** *(very good in tomato rice)*
- Salt — as required
- Oil — **1½ tbsp**

**Tempering**
- Mustard seeds — **½ tsp**
- Urad dal — **½ tsp** *(optional)*
- Chana dal — **½ tsp** *(optional)*
- Cumin — **½ tsp**
- Cashews or peanuts — optional

### Method

1. Heat oil. Add **mustard**, then cumin, urad/chana dal if using.
2. Add **curry leaves + green chilli**.
3. Add onion and sauté until soft/light golden.
4. Add ginger and fry for about **30 seconds**.
5. Add tomatoes + a little salt. Cook **5–7 minutes**, until the tomatoes become completely soft and the oil starts separating.
6. Add **turmeric + chilli powder + sambar powder**. Fry for another **1–2 minutes** on low heat. The tomato mixture should become a fairly thick masala—not watery.
7. Reduce heat and add **2 cups cooked rice**.
8. Mix gently without breaking the rice. Taste and adjust salt.
9. Switch off and add **fresh coriander leaves**.

### 🌿 Coriander?

**Yes — definitely works here.** Unlike the classic hotel-style tomato chutney where coriander is optional depending on the style, for tomato rice I would add **a small handful at the very end**. It gives the rice freshness without overpowering the tomato.

**Nice combination:** tomato rice + **onion raita** or plain curd + appalam.

## 05/09/2026 10:27 — Tomato Rice Adjustments 🍅🍚

Yes partner — **dried red chilli instead of green chilli works very well**, and **garlic is good in tomato rice too**.

For **2 cups cooked rice**, I'd use:

- 🌶️ **Dried red chilli — 2**, broken
- 🧄 **Garlic — 4–5 cloves**, finely chopped or lightly crushed
- 🧅 Onion — 1
- 🍅 Tomatoes — 3
- 🌿 Curry leaves — 8–10
- 🌿 Coriander — small handful at the end

### When to add

During tempering: **mustard → cumin → dal (if using) → dried red chillies → curry leaves**.

Then add **garlic** and fry for about **30–45 seconds**, just until fragrant. Don't brown it heavily.

Then **onion → tomatoes → turmeric + chilli powder + sambar powder → rice**.

**One adjustment:** because you're adding dried red chillies, start with only **¼ tsp chilli powder**. You can increase later if you want it hotter.

## 05/09/2026 10:29 — Tomato Vermicelli 🍅🌶️

Yes partner. **Roasted vermicelli works perfectly** — essentially a **tomato semiya/upma**. But unlike the tomato-rice version, **don't cook the vermicelli separately and mix unless you want to**; cooking it directly in the tomato masala gives more flavour.

For **1 cup roasted vermicelli**:

- Roasted vermicelli — **1 cup**
- Tomato — **2 medium**, chopped
- Onion — **1 medium**, sliced
- Garlic — **4–5 cloves**, chopped/crushed
- Dried red chilli — **2**, broken
- Curry leaves — 8–10
- Mustard — ½ tsp
- Cumin — ½ tsp
- Turmeric — ¼ tsp
- Sambar powder — **½ tsp**
- Chilli powder — **¼ tsp or skip** initially
- Salt
- Oil — 1–1½ tbsp
- Coriander — small handful
- **Hot water — about 1½–2 cups** *(check the vermicelli packet ratio if available)*

### Cooking order

**Oil → mustard → cumin → red chilli → curry leaves → garlic → onion → tomato.**

Cook the tomatoes until **really soft and pulpy**. Add turmeric, sambar powder and salt.

Then add the **roasted vermicelli** and mix with the tomato masala for about **30 seconds**.

Add **hot water**, mix once, cover and cook on **low heat** until the vermicelli absorbs the water and becomes soft. Don't keep stirring—it can turn mushy.

Switch off, rest covered for **3–5 minutes**, fluff gently, then add **coriander**.

### 🌶️ With your red chilli + garlic combination

I'd actually **skip chilli powder initially**. Two dried red chillies + garlic + sambar powder should give a nice Tamil-style flavour. If you like it properly spicy, add **¼ tsp chilli powder** with the turmeric.

## 05/09/2026 10:31 — Tomato Vermicelli with Ginger 🍅🌶️

Yes partner — **ginger should be there**. I left it out when adapting from the rice version.

For **1 cup roasted vermicelli**, use **ginger 1 tsp finely chopped/grated** along with **4–5 garlic cloves**.

### Step by step

1. Heat **1–1½ tbsp oil** in a pan.

2. Add **½ tsp mustard seeds**. Let them splutter.

3. Add **½ tsp cumin**.

4. Add **2 dried red chillies**, broken into pieces.

5. Add **8–10 curry leaves**.

6. Add **1 tsp ginger + 4–5 chopped/crushed garlic cloves**. Fry for **30–45 seconds** until fragrant. Do not burn the garlic.

7. Add **1 sliced onion**. Sauté until soft and lightly golden.

8. Add **2 chopped tomatoes + a little salt**. Cook until the tomatoes become very soft and pulpy, around **5–7 minutes**.

9. Add:
   - **¼ tsp turmeric**
   - **½ tsp sambar powder**
   - **¼ tsp chilli powder**, optional

   Fry for about **1 minute**.

10. Add **1 cup roasted vermicelli**. Mix it with the tomato masala for **30–60 seconds**.

11. Add about **1½ cups hot water** first. Add a little more only if your vermicelli needs it.

12. Add remaining salt, mix once, cover, and cook on **low heat** until the vermicelli is soft and the water is absorbed.

13. Switch off the heat and leave it covered for **3–5 minutes**.

14. Fluff gently with a fork.

15. Finish with **chopped coriander leaves**.

**Best flavour order:** red chilli + curry leaves → **ginger + garlic** → onion → tomato. Ginger gives freshness and removes the slightly flat taste that tomato vermicelli can otherwise have.
