### The bigger issue I found: the market moved

The strongest new concern is **Zoom itself**.

Zoom Revenue Accelerator already provides AI conversation intelligence, multilingual capabilities and CRM integration. citeturn1search0 Zoom also supports Salesforce/HubSpot CRM synchronization and CRM workflows. citeturn1search2

More importantly, on **15 September 2026 — only five days ago — Zoom announced its broader AI-powered Revenue OS**, adding Engage and Forecast around Revenue Accelerator. citeturn1search3

So I do **not** recommend Claude simply building more and more generic meeting-intelligence functionality. That risks chasing Zoom feature-for-feature.

The better potential wedge I put into the workbook is:

> **trusted, evidence-grounded, governed CRM writeback from meetings** — field-level policies, source evidence, confidence/abstention, human approval where required, immutable audit, idempotency and CRM portability.

That builds directly on one of MeetSync's strongest existing areas: its review + mapping + durable queue + audit writeback architecture. The uploaded status document itself calls the CRM writeback engine the **“defensible part of the product.”** fileciteturn0file2L216-L227

### I added S21–S35 for Claude

The workbook contains **15 further sprints**, rather than simply continuing S15–S20 mechanically.

The first four are the most important:

**S21 — Reality Gate: Zoom live beta**  
Real Zoom installation, real identity, real webview rendering, real webhook/transcript flow.

**S22 — HubSpot production connector**  
Real OAuth, token lifecycle, field metadata, matching, idempotency, reconciliation and real controlled writes.

**S23 — Evidence-grounded extraction**  
Schema-constrained intelligence with source evidence, confidence, abstention, golden-set evaluation and measurable precision/recall — not another unmeasured LLM demo.

**S24 — Writeback policy & trust wedge**
