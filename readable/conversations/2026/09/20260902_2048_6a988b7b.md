## 🍅🌶️ Tomato Chilli Chutney

A simple **spicy South-Indian tomato chutney**—good with **idli, dosa, chapati, curd rice**, or even plain rice.

### Ingredients
- Tomato — **3 medium**, chopped
- Dry red chilli — **4–6** depending on spice
- Onion — **1 small**, chopped
- Garlic — **3–4 cloves**
- Oil — **1½ tbsp**
- Mustard seeds — **½ tsp**
- Urad dal — **optional** (you can completely skip it)
- Curry leaves — **6–8**
- Salt — to taste
- Tamarind — **tiny piece / ½ tsp paste**, optional
- Sugar or jaggery — **¼ tsp**, optional, if tomatoes are very sour

### Method
1. Heat **1 tbsp oil**. Fry the **red chillies + garlic** for about 20–30 seconds.
2. Add onion and fry until soft.
3. Add chopped tomatoes and salt. Cook **7–10 minutes**, until the tomatoes become completely soft and most of the water evaporates.
4. Add tamarind if using. Let everything cool slightly.
5. Grind to a **slightly coarse or smooth chutney**. Usually **don't add water**; add only 1–2 tbsp if necessary.
6. Heat the remaining oil. Add mustard seeds and curry leaves. Once they splutter, pour over the chutney.

**For a stronger chilli chutney:** use **6–8 dry red chillies**, reduce onion to ½, and add **5–6 garlic cloves**.

And **no urad dal is required** for this chutney.

## 🍅 Simple Tomato Chutney — South Indian Style

This is **tomato chutney**, less chilli-heavy than the tomato-chilli chutney above.

### Ingredients
- Tomatoes — **3 large**
- Onion — **1 medium**
- Garlic — **3 cloves**
- Dry red chillies — **2–3**
- Oil — **1½ tbsp**
- Mustard seeds — **½ tsp**
- Curry leaves — **6–8**
- Salt — as needed
- Tamarind — **very small piece**, optional

**No urad dal needed.**

### Method

1. Heat **1 tbsp oil**.
2. Add dry red chillies and garlic; fry for **20–30 seconds**.
3. Add chopped onion. Fry until soft and slightly golden.
4. Add chopped tomatoes + salt.
5. Cook on **medium-low heat for 8–12 minutes** until the tomatoes are very soft and the raw smell disappears.
6. Cool for a few minutes, then **grind without water**. Add just a spoonful of water if the mixer needs it.
7. Heat **½ tbsp oil**, splutter mustard seeds, then add curry leaves.
8. Pour the tempering over the chutney and mix.

### For idli/dosa

Make it **slightly thick**, not watery. If you want the classic **Tamil hotel-style tomato chutney**, I can give you that version too.

## 🍅 Classic Tamil Hotel-Style Tomato Chutney

This is the **red, slightly tangy, moderately spicy chutney** commonly served with idli/dosa in Tamil Nadu hotels.

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Ripe tomatoes | 4 medium |
| Onion | 1 medium |
| Dry red chillies | 4–5 |
| Garlic | 4 cloves |
| Oil | 2 tbsp |
| Mustard seeds | ½ tsp |
| Curry leaves | 8–10 |
| Salt | As needed |
| Tamarind | Very small piece |
| Sugar/jaggery | ¼ tsp, optional |

**Urad dal is not necessary.**

### 👨‍🍳 Method

1. Heat **1½ tbsp oil** on medium heat.
2. Add **dry red chillies** and fry for about **20 seconds**. Don't burn them.
3. Add **garlic + chopped onion**. Fry for **3–4 minutes** until the onion turns soft and lightly golden.
4. Add **chopped tomatoes + salt + tiny piece of tamarind**.
5. Cook uncovered on medium-low for **10–15 minutes**. This is important—the tomatoes should become **completely mushy and jammy**, with the raw tomato smell gone.
6. Switch off and cool.
7. Grind until **fairly smooth**. Add only **1–3 tbsp water** if required.
8. Heat remaining **½ tbsp oil**. Add mustard seeds; once they splutter, add curry leaves.
9. Pour this tempering into the chutney.

### ⭐ For that hotel taste

The important part is **more tomato than onion + enough oil + thoroughly cooking the tomato**. Don't make it watery.

For **4 tomatoes**, start with **4 red chillies**. After grinding, taste it. If the tomatoes are very sour, mix in **¼ tsp sugar/jaggery**—not enough to make it sweet, just enough to balance the acidity.

**Best with:** hot idli, dosa, uthappam and paniyaram.

Yes — **coriander leaves can be added**, but for the **classic red Tamil hotel-style tomato chutney**, it’s usually **optional**, not a main ingredient.

For **4 tomatoes**, use about **2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves**. Add them **at the very end of cooking**, stir for 20–30 seconds, switch off, cool and grind.

If you add a **large handful**, it starts tasting more like tomato-coriander chutney and the colour becomes less bright red.

**For hotel-style:** 🍅 tomato dominant + 🌶️ red chilli + 🧄 garlic + 🧅 onion, with **just a little coriander if you like it**.
