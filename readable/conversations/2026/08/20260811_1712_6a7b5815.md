# 6. Recommended 30-day paid pilot

## Pilot objective

Prove that IPBS can create:

1. Faster and measurable enquiry response.
2. Fewer lost or forgotten prospects.
3. A visible nurse/caregiver candidate pipeline.
4. Better follow-up and interview attendance.
5. Timestamped deployment coordination.
6. Clear separation between operational and clinical responsibilities.
7. Daily management information for Methagu’s founder.

## Recommended starting scope

Start with **one or two service types**, preferably:

- Caregiver/attender services.
- Bedridden-patient care.

These appear to have relatively clear operational requirements and public pricing. Highly specialised nursing cases should enter the pilot only after Methagu has provided a clinical escalation process.

## Pilot channels

Begin with a controlled subset:

- One patient-enquiry phone or WhatsApp route.
- One candidate application source.
- One Methagu escalation owner.
- One agreed operating window.
- Tamil and English scripts.

Do not start by taking every number, every social account and every service line.

## Step-by-step pilot

### Stage 1 — Baseline and controls

Before IPBS handles live information:

- Map the existing enquiry process.
- Record current volumes by channel.
- Agree what information IPBS may collect.
- Define clinical escalation triggers.
- Define emergency wording.
- Agree operating hours.
- Establish Methagu and IPBS owners.
- Establish access and confidentiality controls.
- Create status definitions.
- Train coordinators using sample cases.

### Stage 2 — Patient-enquiry lane

For every enquiry:

1. Generate an enquiry ID.
2. Record date, time and source.
3. Confirm caller name and relationship to patient.
4. Record location, requested service, preferred start time and shift.
5. Record language or gender preference where relevant.
6. Avoid collecting unnecessary clinical detail.
7. Obtain or record the required privacy notice/consent.
8. Route the enquiry to Methagu’s authorised clinical or operations owner.
9. Track callback and outcome.
10. Continue approved follow-ups until closed.

### Stage 3 — Recruitment lane

For every candidate:

1. Generate a candidate ID.
2. Acknowledge the application.
3. Capture role, qualification and location.
4. Collect only approved documents.
5. Record document completeness.
6. Schedule Methagu’s interview.
7. Send reminders.
8. Record interview attendance.
9. Record Methagu’s approval, rejection or hold decision.
10. Capture availability for deployment.

IPBS must never mark someone as clinically qualified merely because documents were submitted.

### Stage 4 — Deployment administration

For an approved case:

1. Create a case/deployment ID.
2. Link the approved patient enquiry and approved candidate.
3. Confirm shift, date, location and reporting contact.
4. Obtain confirmation from both sides.
5. Record arrival/start status.
6. Escalate absence or delay.
7. Schedule first follow-up.
8. Track replacement or continuation.

# 9. Data protection and healthcare safeguards

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies to digital personal data processing, and the final Digital Personal Data Protection Rules were published in 2025. Methagu and IPBS therefore need clearly defined notice, purpose, access, retention, processor and security arrangements before handling patient, family or applicant information at scale. citeturn261077search1turn261077search6

At minimum, the pilot requires:

- Written data-processing and confidentiality terms.
- A clear statement of who is the data fiduciary and who is processing on its behalf.
- An approved patient-enquiry privacy notice.
- A separate recruitment privacy notice.
- Purpose-based data collection.
- Minimum necessary fields.
- Role-based access.
- Business-controlled accounts rather than personal accounts.
- Defined data-retention and deletion periods.
- Incident and mistaken-sharing procedure.
- Consent/notice before recording calls.
- No patient details in open WhatsApp groups.
- No copying of identification documents into uncontrolled personal devices.

Methagu should also confirm the applicability and status of registrations under Tamil Nadu clinical-establishment requirements and verify that nurses hold current professional registrations where required. The absence of publicly visible registration information does **not** prove non-compliance; it simply requires verification during due diligence. citeturn261077search0turn261077search3turn261077search7
