## One important working rule

Don't paste **both prompts into both AIs**.

The pattern should be:

```text
                    YOU
                     │
          ┌──────────┴──────────┐
          ↓                     ↓
       CLAUDE                CHATGPT
       BUILDER                REVIEWER
          │                     ▲
          │ V002 ZIP            │
          └─────────────────────┘
                     │
              Review findings
                     ↓
                  CLAUDE
                     │
                  V003 ZIP
                     ↓
                  CHATGPT
```

That separation is one of the useful things we've learned from LOS: **Claude should be allowed to build, while I remain sufficiently independent to challenge the architecture, implementation, tests, evidence and Claude's own claims rather than becoming a second co-author who simply approves them.** memcite
