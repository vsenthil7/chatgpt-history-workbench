Does upi id have @

### 0002 · CHATGPT · 2026-08-11T10:18:12Z

Yes. **A UPI ID normally contains `@`**.

Typical format:

**`username@provider`**

Examples:
- `9876543210@paytm`
- `name@okhdfcbank`
- `name@oksbi`
- `name@ybl`
- `mobilenumber@upi`

The part **before `@`** identifies you, and the part **after `@`** is the UPI handle/provider.
