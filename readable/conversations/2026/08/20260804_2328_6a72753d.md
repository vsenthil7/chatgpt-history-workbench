### Why is it called "Idly Rava"?

Although it is called **rava**, it is **not wheat semolina (sooji/rava)**.

**Idly rava = Coarsely ground rice.**

The rice is:
1. Cleaned.
2. Sometimes partially parboiled (depends on the brand).
3. Broken into small granules instead of being ground into flour.

It is specially made for preparing soft idlis.

### Why are there no cooking instructions?

Many Indian grocery brands assume customers already know the traditional recipes, so they often print only:
- Ingredients
- Storage instructions
- Allergy advice
- Best-before date

Unfortunately, many brands don't include recipes.

## Can it be used for dosa?

Yes.

Mix:
- Idly rava
- Urad dal
- Fenugreek seeds

Ferment overnight.

You'll get soft dosas.

---

## Can it replace normal rava (semolina)?

**No.**

You **cannot** use it for:
- Rava kesari
- Rava dosa
- Semolina upma
- Halwa

Those require **wheat semolina**, whereas this product is made entirely from **rice**.

## You can make today

### 1. Instant Rice Rava Upma (Easiest) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Ingredients:
- 1 cup idly rava
- 2 cups water
- 1 onion (optional)
- 1–2 green chillies
- Curry leaves (optional)
- Mustard seeds
- Oil or ghee
- Salt

Method:
1. Heat 1 tbsp oil.
2. Add mustard seeds.
3. Add onion, chilli, curry leaves and sauté.
4. Add 2 cups water and salt.
5. Bring to a boil.
6. Slowly add 1 cup idly rava while stirring.
7. Cover and cook for 8–10 minutes.

## Can you make idli without urad dal?

Yes, but it won't be traditional.

Options include:
- Mixing with yoghurt (curd) and a little baking soda or Eno for instant idlis.
- Using leftover cooked rice plus curd.

The texture will be different but still edible.
