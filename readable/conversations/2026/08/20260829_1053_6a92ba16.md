### 🥇 No. 1: *Logistics*

Guinness currently identifies **Logistics (2012)** as the **longest film made**, at **857 hours** — **35 days and 17 hours**. citeturn0search0turn0search26

It isn't a conventional story movie. Swedish artists **Erika Magnusson and Daniel Andersson** followed the supply-chain journey of a pedometer, backwards from a Stockholm shop to the factory in Shenzhen, China, essentially in real time. citeturn0search0

So watching it continuously would mean:

**Start:** Saturday 29 August  
**Finish:** roughly **5 weeks later**. 😄

### Important complication: *Ambiancé*

You'll sometimes see **Ambiancé** ranked #2 at **720 hours / 30 days**. But that's questionable because although the project was announced with that runtime, its planned full screening apparently **never occurred**. So I wouldn't rank it as confidently as *Logistics*. citeturn0search27

Also, **The Cure for Insomnia** used to be widely described as the world's longest film. It's **87 hours**, but *Logistics* is nearly **10× longer**. citeturn0search24turn0search0
