## Why this is defective

An RTM must distinguish between:

```text
requirement exists
```

and:

```text
requirement is mechanically enforced
```

Marking a universal consistency claim **ENFORCED** while the released package itself contains a counterexample weakens the purpose of the RTM.

The safer alternatives are:

1. strengthen the gate until the statement becomes true; or
2. narrow the RTM requirement to precisely what is currently checked.

It must not claim more assurance than the gate provides.
