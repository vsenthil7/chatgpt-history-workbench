# Basic Tamil-Style Mor Kuzhambu

## 🥣 Core Taste Profile
- Sour + cooling from curd/buttermilk
- Mild spice
- Coconut body
- Light tang from turmeric + green chilli
- Tempering aroma gives final identity

---

# Ingredients

## Main
- Thick curd / yogurt – 2 cups
- Water – 1 to 1.5 cups
- Turmeric – ¼ tsp
- Salt

## Vegetables (choose one)
- Ash gourd (white pumpkin)
- Ladies finger (okra)
- Pumpkin
- Brinjal

## Grind Paste
- Coconut – ½ cup
- Green chilli – 2 to 4
- Cumin – 1 tsp
- Soaked raw rice – 1 tsp  
  *(or soaked chana dal 1 tsp)*

Optional:
- Small piece ginger
- 2–3 soaked cashews for creamy body

---

# Tempering
- Coconut oil / gingelly oil
- Mustard
- Fenugreek few seeds
- Dry red chilli
- Curry leaves
- Hing

---

# Step-by-Step

## 1️⃣ Cook vegetable
Cook chosen vegetable with:
- turmeric
- little salt
- little water

Do not overcook.

---

## 2️⃣ Grind
Grind:
- coconut
- green chilli
- cumin
- soaked rice

into smooth paste.

---

## 3️⃣ Prepare curd mix
Whisk curd + water until smooth.

Add:
- ground paste
- cooked vegetable

---

## 4️⃣ Heat carefully
Very important:
- LOW flame only
- Do NOT boil heavily

Heat until:
- slight foam
- steam rises
- tiny bubbles at edge

If boiled hard:
- curd may split
- taste becomes harsh

---

## 5️⃣ Temper
Add hot tempering on top.

Done.

---

# Variants & What Changes

| Addition | Effect |
|---|---|
| Coconut more | Richer, traditional |
| Cashew | Creamy hotel-style texture |
| Rice | Slight thickness + stability |
| Chana dal | Nutty body |
| Ginger | Fresh sharpness |
| Garlic | Rare in traditional mor kuzhambu |
| Coconut oil | Kerala aroma |
| Gingelly oil | Tamil home taste |

# Important Science

## Why low flame?
Curd proteins tighten and separate when overheated.

## Why rice added?
genui{"math_block_widget_always_prefetch_v2":{"content":"\\text{Starch} + \\text{Heat} \\rightarrow \\text{Thickening \\& Stabilization}"}}

Rice starch stabilizes the curry and reduces splitting.

## Why fenugreek little only?
Too much:
- turns bitter
- dominates curd flavor

# Simple Ratio System

## Light Version
- 2 cup curd
- ¼ cup coconut

## Medium Traditional
- 2 cup curd
- ½ cup coconut

## Rich Feast Version
- 2 cup curd
- ½ cup coconut
- 3 cashews
