## 3.2 MI300X availability realities

AMD MI300X is attractive because of **192GB HBM3 memory**, which helps with larger models and larger context/runtime workloads. AMD documentation and product material position MI300X for large AI/LLM workloads with high memory bandwidth. citeturn355764search2turn355764search20

```text
You cannot assume unlimited MI300X access.
You cannot assume hackathon credits are enough for continuous testing.
You cannot assume every model runs cleanly without tuning.
You cannot assume CUDA-first libraries behave identically on ROCm.
```
