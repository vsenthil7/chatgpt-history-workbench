## 📘 What is “Catch-22”?

**entity["book","Catch-22","novel by Joseph Heller"]** is a famous novel, and the term has become part of everyday language.

### 🔁 Meaning:
A **Catch-22** is a situation where:
- You’re stuck in a **no-win loop**
- The **rules contradict each other**
- Solving the problem is impossible because the **solution depends on the problem itself**

---

## 🧠 Simple Example

> You need experience to get a job  
> But you need a job to get experience  

➡️ That’s a **Catch-22**

---

## ✈️ Origin (from the novel)

In the story:
- A pilot can avoid dangerous missions if he is **mentally unfit**
- But if he *asks* to be excused, it proves he is **sane enough to fly**

➡️ So he can **never escape**  
➡️ That’s the Catch-22
