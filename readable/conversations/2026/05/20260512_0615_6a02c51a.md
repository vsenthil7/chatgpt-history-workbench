# Track 1 — Agent Security & AI Governance

## Product Idea  
# **Verixa TrustOps**
### Enterprise AI Runtime Governance & Audit Platform

## Business Requirement

Enterprises need a runtime layer that can control AI agents before they take business actions.

Not just prompt filtering.  
Not just logging.

They need:

- who asked
- what AI decided
- what system was touched
- what data was used
- what policy was triggered
- what action was blocked or approved
- what evidence exists for audit

## Problem Statement

AI agents are being connected to real systems:

- CRM
- ERP
- finance tools
- HR systems
- email
- ticketing
- internal APIs

But enterprises do not have a proper control layer.

Current risk:

> “AI can act faster than governance can understand.”

## Main Users

| User | Need |
|---|---|
| CISO | Stop AI misuse, leakage, unsafe execution |
| Compliance Officer | Audit trail and regulatory evidence |
| CTO / Enterprise Architect | Central governance layer |
| AI Platform Team | Runtime monitoring and controls |
| Business Process Owner | Safe AI automation |
| Legal/Risk Team | Explainability and accountability |

## Core Use Cases

### Use Case 1 — Governed AI Action Approval

An AI agent wants to send customer refund approval.

System checks:

1. Who requested it?
2. What data was used?
3. Is refund amount within limit?
4. Is customer category sensitive?
5. Was policy violated?
6. Should it auto-approve, block, or require human review?

## User Story

> As a compliance officer, I want every AI-driven business action to be logged with reason, policy, risk score, and evidence so that I can defend the decision during audit.

## Step-by-Step Scenario

1. Sales manager asks AI:  
   “Approve discount for this enterprise customer.”

2. AI agent prepares action.

3. Verixa TrustOps intercepts the action.

4. It checks:
   - user role
   - customer risk category
   - discount threshold
   - policy rules
   - previous customer history

5. System detects discount exceeds policy limit.

6. Action is not executed.

7. Human approval is requested.

8. Full audit replay is stored.
