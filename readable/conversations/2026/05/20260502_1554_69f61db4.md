| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|

### Step 5 — User asks grounded question

Example:

> “Summarize all tender documents and show missing compliance items.”

AI does:

1. checks permission,
2. searches documents,
3. retrieves relevant chunks,
4. sends context to model,
5. produces answer with sources.

### Step 6 — Risk layer checks answer

Checks for:

| Risk | Action |
|---|---|
| PII leak | redact/block |
| legal advice risk | warning/approval |
| wrong source | ask clarification |
| financial risk | require human review |
| external sharing | block unless approved |

### Step 7 — Audit log stores trace

Log keeps:

> who asked, what data used, model, output, action, time, region, approval.
