# ✅ 1. What Your BPO Profile MUST Include

Keep it simple, but professional:

### 1. Company Overview
- Name of your startup
- What you do (BPO services)
- Your unique idea (housewives working remotely)

### 2. Vision & Mission
- Why you started
- What problem you solve

### 3. Services Offered
Examples:
- Customer Support (Call / Chat / Email)
- Data Entry
- Lead Generation
- Order Processing
- Social Media Handling

### 4. Target Clients
- Who you work with (schools, clinics, e-commerce, etc.)

### 5. Your Strength (Very Important)
- Trained housewives workforce
- Flexible working hours
- Cost-effective
- Regional language support (Tamil – big advantage!)

### 6. Work Process
Explain how you handle work:
- Training
- Task assignment
- Quality check
- Reporting

### 7. Tools & Skills
- CRM tools
- Excel
- Communication tools (WhatsApp, email, etc.)

### 8. Experience (Even small counts)
- Your personal experience
- Any previous work handled

### 9. Contact Details
- Name
- Phone
- Email
