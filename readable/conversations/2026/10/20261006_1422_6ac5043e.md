### Outbound has an important restriction

You **cannot simply take 10,000 mobile numbers and start WhatsApp-calling them.**

The WhatsApp user must first give the business permission to call. Meta supports several permission mechanisms, including a permission-request message and callback permission after the customer has called the business. citeturn1search4

So:

```text
WhatsApp message
      ↓
"Allow this business to call me?"
      ↓
Customer allows
      ↓
TalkProof can initiate WhatsApp call
      ↓
AI / human conversation
```

That's very different from ordinary outbound PSTN dialling.

### And Twilio already supports this

Interestingly, urlTwilio's WhatsApp Business Calling documentationturn1search2 confirms both inbound and outbound WhatsApp calling.

But there's a major boundary: **Twilio says WhatsApp endpoints cannot be bridged to PSTN endpoints.** citeturn1search2

So we shouldn't model WhatsApp as merely another telephone carrier.
