**No. At present, it does *not* work like Google sponsored search across paid ChatGPT users.**

If I am on **Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise or Edu** and ask:

your paid ChatGPT advertisement **cannot be inserted into my results**. OpenAI explicitly says those plans do **not** receive ads. citeturn0search0turn0search2

Google is different:

```text
GOOGLE

Free Google user ────────┐
                         ├─ Search "call centre QA software"
Paid/Workspace user ─────┘
                              ↓
                       Sponsored results
                              ↓
                         Our product
```

Whereas currently:

```text
CHATGPT

Free ───────┐
Go ─────────┼── Relevant conversation
            │          ↓
            │      Sponsored ad
            │          ↓
            │      Our product
            │
Plus ───────┐
Pro ────────┤
Business ───┤── ❌ NO ADS
Enterprise ─┤
Edu ────────┘
```

OpenAI also makes an important distinction: **buying an ad does not buy ranking inside ChatGPT's answer.** Ads are separate from the model response and appear below it; advertisers cannot pay to shape, rank or alter the answer. citeturn0search2

### So this makes your enterprise concern stronger

Imagine a CTO on **ChatGPT Enterprise** searches:

> “Compare the best enterprise voice QA platforms.”

We **cannot pay OpenAI to put TalkProof at #1, #2 or #3** in that answer.

That's fundamentally unlike Google Search Ads.

The only way our product gets into that organic ChatGPT answer is because ChatGPT's retrieval/model considers it relevant—not because we paid for advertising.

And there is an interesting future development: OpenAI is testing **Sponsored Agents**, where someone can open an ad and actually converse with an AI representative of the advertiser. But that's currently a **limited alpha for selected advertisers**, not generally available. citeturn0search16

### My revised channel view

For our **enterprise products**, this makes:

**Google Search Ads potentially more directly valuable than ChatGPT Ads** for capturing enterprise buyers with explicit purchase intent, because ChatGPT's most valuable paid/business audience is deliberately excluded from advertising.

ChatGPT Ads could still be useful for **SMB/self-service versions** of our products.

That distinction should stay on this page because it materially changes the commercial case for us.
