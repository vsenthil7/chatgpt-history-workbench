## 10/10/2026 13:23 — Unexpected Outskill Registration

Yes, partner — **this email is unusual if you definitely did not register for this event.** But the email itself gives us a strong clue.

### The biggest clue

Zoom says:

> **“Hi StayingAhead Form,”**

It does **not** address you by your name.

Yet the registration was created using your Yahoo address, and Zoom generated a **personal registration token/link** for it.

That suggests something submitted your email address to the webinar registration form. It does **not** by itself mean your Yahoo or Zoom account was compromised.

### Most likely explanations

1. **An Outskill/partner form automatically registered you.**  
   You may previously have entered this email into an Outskill form, AI course/masterclass page, lead magnet, waiting list, advertisement, or another form. Their automation could have registered that lead for today's webinar.

2. **A bulk/imported registration happened.**  
   The strange name **“StayingAhead Form”** is particularly interesting. It sounds more like a form/campaign/source label than a person's name. They may have pushed contacts from a marketing system into Zoom Webinar.

3. **Someone entered your email address.**  
   Possible, including a typo or somebody registering on your behalf, but the `"StayingAhead Form"` value makes an automated workflow more plausible.

4. **Account compromise.**  
   Possible in principle, but **nothing you've shown establishes this**. Receiving a genuine Zoom registration confirmation only establishes that Zoom received a registration containing your email address.

### One more important point

The sender shown is `no-reply@zoom.us`, and the links you've pasted are on `us06web.zoom.us`. That is consistent with a Zoom-generated webinar confirmation rather than an ordinary marketing email pretending to contain a Zoom link.

But I would **not click the personalized Join/Cancel links yet** while we're investigating.

### Why I think we should investigate this one

The message arrived at **12:01 UK time today**, so we have a very precise point to work backwards from.

The key question isn't initially *“Did someone access Yahoo?”*.

It's:

**Where did Outskill obtain `v_senthil7@yahoo.com`, and what caused a Zoom registration to be created at around 12:01 today?**

Search your Yahoo inbox for:

`Outskill`  
`Generative AI Mastermind`  
`StayingAhead`  
`Staying Ahead`

Also look for earlier Outskill emails. If we find that your email has been in their system for weeks/months, this becomes much easier to explain.

Given the security investigation we were already doing around your Yahoo/Chrome activity, I would **keep this as a separate evidence item rather than immediately connecting the two incidents**. Same day does not establish the same cause.

If you paste the **earliest Outskill email you can find**, I'll trace backwards and tell you whether this looks like marketing automation, an old registration being reused, someone registering you, or something that warrants a security response. memcite
