## Your recordings expose a bigger requirement

The browser agent shouldn't merely do:

> click where I tell you.

You should be able to say something like:

> **"Find out exactly what my current plan gives me, including AI/Flow limits. Gather evidence and compare the options."**

Then it should navigate the site, open the relevant pages, extract the information, preserve source/evidence and return the result.

Or:

> **"Take this 40-video playlist and build the learning course."**

Then it should process all 40 without you repeatedly copying URLs, transcripts and errors between systems.

That is substantially more valuable than basic RPA.

---

# But I would NOT give it unrestricted desktop control

This connects directly to the privacy boundary we've already established.

We don't need:

```text
AI watches your entire computer continuously
AI reads everything
AI records everything
```

Instead:

```text
Explicit task
     ↓
Explicit browser/profile/folder
     ↓
Agent receives temporary control
     ↓
Every action logged
     ↓
Sensitive/destructive action → human approval
     ↓
Task completed
     ↓
Control terminates
```

Examples of approval boundaries:

**Automatic:** navigate, search, scroll, download an allowed file, extract page text, process playlist items.

**Approval required:** send email/message, buy something, submit forms, delete something, change account/security settings, publish content.

That fits our humane automation principle much better.
