# 15. HUMAN VISIBILITY MUST REMAIN

AI-readable knowledge alone is insufficient.

Conversation pages are valuable because humans can read the progression of thought.

We therefore need both:

**human narrative/history**

and

**machine-reusable structured knowledge**.

Design how both can coexist without duplicating everything unnecessarily.

# 16. TEE — TIME, ENERGY, EFFORT

Treat human TEE as a primary architecture metric.

Measure/estimate:

- manual copying;
- manual exporting;
- searching;
- context rebuilding;
- duplicate research;
- AI handover preparation;
- filing;
- recovery.

The architecture should reduce these substantially.

Automation that saves AI tokens but increases human cognitive load is NOT automatically an improvement.

# 17. DO NOT MIGRATE EVERYTHING BLINDLY

Before historical migration, define:

- what deserves preservation;
- what deserves summarisation;
- what deserves full transcript preservation;
- what can be discarded;
- what contains sensitive information;
- retention classes.

We may have hundreds of conversations. Treat migration as an information architecture problem, not a bulk-copy exercise.
