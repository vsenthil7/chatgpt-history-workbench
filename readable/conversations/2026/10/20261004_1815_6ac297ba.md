## 04/10/2026 19:15 — OpenAI Certification Explained

Partner, it means **there are currently two different things**, and the wording is easy to confuse.

| | **OpenAI Academy** | **OpenAI Certified** |
|---|---|---|
| Available to you as an individual? | **Yes** | **Generally no, currently** |
| Cost | **Free** | Enterprise/Edu programme |
| Courses | Yes | Yes |
| Assessment | Yes | Yes |
| Credential | Badge + pathway **certificate of completion** | OpenAI-issued credential |
| Is it an official *certification*? | **No** | **Yes / certification programme** |
| Current access | Globally available with ChatGPT account | **Invite-only Enterprise & Edu workspaces** |

OpenAI explicitly says Academy badges are earned by completing the course and passing its assessment at **80%+**. Completing all courses/assessments in an eligible pathway gives you a **pathway certificate of completion**. But OpenAI explicitly distinguishes these from a professional **OpenAI Certification**. citeturn0search0

### So what should *you* do?

For your technical-founder/freelancer direction, **I would still do the Academy technical pathways**.

For example, the **API pathway** currently contains:

1. Scope AI Solutions
2. Evaluate AI Applications
3. Design and Build Agentic Systems
4. Build with Retrieval-Augmented Generation

But you **shouldn't write**:

### Then what is the real "OpenAI Certified"?

That's a separate programme. OpenAI says the **OpenAI Certified** experience is currently available only to eligible **ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu workspaces, invite-only**. It uses Coursera for learning/assessment and Credly for credentials. citeturn0search1

So if you're operating as an **individual freelancer/technical founder**, you cannot currently treat OpenAI Certified as a qualification you can simply register for, study for and obtain on demand.

**Therefore the sentence you quoted basically means:**

And there's an important point for **you specifically**: the Academy API pathway isn't merely a badge exercise.
