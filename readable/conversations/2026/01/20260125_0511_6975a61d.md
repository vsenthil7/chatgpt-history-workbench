### At-TE-202601-2-S09-13-CT-Session-9-Intuitive Thinking  

**Session Goal (for you, not to read aloud):**  
Help the student trust their **gut feelings**, express preferences **without fear**, and realise that **there’s no single “right” answer** in intuitive thinking.

**Total Time:** ~45–50 mins  
**Tone:** Light, conversational, non-judgmental  
**Key Rule:** No correcting answers — only asking *why*

---

## Activity 1 – Icebreaker: Perfect Slogan (5 mins)

### How to run it
1. Read the question slowly.
2. Let the student answer first.
3. Acknowledge → share your own.

### Question to ask
> “Tell me one famous dialogue or slogan that best describes your life.”

### If the student gets stuck, nudge with:
- “It can be from a movie, ad, meme, or even something your parents say.”
- “No need to explain too much — just say what feels right.”

### Your role (important)
- Smile, laugh, normalize.
- Avoid analysis like *“deep meaning”*.
- If answer is funny or simple → **validate it**.

💡 *Hidden learning:*  
Life choices can be described intuitively, not logically.

---

## Activity 2 – This or That (20 mins)

### How to run it (very important)
For **each question**, follow this flow:
1. Ask the question
2. Student answers
3. Ask **“Why?”**
4. You answer + your reason

👉 Emphasise:
> “There’s no correct answer. Just what feels right to you.”

---

### Questions + Mentor Cues

You **don’t need to ask all** — 6–8 is enough.

1. **Cotton dress / Woollen dress during summer?**  
   *Prompt:* Comfort vs habit

2. **Friday night: movie at home / go out with friends?**  
   *Prompt:* Energy level matters

3. **Big test Monday: study all weekend / take Sunday off?**  
   *Prompt:* Stress vs balance

4. **Won ₹1000: save / spend on something fun?**  
   *Prompt:* Security vs happiness

5. **New city: close to office / trendy neighbourhood?**  
   *Prompt:* Time vs lifestyle

6. **Free weekend: relax at home / quick trip?**  
   *Prompt:* Rest vs excitement

7. **Dinner plans: new restaurant / familiar favourite?**  
   *Prompt:* Curiosity vs comfort

8. **Raining Saturday: read at home / mall shopping?**  
   *Prompt:* Mood-based choices

9. **Two invites: party / concert?**  
   *Prompt:* Social vs experience

10. **Gift coupon: buy clothes / save it?**  
    *Prompt:* Immediate joy vs later use

---

### If the student keeps asking “Which is better?”
Say this line clearly:
> “In intuitive thinking, *your reason* makes it right.”

---

## Activity 3 – What Would Happen If… (15 mins)

### First: Explain the idea
Say:
> “Here, we’re not guessing facts. We’re imagining effects.”

### Explain the example briefly
- Technology gone → daily life changes
- Some problems, some benefits

---

### Ask one question at a time

#### Question 1  
**What would happen if we had to live in a world without electricity?**

👉 Prompt with:
- Home
- School
- Travel
- Night time

---

#### Question 2  
**What would happen if all plants on Earth disappeared?**

👉 Prompt with:
- Food
- Oxygen
- Animals
- Humans

---

#### Question 3  
**What would happen if animals could talk?**

👉 Prompt with:
- Pets
- Zoos
- Forests
- Humans’ behaviour

---

### Your role
- Let answers be messy.
- Encourage **multiple points**.
- If answer is unrealistic → don’t correct, ask *“What else?”*

💡 *Hidden learning:*  
Beliefs shape how we imagine the world.

---

## Learning of the Day (5 mins)

### Ask exactly this:
> “What is one thing you learned today?”

If the student struggles, give options:
- “Did you learn something about your choices?”
- “Did you feel more confident answering?”

### Accept any answer.  
Then gently link to one of these:
- Making quick decisions
- Expressing beliefs freely
- Understanding personal preferences

---

## Activity 4 – Homework Discussion + New Homework (5 mins)

### Part 1: Discuss previous homework  
**Topic:** Should arts and crafts be part of education?

Ask:
- “What did you think?”
- “Any one point you liked most?”

Do **not** turn this into a debate.

---

### Part 2: Explain new homework

#### Homework Topic  
**Difference between Corporate Professional and Business Man**

Explain simply:
- Corporate professional → works for a company
- Businessman → runs their own business

Give examples:
- Job security vs risk
- Fixed salary vs variable income
- Following rules vs making rules

### Homework instruction (clear)
> “Research, think, and write **any 5 points**.  
Simple language is enough. We’ll discuss next session.”

---

## End-of-Session Reminders (1 min)

Say clearly:
- “Please mark today’s attendance.”
- “I’ll mark mine too.”

- Did I ask *why* instead of *what is right*?
