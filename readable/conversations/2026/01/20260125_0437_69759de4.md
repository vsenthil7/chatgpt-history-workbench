Activity 1 - Icebreaker - Reverse order (5 mins)

This activity makes you and the student comfortable with each other.

Instructions:

Tell the names of three random objects.
Example: Chair, Exam, Pen.
Ask the student to repeat the names of the objects in the reverse order.
Example: Pen, Exam, Chair.
In upcoming rounds, increase the count of words to 5, 7, 9, etc.
Next, ask the student to tell five words.
Now, you have to tell the five words in the reverse order.
Take turns and repeat this till time permits.
Help the student wherever required.

Activity 2 - Self-Introduction (20 mins)

This activity helps you and the student get to know each other and set event expectations.

Introduction

You and the student self introduce each other with the help of the following questions.

Tell about yourself:
Where are you from?
Your educational Background.
Work experience and about your current company (If any).
Your professional roles (If any).
Talk about any 5 of your favourite things.
Example: Food, celebrities, etc.
Speak about your hobbies and how did you get into them?
Share your favourite place with reason.
Share your nicknames (If any).
What is one thing that can instantly make your day better?
What is the best joke you have ever heard?
Do you ever cook in your home?
Do you have a pet or want to have a pet? If yes, which pet animal?
What is your favourite movie type?
Examples: Action movies, comedy movies, etc.

Setting expectations for the upcoming sessions.

Discuss the following pointers with the student to set the expectations for the upcoming sessions.

Each session focuses on building Creative Thinking skills.
Each session is a combination of fun games, activities, and concepts.
The student must actively engage and participate.
The student must complete the homework regularly.
Share your expectations for this event.
Ask the student: What are your expectations from the event?

Activity 3 - Introduction to Creative Thinking (15 mins)

This activity helps the student learn about the event.

Name: Set your Event Goal

Instructions:

Ask the student to create a vision for this event and share 3 goals to achieve it.
Give the student 4 to 5 minutes to think and answer.
Encourage the student to think about their strengths and weaknesses.
Help the student wherever required.

<aside> 🎋 Example:

Vision: After the event, I want to see myself as an innovative and resourceful thinker.

Goal 1: I will practice my thinking process.
Goal 2: I will keep “Thinking out of the box” in my mind.
Goal 3: I will improve my way of seeing things. </aside>
