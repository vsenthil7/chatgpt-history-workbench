KV-Cache Wins You Can See: Scaling LLM Inference with llm-d
Modern LLMs are fundamentally constrained not just by raw compute, but by how they reuse work. The KV-cache - the state of prior tokens - collapses expensive recomputation into fast lookups. Yet in distributed clusters, traditional load-balancing destroys this locality and wastes GPU cycles. In this session, we'll demystify why KV-cache hit rates are the single most important metric for cost-efficient LLM inference and how llm-d restores and amplifies these wins. You'll learn how cache-aware scheduling delivers order-of-magnitude improvements in latency and throughput on the same hardware. We'll break down core concepts - from automatic prefix caching in single-node deployments to precise global cache scheduling enabling efficient and effective production-grade AI inference systems.

Judging Criteria

Each category will be judged on a scale from 1 - 5 with a total of 20 possible points.

1. Completeness and feasibility (5 points): How feasible is the solution? How fully has the idea been thought-out and planned? How complete is the proof-of-concept submitted? How clear is the application of IBM technology?
2. Creativity and innovation (5 points): How unique and original was the approach in applying AI technology to address the stated issue? Is the solution differentiated in the market?
3. Design and usability (5 points): How good is the design, user experience, and ease-of-use of the solution? How quickly and easily could it be put to use in real-world scenarios and adopted by target users?
4. Effectiveness and efficiency (5 points): Does the solution address a high priority and relevant issue within the hackathon theme? Does it achieve its goal effectively and efficiently? Can it achieve a measurable impact in the field? Does it have potential to scale to more users or use cases?
