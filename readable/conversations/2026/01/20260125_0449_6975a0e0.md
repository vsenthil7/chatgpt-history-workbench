<aside> <img src="/icons/bounce_red.svg" alt="/icons/bounce_red.svg" width="40px" /> Technique - SCAMPER

SCAMPER is a methodology to bring in new creative ideas.

You can use the tool by asking questions about existing products.

These questions help you come up with creative ideas for developing new products and for improving current ones.

SCAMPER

S → Substitute

C → Combine

A → Adapt

M → Modify/Minify/Magnify

P → Put to another use

E → Eliminate/Elaborate

R → Reverse/Rearrange

</aside>

Activity - SCAMPER with a Pencil

<aside> 1️⃣ Substitute Explanation:

It is the process of thinking about changing a part of the existing product with the help of another product.

Questions to ask:

What materials or resources can you substitute or exchange to improve the pencil?
What other product or process could you use?

Possible Answer: Use a rubber grip instead of wood for better comfort.

</aside>

<aside> 2️⃣ Combine Explanation:

It is a process of thinking about combining two or more parts of the product to make something new and to produce greater use and effect.

Questions to ask:

What could you combine to maximise the uses of the pencil?
What would happen if you combine the pencil with another product to create something new?

Possible Answer: Attach a small ruler to the side of the pencil.

</aside>

<aside> 3️⃣ Adapt Explanation:

Process of thinking about which parts of the product or process could be adjusted.

Questions to ask:

How could you adapt/adjust the pencil to make another purpose or use?
What other products or ideas could you use for inspiration?
Who or what could you follow to adapt/adjust the pencil?

Possible Answer: Add a clip to the pencil so it can be attached to a notebook.

</aside>

<aside> 4️⃣ Modify/Minify/Magnify Explanation:

Process of thinking is a way of changing a certain part or all parts of the product or twisting it in an unusual way.

Questions to ask:

What could you add to modify the pencil?
How could you change the pencil's shape, look, or feel?
What element of the pencil could you strengthen to create something new?

Possible Answer: Make the pencil triangular for easier holding.

</aside>

<aside> 5️⃣ Put to another use Explanation:

Process of changing when, where, or how to use it according to needs.

Questions to ask:

Can you use the pencil somewhere else, perhaps in another industry?
How would the pencil behave differently in another setting?
Could you recycle the waste from the pencil to make something new?

Possible Answer: Use the pencil's end as a touch screen stylus (A pen-shaped instrument designed for touch screen use).

</aside>

<aside> 6️⃣ Eliminate/Elaborate Explanation:

Process of thinking about the product in the end if you eliminate certain parts of the product or process and consider whether it is right or wrong.

Questions to ask:

How could you simplify the pencil?
What features, parts, or rules could you eliminate?
How could you make it smaller, faster, lighter, or more fun?
What would happen if you took away part of the pencil? What would you have in its place?

Possible Answer: Remove the need for sharpening by using refillable lead.

</aside>

<aside> 7️⃣ Reverse/Rearrange Explanation:

Process of thinking what will happen if the parts or process of the product are reversed or rearranged.

Questions to ask:

What would happen if you reversed the above process or sequenced things differently?
What if you try to do the exact opposite of what you are trying to do now?
How could you reorganise the pencil?
What roles could you reverse or swap?

Possible Answer: Create a pencil that writes with both ends, one with black and the other with coloured lead.

</aside>

## 🟢 Activity 1 – Icebreaker: *Make a Change* (5 mins)

### 🎯 Purpose  
Loosen thinking, remove fear of “wrong answers”.

### 🗣 How to Run (Script-style)
> “Imagine you’ve won **₹50 Lakhs** in a lottery.  
> You must spend *all of it within 24 hours*.  
> You **cannot save or invest** any amount.  
> Whatever is left will disappear.  
> What will you do?”

### Flow
1. **Student answers first**
2. You listen — *no judgement*
3. You share **your own answer** (can be practical or wild)
4. If student freezes → prompt gently:
   - “For people?”
   - “For fun?”
   - “For learning?”

✅ **No correction here. Only encouragement.**

---

## 🟢 Activity 2 – Creative Tool: **SCAMPER with a Pencil** (20 mins)

### 🎯 Purpose  
Teach a **repeatable creativity technique**, not “ideas”.

### Setup (1 min)
Ask student to keep:
- Pen
- Paper / notebook

Explain simply:
> “SCAMPER is a way to ask smart questions about an existing object to create new ideas.”

---

### 🔁 SCAMPER Walkthrough (Follow this pattern EVERY step)

For **each letter**:
1. Explain meaning (1 line)
2. Ask questions
3. Student answers
4. You add example **only if needed**

---

### ✏️ SCAMPER Breakdown – Pencil

#### **S – Substitute**
**Meaning:** Replace one part with something else  
**Ask:**
- “What material can we replace?”
- “What else can do the same job?”
**Example (if needed):**
> Rubber grip instead of wood

---

#### **C – Combine**
**Meaning:** Merge two things  
**Ask:**
- “What can we attach to the pencil?”
- “What two tools can become one?”
**Example:**  
> Pencil + ruler

---

#### **A – Adapt**
**Meaning:** Adjust for another purpose  
**Ask:**
- “How can this copy something else?”
- “Who uses something similar?”
**Example:**  
> Clip to attach to notebook

---

#### **M – Modify / Minify / Magnify**
**Meaning:** Change size, shape, strength  
**Ask:**
- “What if it’s bigger/smaller?”
- “What shape change helps?”
**Example:**  
> Triangular pencil

---

#### **P – Put to Another Use**
**Meaning:** Use it elsewhere  
**Ask:**
- “Can it work in tech?”
- “Can waste become useful?”
**Example:**  
> Touchscreen stylus

---

#### **E – Eliminate / Elaborate**
**Meaning:** Remove or simplify  
**Ask:**
- “What part can we remove?”
- “What makes it simpler?”
**Example:**  
> Refillable lead (no sharpening)

---

#### **R – Reverse / Rearrange**
**Meaning:** Flip roles or order  
**Ask:**
- “What if opposite?”
- “What if both ends work?”
**Example:**  
> Dual-ended pencil (black + colour)

---

✅ Reinforce once:
> “See — creativity is not magic. It’s **asking better questions**.”

## 🟢 Activity 3 – Chair Transformation Challenge (15 mins)
