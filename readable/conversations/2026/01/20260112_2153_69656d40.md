## Project Story (Markdown)

### ## Inspiration

Modern digital money is programmable in theory, but most systems stop at simple transfers.  
We were inspired by a gap we kept seeing in enterprise finance: **tokens move, but intent, policy, and accountability do not**.

MNEE provided a compliant ERC-20 foundation. Our goal was to explore:  
> _What if money could carry rules, context, and auditability — not just value?_

This project is an experiment in turning MNEE into a **governable financial runtime**, not just a payment rail.

---

### ## What it does

PortView is a **programmable treasury layer** built on MNEE that enables:

- Policy-controlled MNEE disbursements  
- Treasury segmentation (operational, reserve, escrow)  
- Rule-based approvals and execution  
- Full on-chain + off-chain audit trails  
- AI-assisted orchestration for monitoring and validation  

At its core, PortView treats MNEE not as a passive token, but as **executable financial infrastructure**.

---

### ## How we built it

- **Smart Contracts (Ethereum)**  
  - ERC-20 MNEE integration  
  - Treasury vaults, role-based execution, policy guards  

- **AI-Assisted Orchestration Layer**  
  - Validates intent before execution  
  - Enforces idempotency and deadline guards  
  - Generates audit metadata for every transaction  

- **Backend & Interfaces**  
  - Service layer to trigger treasury actions  
  - Read-only dashboards for balances, flows, and logs  

- **Testing & Validation**  
  - Contract-level unit tests  
  - Manual treasury flow simulations  
  - Failure and rollback scenarios  

The architecture was designed to be **enterprise-grade first**, not a demo script.

---

### ## Challenges we ran into

- Mapping real-world treasury controls into deterministic smart-contract logic  
- Preventing double execution and replay attacks  
- Balancing AI assistance with **non-custodial, deterministic guarantees**  
- Designing audit trails that are meaningful to humans, not just machines  

---

### ## Accomplishments that we're proud of

- A working programmable treasury using **real MNEE (ERC-20)**  
- Policy-driven execution instead of ad-hoc transfers  
- Clear separation between **decision intelligence (AI)** and **execution authority (contracts)**  
- A foundation that can scale from hackathon prototype to enterprise kernel  

---

### ## What we learned

- Programmable money is as much about **governance** as it is about code  
- AI is most powerful when it **advises and validates**, not when it controls funds  
- Enterprises need explainability and auditability more than raw automation  

---

### ## What's next for PortView

- Multi-signature and DAO-style governance  
- Advanced policy DSL for treasury rules  
- Real-time risk scoring before execution  
- Deeper analytics on MNEE flow patterns  
- Production-ready SaaS deployment
