Activity 1 - Icebreaker - Twisted Tongues (5 mins)

Instructions:

Share the given tongue twisters with the student.
You and the student:
Choose one tongue twister.
Take turns to tell the chosen tongue twister for a minute as many times as possible.
Whoever tells correctly and continuously, gets the point.
Keep the activity fun and interesting.
Help the student wherever required.

<aside> 😎 Tongue Twisters:

She sells seashells by the seashore.
I scream you scream, we all scream for ice cream.
Light the night light tonight.
The blue bluebird blinks. </aside>

Activity 2 - Connect the words (20 mins)

This activity helps the student to associate things.

Instructions:

You and the student:

Use pen and paper.

Write down 10 objects separately without telling them.

Once completed, both of you should tell the first word from the respective list.

Example: If your word is 'Fan' and the student's word is 'Apple'.

Each of you write a sentence using those two words.

Example:

You: Ram was sitting under the fan when his mother gave him an apple to eat.

Student: Geetha switched on the fan and sat down to eat an apple.

Share the sentence with each other.
Repeat the same for all 10 objects.
Help the student wherever required.

Activity 3 - New out of two (15 mins)

This activity develops the student's creativity and associative thinking skills.

Instructions:

Tell any two existing items to the student.

Example:

Item 1: T-shirt

Item 2: Rope

Ask the student:

Create a new product by combining those two items.
Example: A backpack
Share your new product idea in detail.
Example: My idea is a backpack made from recycled t-shirts for the bag and rope for the straps.
Who would be the target customers for your new product?
Example: The target customers are eco-aware students or travellers looking for a unique backpack.
Explain a marketing strategy for your product.
Example: I will explain the creativity, individuality, and sustainability of these upcycled backpacks.

Repeat the same with other sets of items.

Sample sets are given below for your reference.

Play as many rounds as possible till the time permits.

Help the student wherever required.
