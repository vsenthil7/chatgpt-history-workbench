This activity makes you and the student comfortable with each other.

Instructions:

Tell a theme to the student.
Example: Garden.
Ask the student to say five related words based on the theme.
Example: Bird, Flower, Child, Swing, Tree.
Switch roles, and play as many rounds as possible till time permits.

Activity 2 - Ad Zap (20 mins)

This activity helps the student generate new ideas.

Instructions:

Share the given products with the student.
Products: Pencil, pen, toothpaste, shampoo, Smartphone.
Ask the student:

Choose one product.

Example: Smartphone.

Give a creative quote for the product.

Example: Smart people will choose Smartphone!

Come up with a creative advertisement to sell that product.

Example: A creative way would be to highlight key features:

Camera: It has a crystal clear view and night vision.
Battery: Say goodbye to constant charging. Our smartphone's battery lasts all day.
Discover new ways to express yourself, whether it's through photography, music, or social media.
With our smartphones, the possibilities are endless.
Give the student 5 minutes to create an advertisement.
Once the time is up, ask the student to explain the advertisement to you.
Play as many rounds as possible till the time permits.
Help the student wherever required.

This activity encourages the student to think creatively and generate different ideas.

Piggy bank

Decorative light
Whoever completes first gets a point.
In the end, the person with the most points wins.
Sample objects are given for your reference.
Play as many rounds as possible till the time permits.

## 🎯 Session Objective (tell yourself, not the student)
Help the student:
- Generate ideas **quickly**
- Express ideas **confidently**
- Understand that **there is no “wrong” idea**

---

# 🧩 Activity 1 – Icebreaker: Theme Card (5 mins)

### How to start (exact words you can use)
> “Let’s warm up. I’ll say a theme. You tell me **any 5 words** related to it. No thinking too much.”

### Round 1
- **Theme:** Garden  
- If student struggles, gently prompt:
  - “It can be anything you see, hear, or feel.”

### Switch Roles
- Let **student choose the theme**
- You answer fast → this models speed & confidence

### If student freezes
Give **one starter word**, then ask them to continue.

✔️ **Skill unlocked:** Association + speed  
✔️ **Don’t correct answers. Ever.**

---

# 🧩 Activity 2 – Ad Zap (20 mins)

### Setup (important framing)
> “This is not about English or correctness.  
It’s about **selling an idea creatively**.”

### Step 1: Choose a Product
Give options clearly:
- Pencil  
- Pen  
- Toothpaste  
- Shampoo  
- Smartphone  

Let **student choose** (control matters).

---

### Step 2: Creative Quote (2–3 mins)

Ask:
> “Give one catchy line to sell this product.”

If stuck, give **formats**, not answers:
- “For people who…”
- “Because you deserve…”
- “One small thing that changes…”

✔️ Accept even simple lines.

---

### Step 3: Advertisement Creation (5 mins)
Give the timer clearly:
> “You have 5 minutes. Imagine you’re convincing a friend to buy this.”

Allow:
- Talking
- Bullet points
- Story style

---

### Step 4: Student Explains (crucial)
Ask:
- “Why did you choose this idea?”
- “Who will like this product most?”

✔️ **Do NOT improve their ad**
✔️ Only ask **clarifying questions**

---

### Repeat if time allows
Change product → faster rounds.

✔️ **Skill unlocked:** Ideation + persuasion + articulation

---

# 🧩 Activity 3 – Creative Uses of an Object (15 mins)

### Rules (state clearly)
- One object
- Both of you think of **5 uses**
- Fastest finisher → 1 point

### Start with:
**Object:** Plastic water bottle

Let them go first once.

---

### Sample Objects (rotate quickly)
- Pillow  
- Bucket  
- Shirt  
- Shoe  
- Notebook  

### If student repeats common ideas
Say:
> “Good. Now give me one **crazy or unusual** use.”

✔️ Encourage weirdness  
✔️ Laugh *with* ideas, never *at* them

---

# 🧠 Learning of the Day (5 mins)

Ask **only this**:
> “What is one thing you learned today?”

If silence:
Give choices:
- “Thinking faster?”
- “Selling ideas?”
- “Using objects differently?”

Let them **choose and speak**.

✔️ This locks learning internally.
