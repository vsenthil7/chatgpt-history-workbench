Activity 1 - Interview Questions Practise 2 (45 mins)

Instructions:

Share the given questions with the students one at a time.

Ask the students to share their answers.

After all students have answered, analyse and discuss all answers using the following 4 steps:

Step 1: Strengths

Explanation: Identify the strengths across all answers.
Example: "Everyone provided clear answers and used examples to support their points."

Step 2: Areas for Improvement

Explanation: Identify areas that can be improved for better clarity.
Example: "Many answers could benefit from speaking with more energy or adding more detail to clarify points."

Step 3: Tips

Explanation: Provide general advice that applies to everyone on how to frame answers better.
Example: "When answering, try to organise your thoughts before speaking to ensure clarity and confidence."

Step 4: Group Practise

Explanation: Ask all students to re-answer the question using the tips provided.
Example: "Now, all of you try answering again with a bit more structure in your response."

Keep a friendly and encouraging tone.

Help the students wherever required.
Questions:

<aside> 1️⃣

What are your Short-term and Long-term goals?

Ask the students: Share their answers.

Analyse and discuss the students’ answers:

Step 1: What was good?

Share the identified strengths in their answers.

Step 2: What can be improved?

Share the areas that need improvement in their answers.

Step 3: Tips

Share specific advice on how to frame answers better.

Short-term (1-2 years): Focus on learning
Show you want to master the job and grow your skills.
"Learn the job well and gain practical experience in my field."
Long-term (5+ years): Show growth ambition
Express interest for more responsibility and leadership.
"Become a team leader and help guide junior colleagues."
Connect goals to the job you are applying for
Show how this specific job helps you achieve your goals.
"This position will help me develop the skills I need for my career."

Step 4: Improved Answer

Ask each student to share their answer.

Examples:

My short-term goal is to learn this job well and gain practical experience in the next 2 years. I want to understand all aspects of the role and contribute effectively to the team. My long-term goal is to become a team leader in 5-7 years and help train new employees.
In the short term, I want to master the skills required for this role and become a reliable team member within the first year. My long-term goal is to specialise in this field and take on bigger projects that help the company grow. I chose this company because the learning opportunities here will help me achieve both my short-term and long-term career objectives. </aside>

<aside> 2️⃣

Are you an organised person?

Ask the students: Share their answers.

Analyse and discuss the students’ answers:

Step 1: What was good?

Share the identified strengths in their answers.

Step 2: What can be improved?

Share the areas that need improvement in their answers.

Step 3: Tips

Share specific advice on how to frame answers better.

Give an honest answer with a specific example
If yes: Show proof with a real example from your life.
"Yes, I keep my study materials organised and create a to-do list daily."
Show how the organisation helps you
Explain the benefits of being organised.
"Being organised helps me meet deadlines and reduces my stress."
If you are improving, show progress
Be honest if you are working on it, but show effort.
"I am working on it - I am trying to use a to-do list daily and keep my room clean."

Step 4: Improved Answer

Ask each student to share their answer.

Examples:

Yes, I consider myself an organised person. I keep my study materials arranged by subject and use a daily planner to track my assignments and deadlines. This helps me complete my work on time and reduces stress. My friends often borrow my notes because they are neat and well-organised.
I am working on becoming more organised. I used to be messy, but now I use a to-do list for daily tasks and keep my study space clean. I have started planning my week in advance, which has helped me manage my time better and submit assignments before deadlines. </aside>

<aside> 3️⃣

What is one value you think is important in a workplace?

Ask the students: Share their answers.

Analyse and discuss the students’ answers:

Step 1: What was good?

Share the identified strengths in their answers.

Step 2: What can be improved?

Share the areas that need improvement in their answers.

Step 3: Tips

Share specific advice on how to frame answers better.

Keep it simple and practical
Do not use complicated words - be straightforward.
"I believe in doing my best work and being honest in everything I do."
Mention learning and improvement
Show you see work as a way to grow.
"I think every task teaches me something new and helps me improve."
Show you care about teamwork and quality
Express that you want to help others and do good work.
"I believe in helping my team succeed and completing work properly."

Step 4: Improved Answer

Ask each student to share their answer.

Examples:

I think that hard work and dedication lead to success. I approach every task with enthusiasm and try to complete it well.
I believe in giving my best effort in every task and being honest in all my work. For me, work is not just about earning money, but also about learning new things and growing as a person. </aside>

<aside> 4️⃣

What kind of work environment do you expect?

Ask the students: Share their answers.

Analyse and discuss the students’ answers:

Step 1: What was good?

Share the identified strengths in their answers.

Step 2: What can be improved?

Share the areas that need improvement in their answers.

Step 3: Tips

Share specific advice on how to frame answers better.

Focus on learning and growth
Show you want to improve your skills and knowledge.
"I want an environment where I can learn new skills."
Express interest to contribute
Show you want to add value to the company, not just receive.
"I want to work where I can contribute to the team's success."

I am looking for a workplace where I can add value to the company through my work and dedication. I am flexible and can work well in any professional setting. </aside>

### **Common Pattern for ALL Questions (Do this every time)**

1. **Ask the question**  
2. **Let all students answer** (no interruption)  
3. **Group Analysis (4 steps)**  
4. **Re-answer (Improved version)**

---

## **Q1️⃣ Short-term & Long-term Goals**

### What to listen for (Mentor lens)
- Short-term = learning, skill building  
- Long-term = growth, responsibility  
- Connection to job

### Say this in discussion:
**Step 1 – Strengths**  
> “Many of you clearly spoke about learning and growing—that’s good.”

**Step 2 – Improve**  
> “Some answers were too general or missed linking goals to this job.”

**Step 3 – Tips (Tell clearly)**  
- Short-term (1–2 yrs): learning & experience  
- Long-term (5+ yrs): growth & leadership  
- Always connect to the role/company

**Step 4 – Re-answer**  
> “Now try again using this structure.”

---

## **Q2️⃣ Are you an organised person?**

### What to listen for
- Honest answer  
- Real example  
- Benefit of being organised

### Mentor prompts:
**Strengths**  
> “Good that you gave real-life examples.”

**Improve**  
> “Some answers missed explaining *how* it helps.”

**Tips**  
- Give one clear example  
- Show benefit (time, stress, deadlines)  
- If improving → show effort

**Re-answer cue**  
> “Answer again with one example and one benefit.”

---

## **Q3️⃣ One important workplace value**

### What to listen for
- Simple language  
- Practical value  
- Learning / teamwork

### Mentor framing:
**Strengths**  
> “Your values were genuine and clear.”

**Improve**  
> “Avoid big words—keep it simple.”

**Tips**  
- Honesty, effort, learning, teamwork  
- Work = growth, not just money

**Re-answer**  
> “Say it simply, like you’re talking to a senior.”

---

## **Q4️⃣ Expected work environment**

### What to listen for
- Learning mindset  
- Flexibility  
- Contribution

### Mentor guidance:
**Strengths**  
> “Good focus on learning and growth.”

**Improve**  
> “Avoid demanding specific facilities.”

**Tips**  
- Focus on skills + contribution  
- Show adaptability  
- Any professional environment is fine

**Re-answer**  
> “Answer as someone ready to work anywhere.”

---

## **Q5️⃣ Do you have any questions for us?**

### What to listen for
- Curiosity  
- Learning mindset  
- Job interest

### Mentor cues:
**Strengths**  
> “Nice that some of you asked about the role.”

**Improve**  
> “Everyone should always have at least one question.”

**Tips**  
- Ask about role, learning, expectations  
- Avoid salary/leave questions now

**Re-answer**  
> “Each of you ask one smart interview question.”
