## 🧩 S01–S04 | Mock Interviews 1 & 2 — Facilitator Playbook

### ⏱️ Session Format (Both Sessions)
- **Mode:** 1:1 mock interview
- **Time per student:** ~20 minutes
- **Guidance:**  
  - ❌ No hinting  
  - ❌ No sample answers shared  
  - ✅ Let student think & answer fully  
  - ✅ Observe, don’t interrupt  

---

## 🎯 Core Evaluation Framework (Use for ALL Questions)

Rate **each answer mentally**, then decide overall score:

1. **Listening Skills** – understood the question?
2. **Communication** – clarity, pace, language
3. **Response Organisation** – structured vs scattered
4. **Answer Quality** – relevance, examples
5. **Confidence** – voice, hesitation, ownership

➡️ Final **Performance Rating (1–5)** entered after session.

---

## 🧠 Mock Interview 1 — What You Are Testing

**Focus:** Basics + self-awareness

Key signals to observe:
- Can the student **introduce themselves clearly**?
- Do they give **generic traits** or **specific examples**?
- Are goals realistic and aligned?
- Do they freeze or ramble?

⚠️ Common issues in MI-1:
- Memorised answers
- No examples
- Low confidence
- Weak structure (“I am… I did… I like…” with no flow)

---

## 🔁 Mock Interview 2 — What You Are Testing

**Focus:** Improvement + maturity

Key signals to observe:
- Better **structure** than MI-1?
- Use of **examples / stories**?
- Can they explain a **project or skill** clearly?
- Are answers more **professional and calm**?

⚠️ Red flags if still present:
- Same generic answers as MI-1
- No growth in confidence
- Still avoiding examples

---

## 🧪 MI-1 vs MI-2 — How to Compare (Mentally)

Ask yourself:

- Did answers become **clearer**?
- Did the student move from **claims → examples**?
- Was there visible **confidence growth**?
- Did hesitation reduce?

Even **small improvement = success**.

---

## 🗣️ Feedback to Student — Live Structure (Use This Flow)

You should always cover **4 parts**, in this order:

### 1️⃣ What Improved
> “In your second interview, your answers were clearer and better structured. You also started giving examples, which was missing earlier.”

### 2️⃣ Strengths
> “Your listening skills are good, and you stayed calm even when questions were challenging.”

### 3️⃣ Growth Area
> “Try to add one real example whenever you mention a skill. That will make your answers stronger.”

### 4️⃣ Encourage
> “You’ve definitely improved today. Keep practising like this, and you’ll be much more confident in real interviews.”

Then ask:
> “How did *you* feel between the first and second interview?”

---

## 📝 Homework — Why It Matters (Say This Clearly)

Explain **purpose**, not just steps:

- Repetition builds **clarity**
- Speaking aloud builds **confidence**
- Structure becomes natural with practice

Homework logic:
1. List questions
2. Prepare answers
3. Practise **multiple times**

⚠️ Tell them clearly:
> “Tomorrow is not memorisation. It’s about clarity.”

---

## 📌 After-Session Checklist (Do Not Skip)

- ✅ Student marked attendance
- ✅ You marked attendance
- ✅ Performance rating entered
- ✅ Student knows next homework clearly
