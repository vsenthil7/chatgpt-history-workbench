## 🎯 Overall Structure (Big Picture)

- **Total Sessions:** 5  
- **Modes Used:**  
  - Group Mentoring  
  - 1-to-1 Mentoring  
- **Primary Goal:**  
  Prepare students for interviews **and** help them articulate a realistic career roadmap.

## 🔹 Pre-Step (Mandatory if first time)

### Group Introduction (Before Session 1)
**Mode:** Group Mentoring  
**When:** Only if students are new  

**What to do**
- Introduce yourself using the **Group Introduction page**
- Help students introduce themselves
- Skip this entirely if already done earlier

⏱️ *Estimated time:* 15–20 mins (one-time)

---

## 🔹 Core Curriculum (5 Sessions)

### 🧩 Sessions 1–4: Mock Interview Cycle

This is a **looped design**:  
Interview → Practice → Practice → Interview

---

### **Session 1 – Mock Interview 1**
**Mode:** 1-to-1 Mentoring  

**What you do**
- Conduct individual mock interviews  
- **~20 mins per student**
- Finish *all* students before moving to Session 2

⏱️ *Time math example:*  
- 5 students → ~100 mins total  
- 10 students → ~200 mins total  

---

### **Session 2 – Interview Questions Practice 1**
**Mode:** Group Mentoring  

**What you do**
- Teach common interview questions
- Help students frame answers
- Encourage speaking, not memorising

⏱️ *Estimated time:* 30–45 mins

---

### **Session 3 – Interview Questions Practice 2**
**Mode:** Group Mentoring  

**What you do**
- Deeper practice
- Improve clarity, confidence, structure
- Correct common mistakes noticed in Session 1

⏱️ *Estimated time:* 30–45 mins

---

### **Session 4 – Mock Interview 2**
**Mode:** 1-to-1 Mentoring  

**What you do**
- Second individual mock interview
- Compare with Session 1 performance
- Give targeted feedback

⏱️ *Same time load as Session 1*

---

## 🔹 Final Session

### **Session 5 – Career Roadmap Presentation**
**Mode:** Group Mentoring  

**What you do**
- Students present their **Career Roadmaps**
- These are already created in the Job Readiness program
- Your role:
  - Motivate
  - Clarify
  - Ground expectations (not judge)

⏱️ *Estimated time:* 45–60 mins

---

## 📌 Operating Rules (From Instructions Page)

### ✅ Must Do
- Prepare before every session
- Speak in **regional language**
- Mark attendance after call
- Follow the Mock Interview handbook
- Stay punctual & noise-free

### ❌ Must Not Do
- Don’t skip sessions
- Don’t promise money, gifts, or support

### 🎤 During Activities
- Encourage everyone to speak
- Keep it live, friendly, non-threatening
- Help students when they get stuck

This is **not 5 random calls**.

- Session 1 → baseline
- Sessions 2–3 → skill correction
- Session 4 → measurable improvement
- Session 5 → future articulation
