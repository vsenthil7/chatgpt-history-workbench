## 📸 2️⃣ HOW TO TAKE PHOTOS (so OCR + Inventory works)

This is **critical**. Most shops fail here.

### ✅ Best Practice (Low Cost, High Accuracy)

#### Option A — **Shelf + Tag Split Method (RECOMMENDED)**
Take **2 photos per shelf section**:

1️⃣ **Product Photo**
- Straight-on
- Crop to **ONLY product packs**
- No price tags
- No glare (step back 1.5m)
- Use same angle every time

2️⃣ **Price Tag Photo**
- Zoom ONLY price labels
- Keep camera parallel to shelf
- Capture barcode if visible

➡️ Link using `Image_Ref`  
➡️ Price becomes replaceable without touching catalogue

---

#### Option B — **Single SKU Close-up**
For new products / low volume:
- Hold product
- White background
- One image front
- One image back (ingredients + barcode)

Used when onboarding new SKUs.

---

#### Option C — **Video Sweep (Advanced)**
- Slow video sweep of shelf
- Extract frames later
- Used by Amazon Go style systems
- Overkill for phase-1, but future-proof

---
