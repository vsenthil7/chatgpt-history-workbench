Question 1: Tell me about yourself.

Sample Answer: Hi, I am Divya. I am from Chennai. I completed my B.Sc. Mathematics from Hindu College. I completed my twelfth grade from Sarada Matriculation School with 89%. Question 2: What would you consider your greatest strength and weakness?

Sample Answer: My strength is my analytical and planning skills, developed over the years, help me to complete my work before the deadline. I am a little bit nervous while speaking in a group, but I have given a good number of paper presentations to overcome this.

Question 3: What are your short-term and long-term goals?

Sample Answer:

Short-term goal: My short-term goal is to learn new software (mention software that is useful to know for this position).

Long-term goal: My long-term goal is to grow with the company and to improve the company’s sales.

Question 4: What are the skills you have apart from your subject?

Sample Answer: I am a good singer. I have participated in many singing competitions and have won prizes.

Question 5: Did you go for any Internship? What skills have you learned from there?

Sample Answer: No, I have not done any internship before. However, I have studied (state the subject related to the field of work) in college and am confident that I can apply this theoretical knowledge to the job and learn quickly.

Question 6: Why should we hire a fresher like you?

Sample Answer: I have great communication skills and am very detail-oriented, which I believe are important qualities for this position.

Question 7: Are you an organised person?

Sample Answer: I consider myself to be a very organised person. I always keep checklists and cross off items as I finish them. Staying organised helps me stay focused and complete my homework before deadlines.

Question 8: What is one value you think is important in a workplace?

Sample Answer: One value I think is important in a workplace is curiosity. Being curious helps me stay engaged and constantly seek opportunities to learn and grow, which contributes to both personal and team success.

Question 9: What kind of work environment do you expect?

Sample Answer: I prefer a fast-paced working environment, but I am open to new experiences as they may help me to develop vital skills.

Question 10: Is there anything you would like to ask?

Sample Answer: Yes, I have a question. What would a typical day look like in this role?

📍Mock Interview Questions

Question 1: Can you introduce yourself?

Sample Answer: Hi, I am Divya. I am from Chennai. I completed my B.Sc. Mathematics from Hindu College. I completed my twelfth grade from Sarada Matriculation School with 89%. I love playing chess and have represented my school in various district and state-level chess competitions. I am also a part of an NGO where I did many volunteering services to help the underprivileged and visited a number of old-age homes. I have leadership skills as I was the class representative in my college for 2 years.

Question 2: What would you say are your best qualities?

Sample Answer: I am hardworking, organised, and calm under pressure. I often help my friends during exam time with group study sessions.

Question 3: Tell me about a new skill you have developed recently.

Sample Answer: I learned Canva by watching YouTube tutorials and practising on my own. I used it to design posters for my college events.

Question 4: What drives you to do your best work?

Sample Answer: I feel motivated when I see results from my efforts. It gives me a sense of achievement and encourages me to keep improving.

Question 5: Tell me about a difficult situation you handled successfully.

Sample Answer: During a group project, two teammates had a conflict and stopped communicating. I spoke to them separately to understand both sides, then brought them together to discuss the issue calmly. I encouraged them to focus on our common goal and divide the tasks equally. As a result, we resolved the problem quickly and completed the project on time.

Question 6: What interests you about joining our organisation?

Sample Answer: I admire your focus on innovation and learning. I want to grow by learning from experienced professionals in your company, and also contribute by applying my skills to solve real problems. I can also contribute by taking on more responsibilities and helping the organisation grow.

Question 7: Can you explain a specific project from your resume?

Sample Answer: In my final year, I worked on a “Smart Attendance System” mini-project using Python. I was responsible for coding the face recognition module. We tested it with 30 students, and it worked with over 90% accuracy. I learned how to work in a team and meet deadlines during this project.

Question 8: Where do you see yourself five years from now?

Sample Answer: In the next five years, I want to become an expert in my field and take up a leadership position. I plan to gain hands-on experience, attend training programs, and continuously upgrade my skills. I also hope to contribute to the success of the organisation by taking on bigger responsibilities.

Question 9: What type of workplace setting would you prefer?

Sample Answer: I prefer a friendly and supportive workplace where I can learn from others, share ideas, and receive feedback. At the same time, I value a space where I can focus and work independently when needed. I am open to adapting based on the team and company culture.

Question 10: Do you want to ask any questions?

Sample Answer: Yes, I would like to know more about the training provided to new employees.
