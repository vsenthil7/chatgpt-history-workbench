## Short answer (important)

**No — Pro (£200/month) does NOT mean “infinite permanent memory.”**  
It means **larger context windows, higher limits, and better continuity**, but **memory is still bounded, curated, and governed**.

Think of it as **“much bigger working memory,” not “full brain dump storage.”**

---

## What actually changes between Plus (£20) and Pro (£200)

### 🧠 1. Context vs Memory (this is the key confusion)

There are **two different things** people mix up:

### A) Context window (what the model can “hold” right now)
- How much conversation, files, and instructions can be **actively reasoned over**
- This **does increase** with Pro
- Pro helps with:
  - Very long technical chats
  - Multi-page specs
  - Large codebases
  - Fewer “I forgot earlier” moments **within a project**

👉 This is where Pro **really helps you**, especially given how you work.

---

### B) Long-term memory (what is saved across chats)
- This is **not unlimited** on any plan
- Memory is:
  - Selective
  - User-controlled
  - Summarized
  - Pruned over time
- Even on Pro:
  - Not everything is stored
  - Not everything is recalled
  - Not raw chat logs

👉 Pro does **not** mean “store my entire life or all chats forever.”

---

## What “Keep full context with maximum memory” actually means

Translated into plain language:

> “We give you the **largest available working context**, better session continuity, and improved recall of *important, saved facts* — not unlimited raw history.”

So:
- ✔ Better continuity in **big projects**
- ✔ Fewer mid-project amnesia issues
- ✖ Not a searchable archive of all past chats
- ✖ Not a personal S3 / Google Drive for chats
- ✖ Not guaranteed recall of every detail unless structured or saved
