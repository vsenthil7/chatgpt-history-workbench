### Ingredients (1–2 servings)
- Rava / sooji – 1 cup

- Ghee – 1½ to 2 tbsp (don’t be shy)

### Method (10–12 mins total)

**1️⃣ Dry roast rava**  
- Heat pan on **medium–low**  
- Add rava, roast till light aroma (no colour)  
- Transfer out, keep aside

**2️⃣ Tempering**  
- Same pan → add ghee  
- Add mustard seeds → let them splutter  
- Add red chilli  
- Add onion + salt (salt helps onion sweat)  
- Sauté till onion turns **soft & glossy** (not brown)

**3️⃣ Boil water**  
- Add water to the pan  
- Let it come to a **rolling boil**  
- Taste → adjust salt now (important)

**4️⃣ Add rava**  
- Lower flame  
- Add rava **slowly**, stirring continuously  
- No lumps — keep stirring

**5️⃣ Finish**  
- Cover, cook 2–3 mins on low  
- Switch off  
- Add **½ tbsp ghee on top**, rest 2 mins  
- Fluff gently

## 2️⃣ Why samiya upma *breaks* (turns mushy or snaps)

### ❌ Common reasons
1. **Too much water at once**  
   → gluten weakens → strands snap

2. **High flame after adding samiya**  
   → outside cooks, inside raw → breakage

3. **Over-stirring**  
   → mechanical break (this is BIG)

4. **Acid or spice powders early**  
   (chilli powder, turmeric, tomato)  
   → weakens wheat structure

5. **Old / low-quality samiya**  
   → already brittle

### ✅ Fix rules
- Add samiya only to **rolling boil**
- Stir **once**, then cover
- Always cook on **low flame**
- Never add powders

---

## 3️⃣ Hotel-style softness trick (this is the secret)

### 🔥 The double-steam method
1. Cook samiya normally  
2. Switch off when **just cooked** (not fully dry)
3. Add **½ tbsp ghee**
4. **Cover tightly**
5. Rest **5 full minutes** (no opening)

👉 Steam finishes cooking  
👉 Ghee coats strands  
👉 Result = **soft, fluffy, non-sticky**

### Bonus micro-trick (hotels use this):
- Add **1 tsp ghee to boiling water** *before* samiya  
  → prevents sticking + silkiness

# 🔬 1️⃣ Glycemic Index (GI) — Deeper Explanation

## What GI Measures
GI measures how fast a carbohydrate raises blood glucose compared to pure glucose (GI = 100).

### 🍚 White Rice (Lemon Rice Base)
- GI: ~70–75 (medium–high)
- Rapid starch digestion
- High insulin response
- Faster energy spike → faster drop

### 🌾 Wheat Semiya (Lemon Samiya Base)
- GI: ~55–65 (medium)
- Contains more protein + slight fibre
- Slower glucose release
- More stable energy curve

---

## Why Rice Spikes Faster

Rice starch = mostly **amylopectin**  
→ Highly branched  
→ Breaks down quickly  

Wheat semolina = more **amylose + protein matrix**  
→ Slower enzymatic breakdown

## What Reduces GI in Your Recipe

✔ Ghee (fat slows gastric emptying)  
✔ Peanuts / dal (protein + fat)  
✔ Cooling + reheating (retrogradation lowers GI slightly)  
✔ Lemon (acid slows digestion slightly)
