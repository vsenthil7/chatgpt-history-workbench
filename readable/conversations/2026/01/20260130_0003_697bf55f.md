## ✅ Option 1: Sign using **Adobe Acrobat Reader (Free – PC/Laptop)**
Best if you’re on Windows or Mac.

**Steps**
1. Open the PDF in **Adobe Acrobat Reader**
2. Click **Tools → Fill & Sign**
3. Click **Sign yourself → Add signature**
4. Choose one:
   - **Type** your name
   - **Draw** with mouse/touchpad
   - **Upload image** of your handwritten signature
5. Place the signature in the required box
6. **Save** the PDF

👉 This is legally accepted for most school/bank forms.

---

## ✅ Option 2: Sign using **Mobile (Very easy)**
Best if you already have the PDF on your phone.

### On Android / iPhone
1. Open the PDF in:
   - **Adobe Acrobat Reader app** (recommended)
   - OR **Files / Google Drive**
2. Tap **Fill & Sign** or **Markup**
3. Add signature (draw once → reuse later)
4. Save & share

---

## ✅ Option 3: Sign using **Online (No install)**
Fastest if you don’t want apps.

Use any ONE:
- Smallpdf
- iLovePDF
- DocHub

**Steps**
1. Upload PDF
2. Choose **Sign PDF**
3. Draw / type signature
4. Download signed PDF

⚠️ Avoid this if the document is very sensitive (passport/bank heavy docs).

## ✅ BEST METHOD (Recommended): Adobe Acrobat Reader DC (Free)

### Step 1: Install (only once)
1. Go to: https://get.adobe.com/reader/
2. **Untick** any optional offers (McAfee etc.)
3. Click **Download Acrobat Reader**
4. Install → finish

⏱️ Time: **2–3 minutes**

---

### Step 2: Sign the PDF
1. Right-click the PDF → **Open with → Adobe Acrobat Reader**
2. Click **Tools** (top left)
3. Select **Fill & Sign**
4. Click **Sign yourself → Add signature**
5. Choose:
   - **Draw** (best, looks handwritten)
   - OR **Image** (upload photo of signature)
6. Click where the signature is required
7. Adjust size → place neatly

---

### Step 3: Save properly
- Press **Ctrl + S**
- File name suggestion:  
  `Signed_IFRD_Form_<StudentName>.pdf`

---
