Activity 1 - Interview Questions Practise 1 (45 mins)

Instructions:

Share the given questions with the students one at a time.

Ask the students to share their answers.

After all students have answered, analyse and discuss all answers using the following 4 steps:

Step 1: Strengths

Explanation: Identify the strengths across all answers.
Example: "Everyone provided clear answers and used examples to support their points."

Step 2: Areas for Improvement

Explanation: Identify areas that can be improved for better clarity.
Example: "Many answers could benefit from speaking with more energy or adding more detail to clarify points."

Step 3: Tips

Explanation: Provide general advice that applies to everyone on how to frame answers better.
Example: "When answering, try to organise your thoughts before speaking to ensure clarity and confidence."

Step 4: Group Practise

Explanation: Ask all students to re-answer the question using the tips provided.
Example: "Now, all of you try answering again with a bit more structure in your response."

Keep a friendly and encouraging tone.

Help the students wherever required.
Questions:

<aside> 1️⃣

Tell me about yourself.

Ask the students: Share their answers.

Analyse and discuss the students’ answers:

Step 1: What was good?

Share the identified strengths in their answers.

Step 2: What can be improved?

Share the areas that need improvement in their answers.

Step 3: Tips

Share specific advice on how to frame answers better.

Examples:

Follow a simple structure: Present, Past, Future
Present: Who you are now (name, course, college).
Past: One relevant skill or achievement you have.
Future: Your career interest or what you want to do after graduation.
Keep it short and focused
Talk for only 30-60 seconds.
Cover only the most important points about yourself.
Sound natural, not memorised
Do not sound like you are reading from a script.
Use a conversational tone, not robotic speech.

Step 4: Improved answer

Ask each student to share their answer.

Examples:

I am Kavya from Chennai. I am currently pursuing my final year B.Tech in Computer Science. I love coding and have worked on a few small projects. I am a quick learner and enjoy solving problems.
Hello, I am Meera. I am studying B.Com in my second year. I enjoy reading and volunteering at NGOs. I am good at working with people and learning new things. </aside>

Step 1: What was good?

Step 1: What was good?

Give one strength + one weakness only

# 🧠 Activity 1 – Interview Questions Practise (45 mins)

### Structure you repeat for *every* question:
1️⃣ Students answer  
2️⃣ What was good  
3️⃣ What can improve  
4️⃣ Tips  
5️⃣ Re-answer

---

## Question 1️⃣ Tell me about yourself (8–9 mins)

### Ask:
> “Who would like to start? Speak for about **30–60 seconds**.”

### Step 1 – Strengths (say aloud):
- “Most of you spoke clearly”
- “Good confidence”
- “You shared your name and course well”

### Step 2 – Improve:
- Answers were too long / mixed
- No clear structure
- Some sounded memorised

### Step 3 – Tips (teach once):
**Present – Past – Future**
- Present: name + course
- Past: one skill / achievement
- Future: career interest

### Step 4 – Re-answer:
> “Now try again using this structure.”

---

## Question 2️⃣ Skills apart from subject (8–9 mins)

### Ask:
> “Tell us **2 or 3 skills**, not more.”

### Step 1 – Strengths:
- Students identified useful skills
- Some gave real examples

### Step 2 – Improve:
- Listing skills without proof
- Too many skills at once

### Step 3 – Tips:
- Pick **2–3 job-useful skills**
- Give **one example per skill**
- Mention **one skill you are learning now**

### Step 4 – Re-answer:
> “Now answer again with one example for each skill.”

---

## Question 3️⃣ Strengths & Weaknesses (8–9 mins)

### Ask:
> “One strength and one weakness only.”

### Step 1 – Strengths:
- Honest answers
- Good self-awareness

### Step 2 – Improve:
- Too many strengths
- Weakness without improvement plan

### Step 3 – Tips:
- Strength → prove with example  
- Weakness → always say **how you are improving**

### Step 4 – Re-answer:
> “Now answer again using this format.”

---

## Question 4️⃣ Internship / Experience (8–9 mins)

### Ask:
> “If no internship, that’s okay — share *any* experience.”

### Step 1 – Strengths:
- Students shared real experiences
- Good learning points

### Step 2 – Improve:
- Talking about tasks, not skills
- Ending without future learning goal

### Step 3 – Tips:
- Focus on **skills learned**
- If no internship → college projects / family business / volunteering
- End with **what you want to learn next**

### Step 4 – Re-answer:
> “Now answer again focusing only on skills.”

---

## Question 5️⃣ Why should we hire you? (8–9 mins)

### Ask:
> “Think from the *company’s side*.”

### Step 1 – Strengths:
- Confidence
- Positive attitude

### Step 2 – Improve:
- Talking about personal needs
- Generic words without proof

### Step 3 – Tips:
- What **you give**, not what you get
- One quality + one example
- Show long-term interest

### Step 4 – Re-answer:
> “Now answer again as if you are convincing the interviewer.”

---

# 📊 Activity 2 – Homework (5 mins)

### Explain clearly:
> “Today’s homework is **practice**, not writing.”

### Homework steps:
1. List today’s questions  
2. Prepare answers using tips  
3. Practise speaking aloud  

### Close with:
> “Tomorrow we’ll practise another set, so come prepared 👍”

- “I’ll also mark mine”
