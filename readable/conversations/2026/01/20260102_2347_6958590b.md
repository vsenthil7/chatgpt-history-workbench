---

## 2️⃣ Delete multiple directories

```powershell
Remove-Item "C:\Path\To\Folder" -Recurse -Force -WhatIf
```

---

```powershell

if (Test-Path $path) {
```

- ❌ Be extra careful with `C:\`, `C:\Users`, `C:\Windows`
