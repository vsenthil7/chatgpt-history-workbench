## What the error actually means (plain English)

> **“If an open app is using WASAPI exclusive mode, change it to shared mode.”**

On **Windows**, audio devices work in two modes:

### 1️⃣ WASAPI **Exclusive Mode**
- One app **locks** the audio device
- No other app can record or listen
- Common offenders:
  - Zoom / Google Meet / Teams
  - Browser tab playing video
  - DAW apps (Audacity, OBS, etc.)
  - Sometimes even Bluetooth drivers

### 2️⃣ WASAPI **Shared Mode**
- Multiple apps can access audio together
- This is what **Snipping Tool screen recording needs**

Right now, **some app has hijacked your speaker/mic exclusively**, so Snipping Tool cannot capture audio.

---

## Why this happens specifically with Snipping Tool

Windows Snipping Tool:
- **Does NOT force audio**
- It politely asks Windows: “Can I record system audio?”
- Windows replies: “Nope, another app locked it.”

So video records ✔  
Audio = ❌

---

## Fix it (2-minute guaranteed fix)

### ✅ Step 1: Release Exclusive Mode (MOST IMPORTANT)

1. **Right-click speaker icon** → **Sound settings**
2. Scroll → **More sound settings**
3. Go to **Playback** tab
4. Select your **active speaker** → **Properties**
5. Open **Advanced** tab
6. ❌ **UNCHECK** both:
   - “Allow applications to take exclusive control…”
   - “Give exclusive mode applications priority”
7. Click **Apply → OK**

Repeat the **same steps under the “Recording” tab** for your microphone.

📌 This alone fixes **90%** of cases.

---

### ✅ Step 2: Kill silent audio hogs (important)

Before recording:

- Close:
  - Zoom / Meet / Teams
  - Any tab playing YouTube
  - OBS / Audacity
- Restart browser if unsure

Windows is sneaky — background tabs can hold audio.

### ✅ Step 3: Reboot audio service (fast, optional)
