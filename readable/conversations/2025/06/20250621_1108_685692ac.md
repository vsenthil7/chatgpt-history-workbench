### What Are Wireframes?

- Wireframes are **visual blueprints** or sketches of an app or website’s layout, showing how screens and elements (buttons, menus, content areas) are arranged.
- They help designers and developers understand user flows and interface structure **before** detailed design or coding.

---

### Why Are Wireframes Sometimes Complex?

- When apps involve multiple screens, dynamic content, interactive elements, and user input, wireframes become more detailed and layered.
- Complex wireframes often include:  
  - Multiple states of the UI (e.g., loading, error, empty states)  
  - Interactive flows (menus, popups, transitions)  
  - Accessibility considerations (font size, screen reader compatibility)  
  - Localization (different languages affecting layout)  
- Creating them usually requires **specialized design tools** like Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, or even drawing tablets to create precise, navigable mockups.

---

### Why Can't I Generate Complex Wireframes Here?

- My environment supports **text, simple shapes, and some basic graphics**, but does **not support interactive or high-fidelity graphical UI design**.  
- I can create **simple schematic diagrams or text-based layouts** but not the detailed, pixel-perfect visual wireframes used in UI/UX design.
