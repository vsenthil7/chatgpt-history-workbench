### 3️⃣ **How does World Bank bring money?**

**The World Bank doesn’t give “grants” here — it gives loans (called "credits" or "soft loans") through its arm: International Development Association (IDA) or International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD).**

- **Mechanism:**
  - TN Govt applies for funding with a detailed project proposal.

  - Technically, Govt of India borrows from World Bank.
  - Govt of India passes the loan to Tamil Nadu as part of externally aided projects (EAPs).
