### 🌐 1. “Quantum” in **finance / trading / investment banking**

- The “quantum” here is more about *numbers and probability*, not about subatomic particles.  
- Example: A quant uses **Monte Carlo simulations, stochastic calculus, or AI algorithms** to model risk or optimize portfolios.  
So when companies say they want someone with *quantum* or *quant skills*, they actually mean **quantitative analytics**.

---

### ⚛️ 2. “Quantum” in **physics / technology**
This is the **scientific quantum** — the realm where matter and energy behave probabilistically.  
- Think: electrons, photons, quantum entanglement, superposition, etc.  
- Applications:  
  - **Quantum computing** — using qubits instead of bits to solve problems much faster (Google, IBM, D-Wave are leaders).  
  - **Quantum cryptography** — ultra-secure communication.  
  - **Quantum sensors** — for precision measurements (medical imaging, navigation).  
  - **Quantum materials** — for superconductors, energy storage, etc.  

So this is a **physics-born field** now merging with **AI, data science, and cryptography** — which is why trading companies sometimes keep an eye on it, because quantum computing could give them **massive speed advantages** in optimization or prediction.
