## **1. Finality & Exhaustion Canon**

### **1.1 Final Decision Definition**
A decision is **final** when **all applicable internal remedies and appeal paths** defined in **P16–P18** have been:
- Exhausted, or  
- Waived by the eligible party, or  
- Time-barred under canonical timelines.

Once final, a decision **may not be re-opened**, re-litigated, or indirectly challenged through alternative procedural routes.

---

### **1.2 Internal Finality**
Internal finality attaches when:
- A final appeal authority issues a determination, or  
- An appeal window lapses without filing, or  
- An appeal is dismissed as inadmissible or procedurally defective.

Internal finality **binds the system**, subject only to external override mechanisms in §6.

---

### **1.3 External Finality**
Where a matter proceeds to regulator, arbitrator, or court:
- Finality attaches upon issuance of a **non-appealable external order**, judgment, or settlement.
- The system shall **mirror the external outcome** without reinterpretation.

---

### **1.4 Prohibition on Circular Review**
No finalized matter may be:
- Re-framed as a new dispute on identical facts,  
- Re-opened through policy reinterpretation, or  
- Relitigated via alternate modules or teams.

---

## **2. Precedent Canon**

### **2.1 Precedent Classification**

All resolved matters are classified as one of the following:

**A. Binding Precedent**
- Issued by designated authority bodies
- Interprets canonical rules of general applicability
- Explicitly labeled as “Binding”

**B. Persuasive Precedent**
- Fact-specific determinations
- Inform internal consistency but do not bind future cases

**C. Non-Precedential Outcomes**
- Settlements without admission
- Procedural dismissals
- Discretionary or humanitarian remedies

---

### **2.2 Preconditions for Binding Status**
A precedent may be designated binding only if:
- The decision body has canonical authority
- Reasoning is explicitly articulated
- Scope and applicability are clearly delimited
- Non-retroactivity safeguards are affirmed

---

### **2.3 Rejection of De-Facto Precedent**
Repeated outcomes **do not** become precedent by frequency alone.  
Only **explicit designation** creates precedent.

---

## **3. System Memory & Institutional Record**

### **3.1 System Memory Definition**
System memory consists of:
- Final decisions
- Precedent classifications
- Enforcement actions
- External determinations
- Canon versions and amendments

---

### **3.2 Record Integrity**
All system memory must be:
- Tamper-evident
- Time-stamped
- Authority-attributed
- Retained under locked retention schedules

Silent deletion, rewriting, or backdating is prohibited.

---

### **3.3 Retention Standards**
Minimum retention periods:
- Canonical decisions: **Permanent**
- Binding precedent: **Permanent**
- Persuasive precedent: **Minimum 10 years**
- Non-precedential matters: **As defined in P16**

---

## **4. Canon Locking & Versioning**

### **4.1 Canon Locking**
Once a canonical rule set is published:
- It is **locked**
- It governs prospectively
- It may not be altered except under §5

---

### **4.2 Version Control**
All canonical amendments must:
- Create a new version identifier
- Preserve full prior versions
- Declare effective date
- Declare scope of change

No silent edits. No rolling changes.

---

## **5. Amendment & Exception Pathways**

### **5.1 Ordinary Amendments**
Amendments may occur only through:
- Explicit governance process
- Formal approval authority
- Published rationale
- Prospective application only

---

### **5.2 Exceptional Amendments**
Exceptional amendments (e.g., emergency or regulatory alignment):
- Must be narrowly scoped
- Must declare necessity
- Must not retroactively impair finalized outcomes unless compelled externally

---

## **6. Non-Retroactivity & Reliance Protection**

### **6.1 Non-Retroactivity Rule**
Canonical changes **shall not apply retroactively** to:
- Finalized decisions
- Completed enforcement actions
- Reliance-based user conduct

---

### **6.2 Reliance Protection**
Where parties reasonably relied on a prior canon:
- Their completed actions remain valid
- No penalty may be imposed solely due to later canon changes

---

## **7. Regulator & Court Overrides**

### **7.1 Supremacy Recognition**
Where binding external authority mandates deviation:
- The system shall comply
- The override shall be explicitly logged
- Canon impact shall be narrowly applied

---

### **7.2 Override Containment**
External overrides:
- Do not automatically rewrite canon
- Do not create internal precedent unless explicitly adopted

---

## **8. Closure & System Guarantees**

Upon closure of P19, VisaVerse guarantees:

- Decisions terminate decisively  
- History is preserved, not rewritten  
- Precedent is intentional, not accidental  
- Canon evolves without drift  
- Governance remains stable under time, pressure, and scrutiny
