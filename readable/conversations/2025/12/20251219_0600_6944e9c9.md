## What is **true** about bamboo 🌱

1. **Long underground preparation phase**
   - Many bamboo species spend **several years (3–5 years)** establishing an extensive **rhizome (root-like) network** underground.
   - During this phase, **very little is visible above ground**.
   - Energy is being stored and structural pathways are being built.

2. **Explosive above-ground growth once shoots emerge**
   - When a shoot finally emerges, bamboo can grow **extremely fast**:
     - Some species: **30–90 cm per day**
     - One of the fastest-growing plants on Earth.
   - This rapid growth is possible **because the underground system already exists**.

3. **Above-ground growth reflects underground strength**
   - The height and thickness of the bamboo culm are largely determined by
     how well-developed the rhizome system is.
   - So the idea that *“what you see later depends on what grew invisibly earlier”* is scientifically sound.

---

## What is **not literally true** ❌

1. **“First drop of water makes it suddenly shoot up”**
   - Bamboo does **not** suddenly grow because of one drop of water.
   - Growth depends on:
     - Seasonal conditions
     - Temperature
     - Soil moisture
     - Stored energy in rhizomes
   - The “first drop” is a **symbol**, not a biological trigger.

2. **“Nothing is happening during early years”**
   - A lot *is* happening—just underground.
   - Saying “nothing happens” is metaphorical, not literal.

---
