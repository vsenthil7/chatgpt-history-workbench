## 🧩 Template 001 — “One-Person Product Factory” (Prompt-Assisted Sheet)

**Working name:**  
**PT-001 – One-Person Product Factory (Prompt-Assisted Excel/Sheets Template)**  

**Goal:**  
A single Excel / Google Sheet where a user fills in **10–12 basic inputs** about their idea or niche…  
and the template *auto-generates*:

1. **Product concepts** (3–5 variants)  
2. **Positioning sentences & taglines** (formula + prompt blocks)  
3. **Content angles / lead magnets**  
4. **Ready-to-copy AI prompts** they paste into ChatGPT/GPTs to expand into full copy

This is a **meta-product**:  
- You sell **the sheet itself** as a template.  
- Buyers use ChatGPT with your pre-built prompts → they feel like they have a “mini product team” even if they’re solo.

---

## Structure of PT-001

We’ll keep it tight and robot-friendly.

### Sheet 1: `INPUT_CORE`

User fills only these fields:

- `A2: Niche / Domain`  (e.g. fitness coaches, Excel freelancers)  
- `B2: Target Persona`  (busy moms, senior managers, etc.)  
- `C2: Main Problem`  (what keeps them stuck)  
- `D2: Desired Outcome`  
- `E2: Product Type`  (notebook, sheet, mini-course, email pack, etc.)  
- `F2: Price Target` (low-ticket / mid-ticket / high-ticket, or a number)  
- `G2: Skill Assets` (what the buyer is good at)  
- `H2: Delivery Style` (text-only / video / templates / automations)  
- `I2: Tools they use` (Excel, Notion, Canva, etc.)  
- `J2: Time Commitment` (how long they can create/maintain)

Everything else should be **dropdowns / guided text** where possible, so they don’t overthink.

---

### Sheet 2: `PRODUCT_IDEAS`

Auto-generated using formulas + some pre-written patterns.

Columns:

- `A: Idea ID`
- `B: Product Name (Generated via CONCAT patterns)`
- `C: Short Hook (formula-based)`
- `D: Who It’s For`
- `E: Main Outcome`
- `F: Suggested Format`
- `G: AI Prompt To Refine` (ready text using cell references)

Example **Prompt cell** (for G2):

> “You are a product strategist. Using this context:  
> Niche: [INPUT_CORE!A2]  
> Target Persona: [INPUT_CORE!B2]  
> Main Problem: [INPUT_CORE!C2]  
> Desired Outcome: [INPUT_CORE!D2]  
> Product Type: [this row’s Product Name + Format],  
> generate 3 refined product variations with short descriptions.”

(We’ll wire this as a formula in the actual build.)

---

### Sheet 3: `SALES_SNIPPETS`

For the top 1–2 ideas:

- `A: Selected Product Name` (data validation from PRODUCT_IDEAS)  
- `B: One-Line Value Prop`  
- `C: 3-Bullet Benefits`  
- `D: Risk Reversal / Guarantee Angle`  
- `E: AI Prompt – Sales Page`  
- `F: AI Prompt – 10 Social Posts`  
- `G: AI Prompt – Launch Email`

Each prompt auto-fills from `INPUT_CORE` + selected product, so user just:

1. Copies the prompt text.  
2. Pastes into ChatGPT.  
3. Gets done-for-them copy.

---

### Sheet 4: `DOCS & HOW-TO`

Short, non-boring text:

- How to use the template in **3 steps**.  
- How to turn outputs into:
  - A Gumroad / LemonSqueezy product
  - A Fiverr/Upwork gig
  - A Notion/Excel pack

This becomes part of the **value** of the template (mini-guide).
