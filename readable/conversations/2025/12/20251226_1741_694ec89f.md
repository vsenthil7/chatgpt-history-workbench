## 1) **Risk Taxonomy (FINAL)**

**R1 — Legal / Regulatory Risk**  
Exposure arising from visa law interpretation, jurisdictional variance, or regulatory misuse.

**R2 — Advisory / Decision Risk**  
Risk of user acting on outputs as determinative advice.

**R3 — Operational Risk**  
Failures in workflow execution, integrations, uptime, or human handoff.

**R4 — Data Risk**  
Privacy, retention, access control, breach, or unlawful processing.

**R5 — Interpretation Risk**  
Misreading, over-reliance, or selective extraction of outputs.

**R6 — Override / Misuse Risk**  
Intentional bypass of safeguards, warnings, or human-review steps.

> **Rule:** Every incident must map to ≥1 risk class above. Unmapped risk = system invalid.

---

## 2) **Liability & Responsibility Matrix (LOCKED)**

| Actor | Owns | Does NOT Own |
|---|---|---|
| **VisaVerse Platform** | System availability; declared safeguards; audit logs; disclaimer enforcement | Legal outcomes; decisions; compliance determinations; user actions |
| **Employer / Sponsor** | Final visa strategy; compliance submissions; representations to authorities | AI outputs; platform logic |
| **Human Operator (Licensed/Authorized)** | Advice, review, sign-off where used | Platform errors outside declared scope |
| **End User** | Decisions taken; interpretation; submission of information | Platform compliance; legal guarantees |
| **AI System** | *None* | All liability |

> **Rule:** Liability never transfers implicitly. Only explicit human acceptance transfers responsibility.

---

## 3) **AI Non-Liability Declaration (CANONICAL)**

- AI outputs are **informational, assistive, and non-determinative**.  
- AI **does not provide legal advice**, does not represent authorities, and does not make eligibility determinations.  
- AI **cannot be cited** as an authority or relied upon for compliance assurance.

---

## 4) **Failure & Misuse Responsibility Rules**

**F1 — System Failure (within declared scope):**  
Platform responsible for remediation **only** (no consequential liability).

**F2 — Human Override After Warning:**  
Responsibility shifts fully to the human actor.

**F3 — Misinterpretation Without Review:**  
End user responsibility.

**F4 — Data Misuse or False Inputs:**  
Submitting party responsible.

**F5 — Jurisdictional Conflict:**  
Employer / licensed operator responsible for final choice.

---

## 5) **Disclaimer Logic & Placement (LOCKED)**

- **Entry Gate:** Non-advice notice + responsibility acceptance (mandatory).  
- **Decision Points:** Contextual reminders before any “next step” guidance.  
- **Export / Report:** Embedded non-liability footer + audit reference.  
- **Override Events:** Explicit acknowledgment required.

> **Rule:** If a disclaimer is skipped, the action is blocked.

---

## 6) **Legal Positioning Canon (FINAL)**

VisaVerse is a **decision-support platform**, not a legal service provider.  
All **legal responsibility resides with humans** who review, decide, and act.  
AI functions are **tools**, not agents, and **bear no liability**.
