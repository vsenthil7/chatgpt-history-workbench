# **HACK-003-02-P16 — Enforcement, Remedies & Non-Compliance Resolution (Canonical)**

**Status:** OPEN  
**Mode:** AUTHORITATIVE / CANONICAL (NO CODE)  
**Frozen scope:** YES  

---

## **Purpose of this Page**

Lock the **enforcement and remediation canon** governing how VisaVerse responds when rules are violated, safeguards fail, disclosures lapse, or obligations are breached—ensuring **no silent failures**, **clear accountability**, and **regulator-aligned corrective action**.

This page ensures VisaVerse is not just governed, but **governable under failure**.

---

## **Canonical Rule (Binding)**

**A detected violation without an enforced remedy constitutes a secondary violation.**  
Secondary violations trigger **automatic escalation** and **expanded enforcement**.

---

## **I. Non-Compliance Detection Triggers**

Non-compliance is deemed **detected** when **any** of the following occur:

1. **Automated Triggers**
   - Policy rule breach (P11) confirmed by monitoring (P14)
   - Kill-switch invocation anomaly (P13)
   - Logging, audit, or disclosure gap beyond tolerance (P14, P15)

2. **Human Triggers**
   - Internal audit finding (scheduled or ad-hoc)
   - Operator, reviewer, or whistle report
   - Failed attestation or certification checkpoint

3. **External Triggers**
   - Regulator inquiry or notice
   - Verified third-party report
   - User-filed remedy request with evidence

**Detection Timestamp (DT):**  
The earliest verifiable signal marks DT and anchors **all timelines** below.

---

## **II. Non-Compliance Classification Matrix**

| Class | Description | Risk Level | Examples |
|------:|-------------|------------|----------|
| **C1** | Procedural lapse | Low | Delayed disclosure, minor logging gap |
| **C2** | Control failure | Medium | Safeguard bypass, incomplete audit trail |
| **C3** | Material breach | High | Unauthorized action, duty violation |
| **C4** | Systemic failure | Critical | Repeated breaches, kill-switch misuse |
| **C5** | Obstruction / concealment | Severe | Tampering, falsification, non-cooperation |

*Classification is **provisional at DT** and may escalate with evidence.*

---

## **III. Enforcement Action Ladder (Mandatory Order)**

Enforcement proceeds **stepwise** unless immediate escalation is required.

1. **E1 — Automatic Containment**
   - Scope restriction, feature freeze, or kill-switch (as applicable)
   - Mandatory logging amplification

2. **E2 — Mandatory Remediation Order**
   - Prescribed corrective actions with deadlines
   - Proof requirements issued

3. **E3 — Human Oversight & Review**
   - Named authority assigned
   - Independent verification initiated

4. **E4 — User Remedies Activation**
   - Credits, reversals, corrections, disclosures (see Section V)

5. **E5 — Escalation / Suspension**
   - Partial or full suspension
   - Regulator notification if threshold met

6. **E6 — Termination / External Enforcement**
   - Contract termination, access revocation
   - Formal regulatory referral

*Skipping a step requires **documented necessity** and **audit visibility**.*

---

## **IV. Remediation Workflows & Timelines**

### **Standard Timelines (from DT)**

- **Containment:** ≤ 1 hour (automatic)  
- **Remediation Plan Issued:** ≤ 24 hours  
- **Correction Implemented:**  
  - C1–C2: ≤ 7 days  
  - C3: ≤ 72 hours  
  - C4–C5: ≤ 24 hours (or immediate)

### **Proof Requirements (Mandatory)**

- Verifiable logs before/after
- Independent confirmation (human or system)
- Disclosure update (if applicable)
- Closure attestation signed by authority

Failure to meet timelines → **Secondary Violation**.

---

## **V. User Remedies (Binding Definitions)**

When users are impacted, **at least one** remedy must be applied:

1. **Correction**
   - Data fix, decision reversal, or output correction

2. **Restitution**
   - Credit, refund, or equivalent compensation

3. **Disclosure**
   - Clear explanation of failure and resolution

4. **Assurance**
   - Preventive steps communicated post-remediation

Remedies must be **timely, proportional, and documented**.

---

## **VI. Escalation Paths & Authority Boundaries**

| Level | Authority | Powers |
|------:|-----------|--------|
| L1 | System Automation | Containment, logging, alerts |
| L2 | Designated Operator | Remediation execution |
| L3 | Oversight Function | Escalation, suspension |
| L4 | Governance Body | Termination, regulator notice |

**Conflict Rule:**  
If an authority is implicated, control **automatically elevates** to the next level.

---

## **VII. Repeat-Violation & Suspension Rules**

- **Repeat Threshold:**  
  - ≥2 violations of same class within 90 days → class escalates by one level
- **Chronic Failure:**  
  - Any C4/C5 repeat → mandatory suspension
- **Reinstatement:**  
  - Requires full remediation proof + independent audit sign-off

---

## **VIII. Closure Conditions for This Page**

This page is considered **CLOSED** when the following artifacts are finalized and locked:

- Non-compliance classification matrix  
- Enforcement action ladder  
- Remediation timelines & proof requirements  
- User remedy definitions  
- Escalation & suspension rules  

---

**Canonical Reminder:**  
Governance without enforcement is **illusory**.  
In VisaVerse, **every failure is observable, enforceable, and resolved—or escalated.**
