Karupatti dissolves slower than white sugar. To make it work well in drinks:

- Add 10–30% more volume than white sugar for similar sweetness

**Cold beverages / syrups:**
- Make a **karupatti simple syrup:**  
  - Heat equal parts powdered karupatti + water until dissolved  
  - Cool → use in lassi, iced teas, cocktails  
→ this bypasses incomplete dissolution in cold liquids

### B. **Baking reformulation (science + food-tech rules)**

Karupatti has minerals and moisture; it behaves differently than white sugar:

**Challenges**
- Affects gluten structure  
- Slower caramelisation  
- Added moisture → texture issues  
- Strong flavour that can dominate

1. **Mix with neutral sugar base**  
   - Start at 50% white sugar + 50% karupatti

2. **Use powdered form**
   - Powdered palm jaggery integrates better than chunks

3. **Adjust liquid**
   - Reduce added liquid (milk/water) because jaggery has moisture

4. **Add balancing agents**
   - Vanilla or spice extracts to balance flavour
   - Acidic agent (yogurt/lemon) to help rise

5. **Test small batches**
   - Fine-tune ratios before scaling
