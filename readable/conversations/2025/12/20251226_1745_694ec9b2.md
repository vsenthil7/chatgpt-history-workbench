# **HACK-003-02-P14 — Monitoring, Auditability & Continuous Oversight (Canonical)**

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Mode:** AUTHORITATIVE / CANONICAL (NO CODE)  
**Scope:** Continuous, provable observability and oversight of VisaVerse across runtime, data, policy, and human layers.

---

## **I. Monitoring Domains (LOCKED)**

VisaVerse monitoring is partitioned into **four non-overlapping domains**. Each domain is mandatory.

### **1. Runtime & System Behavior**
Tracks *what the system does*.

- Service lifecycle events (start/stop/restart)
- Model invocation metadata (model ID, version, purpose tag)
- Decision execution traces (allowed / denied / escalated)
- Kill-switch activation attempts & outcomes
- Degraded / safe-mode transitions

**Rule:** No runtime action may occur without a traceable event record.

---

### **2. Data Flow & Transformation**
Tracks *what data moves and how it changes*.

- Data ingress/egress events (source, destination, classification)
- Transformation steps (redaction, summarization, scoring)
- Retention timers (start, pause, expiry, deletion proof)
- Cross-border handling flags (if applicable)

**Rule:** Every data mutation must be reconstructible step-by-step.

---

### **3. Policy & Compliance Enforcement**
Tracks *why something was allowed or blocked*.

- Policy ID evaluated
- Policy version hash
- Decision outcome (pass / fail / conditional)
- Reason codes (machine-readable + human-readable)
- Override attempts (successful or blocked)

**Rule:** Decisions without a policy reference are invalid.

---

### **4. Human Actions & Overrides**
Tracks *who intervened*.

- Operator / reviewer / auditor identity (role-scoped)
- Action type (approve, deny, pause, escalate)
- Justification text (mandatory for overrides)
- Time-bound validity of actions

**Rule:** Anonymous or unattributed human actions are forbidden.

---

## **II. Audit Log Canon (IMMUTABLE)**

### **A. Mandatory Audit Fields**

Every audit record MUST contain:

- `event_id` (globally unique)
- `timestamp_utc`
- `domain` (runtime / data / policy / human)
- `actor_type` (system / human / external)
- `actor_id` (or system component ID)
- `action`
- `object_affected`
- `policy_reference` (if applicable)
- `decision_outcome`
- `reason_code`
- `evidence_pointer` (hash / snapshot / document)
- `integrity_hash`

---

### **B. Retention Rules**

- **Operational logs:** minimum 24 months  
- **Compliance & decision logs:** minimum 7 years  
- **Incident & dispute logs:** retained until formal closure + statutory buffer

Deletion requires **cryptographic proof of expiry**, not silent removal.

---

### **C. Immutability Guarantees**

- Append-only storage
- Hash-chained records
- Tamper detection mandatory
- Any corruption triggers an **incident state**

---

## **III. Continuous Oversight Signals & Alerts**

### **A. Real-Time Alerts (Immediate)**

Triggered when:

- Kill-switch is activated or bypassed
- Policy evaluation fails unexpectedly
- Unauthorized access attempt detected
- Audit log integrity check fails

---

### **B. Threshold & Pattern Alerts**

Triggered on:

- Repeated override attempts
- Drift between expected vs actual decisions
- Anomalous data access patterns
- Model behavior outside declared scope

---

### **C. Periodic Oversight Reviews**

- Daily automated summaries
- Weekly compliance snapshots
- Quarterly governance reviews
- Annual external audit readiness check

---

## **IV. Access & Visibility Matrix (LOCKED)**

| Role | Runtime Logs | Data Logs | Policy Logs | Human Action Logs |
|----|----|----|----|----|
| System Operator | View | Limited | View | None |
| Compliance Officer | View | View | View | View |
| Auditor (Internal) | View | View | View | View |
| Auditor (External) | Read-only | Read-only | Read-only | Read-only |
| Developer | Limited | None | Limited | None |

**Rule:** Visibility is role-based, never discretionary.

---

## **V. External Audit & Regulator Inspection Protocol**

VisaVerse must support:

- On-demand log export (read-only)
- Time-bounded inspection windows
- Independent verification of integrity hashes
- Reconstruction of any decision within stated retention periods

No live system mutation is permitted during inspection.

---

## **VI. Evidence Generation & Incident Readiness**

For any incident, VisaVerse must generate:

- Complete event timeline
- Policy & rule versions in effect
- Human actions taken (if any)
- System state before and after
- Immutable evidence bundle (exportable)

---

## **Canonical Compliance Rule (FINAL)**

> **If a behavior cannot be observed, logged, reconstructed, and explained, it is treated as a system failure.**

Such failures must be:
1. Logged as incidents  
2. Escalated to oversight roles  
3. Resolved before normal operation resumes
