---

### **Screen 1 — Welcome / Context**
**On-screen copy:**  
**VisaVerse**  
*A reflection-only demo for personal readiness and safety awareness.*

**Narration (8–10s):**  
“VisaVerse is a calm, reflection-only experience. It does not assess eligibility, verify documents, or give advice. It helps you pause, reflect, and notice patterns—nothing more.”

**Explicit non-claim (small text):**  
No scoring • No verification • No decisions

---

### **Screen 2 — Work Readiness Reflection (Input)**
**On-screen copy:**  
**Work Readiness**  
Describe your current situation in your own words.

**Narration (10–12s):**  
“Here, you describe your situation freely. The AI listens only to reflect your words back—there is no evaluation, ranking, or recommendation.”

**Explicit non-claim:**  
Your input is used only for this session.

---

### **Screen 3 — Readiness Snapshot (AI Reflection)**
**On-screen copy:**  
**Readiness Snapshot**  
A descriptive mirror of what you shared.

**Narration (12–15s):**  
“The snapshot you see is a descriptive mirror, generated by AI in real time. It summarises themes and signals from your input without judging readiness or suggesting next steps.”

**Explicit non-claim:**  
Not a score • Not an assessment

---

### **Screen 4 — Safety Awareness (Pattern Explanation)**
**On-screen copy:**  
**Safety Awareness**  
Common risk patterns to be aware of.

**Narration (12–15s):**  
“This step highlights common risk patterns people often encounter. The AI explains patterns only—it does not confirm scams, validate documents, or assess legality.”

**Explicit non-claim:**  
Awareness only • No confirmation

---

### **Screen 5 — Safety Reflection (User Insight)**
**On-screen copy:**  
**Safety Reflection**  
What stands out to you?

**Narration (10–12s):**  
“You decide what matters. This reflection step is for your own awareness—VisaVerse does not tell you what to do or what is right.”

**Explicit non-claim:**  
Your judgement, not the system’s

---

### **Screen 6 — Completion / Closure**
**On-screen copy:**  
**Complete**  
This concludes the reflection-only demo.

**Narration (8–10s):**  
“This demo ends here. No data is stored, no conclusions are drawn, and no actions are recommended. VisaVerse exists only to support reflection and awareness.”

**Explicit non-claim (final):**  
No advice • No decisions • No persistence
