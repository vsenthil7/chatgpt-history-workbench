### 🔒 **VisaVerse — Guardrails, Ethics & Non-Claims (LOCKED TEXT)**  
*(Hack-003.08 — Documentation Only)*

---

## 1) Core Ethical Position

VisaVerse is a **reflection-only, awareness-oriented system** designed to help individuals **articulate their situation** and **recognise common risk patterns** related to work readiness and document/scam safety.

It is intentionally **non-authoritative**, **non-predictive**, and **non-advisory**.

The system’s purpose is **clarity, not conclusion**.

---

## 2) What the System DOES

VisaVerse **does**:

- Accept **user-provided, self-described inputs**
- Use **AI to reflect patterns in natural language**
- Present **descriptive summaries** based strictly on what the user shares
- Highlight **common, well-known risk signals** for awareness only
- Maintain a **calm, neutral, non-directive tone**
- Align all outputs exactly with what is shown in the demo

---

## 3) What the System DOES NOT Do (Non-Claims)

VisaVerse **does NOT**:

- ❌ Judge readiness, suitability, eligibility, or likelihood of success  
- ❌ Score, rank, rate, or compare users  
- ❌ Predict visa outcomes, job outcomes, or legal status  
- ❌ Verify documents, identities, organisations, or offers  
- ❌ Confirm or label scams or fraud  
- ❌ Provide legal, immigration, employment, or financial advice  
- ❌ Recommend actions that imply authority or obligation  
- ❌ Store, persist, or reuse personal data  
- ❌ Replace professional, legal, or government guidance  

No output should be interpreted as a decision, validation, warning, or approval.

---

## 4) AI Usage Boundaries

AI within VisaVerse is used **only** for:

- Language understanding of user input
- Pattern reflection using **pre-defined, deterministic schemas**
- Neutral summarisation and awareness framing

AI is **explicitly not used** to:

- Make determinations
- Enforce rules
- Detect crimes
- Validate authenticity
- Provide instructions that imply correctness or safety

All AI outputs are **contextual reflections**, not facts or findings.

---

## 5) Non-Authority & User Responsibility Statement

VisaVerse operates as a **self-reflection aid**.

Users retain **full responsibility** for:

- Interpreting outputs
- Making decisions
- Seeking professional or official advice where required

The system does not claim jurisdiction, expertise, or endorsement from any authority.

---

## 6) Demo & Implementation Consistency Rule

- The demo narration
- The on-screen text
- The AI output language
- This documentation

…are **intentionally aligned**.

There are **no hidden capabilities**, **no future promises**, and **no implied extensions** beyond what is demonstrated.

---

## 7) Regulatory & Judge-Safety Intent

These guardrails exist to ensure:

- Transparency of scope
- Avoidance of implied authority
- Reduction of user harm
- Clear boundaries for judges and reviewers

VisaVerse is presented as a **safe, limited, honest prototype**, not a deployed decision system.
