GPT — What it is (and what it isn’t)

**GPT** = *Generative Pre-trained Transformer*.

- **Generative**: it produces (“generates”) text, code, plans, drafts, etc.  
- **Pre-trained**: it learned patterns from lots of text during training (not memorizing your private chats as a database).  
- **Transformer**: the neural-network architecture that’s good at language (attention-based).

### “Is GPT codes? Apps in you?”
Not exactly.

- **GPT is a model** (a learned probability machine for language).  
- **Code** is one kind of output GPT can generate.  
- **Apps/tools** are *extra integrations* that may be attached around the model (search, file reading, calendar, etc.). Those are not “inside GPT” as code—more like plug-ins that the app (ChatGPT) can choose to use.

### What *is inside* vs *outside*
- **Inside (core)**: the language model + its learned weights (patterns).  
- **Outside (tools)**: browsing, reading files, generating PDFs, calling connectors, etc. (only when allowed + used).
