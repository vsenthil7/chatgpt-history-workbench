## **I. External Escalation — Trigger Matrix (Locked)**

External escalation is **mandatory**, **permissive**, or **prohibited** based on trigger class. No forum shopping. No discretionary bypass.

### **A. Mandatory External Escalation (Auto-Notify)**

Triggered when **any** condition below is met:

1. **Statutory reporting threshold crossed**  
   (e.g., systemic risk, protected class impact, regulated harm category).
2. **Regulator-reserved domain breach**  
   (areas explicitly outside private adjudication).
3. **Court order, warrant, or lawful notice received**.
4. **Cross-border incident with multi-jurisdiction impact** requiring lead-authority designation.
5. **Internal process exhaustion + non-waivable rights implicated**.

**Action:**  
Immediate regulator notification within **T₀ + 24–72 hours** (as applicable), preservation of evidence, suspension of conflicting internal actions.

---

### **B. Permissive External Escalation (User-Initiated / Consent-Bound)**

Allowed when:

1. Internal remedies under **P17** are exhausted **or** time-barred.
2. Arbitration is contractually available and not prohibited by law.
3. Ombudsman review is available as a non-binding step.

**Action:**  
VisaVerse cooperates, produces records, and aligns outcomes—without obstructing user access.

---

### **C. Prohibited External Escalation**

Disallowed when:

1. Escalation would violate an active court/regulator stay.
2. Parallel proceedings would create double-jeopardy equivalents barred by law.
3. Confidentiality or national security prohibitions apply.

**Action:**  
Written notice issued citing prohibition basis; alternative lawful pathway provided.

---

## **II. Regulator Interface — Rules & Timelines (Locked)**

### **A. Single-Point Interface Principle**

* One **Regulatory Liaison Function (RLF)** per matter.
* All communications logged, time-stamped, immutable (P14).

### **B. Notification Standards**

| Event Class | Initial Notice | Full Dossier |
|------------|----------------|--------------|
| Mandatory trigger | 24–72 hrs | ≤ 14 days |
| Regulator inquiry | ≤ 7 days | As ordered |
| Court notice | Immediate | As ordered |

**Minimum Dossier:** scope, facts, impact assessment, controls invoked, remedial actions taken, contact authority.

### **C. Cooperation & Non-Interference**

* No evidence destruction or selective disclosure.  
* Internal enforcement pauses **only** where required by law/order.  
* Conflicts resolved in favor of lawful external directives.

---

## **III. Judicial vs Arbitration Pathways (Defined)**

### **A. Arbitration (Where Applicable)**

* Permitted only if **lawful, consensual, and not barred**.
* Scope limited to contractual/private claims.
* Outcomes binding **within permitted scope** only.

### **B. Courts**

* Default for statutory rights, public law, injunctions, and criminal matters.
* Court orders **supersede** all internal outcomes.

**No Parallelism Rule:**  
If a court accepts jurisdiction, arbitration/internal processes cease on overlapping issues.

---

## **IV. Binding Authority & Alignment Rules (Locked)**

1. **Hierarchy of Authority**  
   **Court Orders / Statutory Regulators** → **Regulator Directives** → **Arbitration Awards** → **Internal Outcomes**.
2. **Automatic Alignment**  
   Systems, enforcement states, and remedies update to match binding outcomes.
3. **Non-Retaliation & Preservation**  
   No adverse action for lawful escalation; records preserved per order.

---

## **V. Cross-Jurisdiction Conflict Canon**

### **A. Lead Authority Determination**

* Based on **primary impact**, **data locus**, and **statutory mandate**.
* Secondary authorities notified; duplicative demands harmonized.

### **B. Conflicting Orders**

* Seek clarification; comply with the **most restrictive lawful order** pending resolution.
* Document rationale; notify all authorities of interim posture.

### **C. Data Transfer & Sovereignty**

* Transfers only under lawful mechanisms; redaction/minimization enforced.
* Where prohibited, provide in-country inspection or escrow access.
