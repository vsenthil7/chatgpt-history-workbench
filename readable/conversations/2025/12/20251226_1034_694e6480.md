## 🧠 **1. Economics: In-Flows and Out-Flows (In/Out)**

At its core, *economics* in a business context tracks how money **flows into** and **flows out of** an organization. This helps determine whether the business is sustainable and profitable.

### **In-Flows (Revenue / Income)**
These are all the ways money comes **into** the business:
- **Sales revenue** — selling goods or services
- **Interest or investment income**
- **Grants & subsidies** (for some firms)
- **Asset disposals**

📌 *In simple terms:* **Money coming IN.**

### **Out-Flows (Expenses / Costs)**
These are all the ways money goes **out** of the business:
- **Operating expenses** (rent, utilities, salaries)
- **Cost of goods sold (COGS)**
- **Marketing & sales**
- **Taxes**
- **Interest payments**

📌 *In simple terms:* **Money going OUT.**

---

## 📊 **2. Profit vs. Loss**

Profit is the financial gain after subtracting all costs from revenue.

### **Profit = Revenue − Expenses**

- If **Revenue > Expenses → Profit**
- If **Revenue < Expenses → Loss**
- If **Revenue = Expenses → Break-even**

**Types of Profit:**
1. **Gross Profit:** Revenue − COGS  
   (Before general overheads like admin or rent)
2. **Operating Profit:** Revenue − Operating expenses  
   (After regular costs but before taxes/interest)
3. **Net Profit:** Final bottom-line profit after all expenses.

**Example:**  
A shop earns £5000 in sales in a month, has £3000 of costs → **Profit = £2000.**

---

## 📚 **3. Expenses Explained**

Expenses are out-flows that help the business operate.

### **Types of Expenses**
| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Fixed Costs** | Rent, salaries, insurance (don’t change with output) |
| **Variable Costs** | Raw materials, hourly wages (change with production) |
| **Semi-Variable** | Electricity, bonuses |
| **One-off/Capital** | Purchasing machinery, asset investment |

Understanding the **nature of expenses** is key to planning budgets and pricing.

---

## 👔 **4. HR Management (Human Resources)**

HR Management is the **function that manages the people** in an organization — the workforce. It combines economics (cost, productivity) with people management.

### **Core HR Roles**
1. **Recruitment & Hiring:** Finding and selecting employees.
2. **Training & Development:** Improving skills & performance.
3. **Performance Management:** Reviews, targets, appraisals.
4. **Compensation & Benefits:** Salaries, bonuses, health cover.
5. **Workplace Policies & Compliance:** Laws, regulations, safety.
6. **Employee Engagement & Culture:** Motivation, morale.

### **HR as an Economic Function**
HR connects economics with people cost:

- **Benefits Negotiation:** Pay scales, health benefits, pensions.
