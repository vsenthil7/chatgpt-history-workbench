## **1. Standing & Eligibility to File**

### **1.1 Who May File**
A formal dispute or appeal may be initiated only by:
* The **directly affected user, entity, or account**
* An **authorized representative** (verified delegation required)
* A **regulator-mandated proxy** (where law requires)

No third-party advocacy, collective action, or anonymous filings are admissible within internal due process.

### **1.2 What May Be Challenged**
Appeals are limited to:
* Enforcement actions under **P16**
* Remedies imposed or denied
* Account suspension, restriction, or termination
* Material disclosure corrections under **P15**

System design choices, policy existence, or lawful regulator-mandated actions are **non-appealable**.

---

## **2. Admissible Grounds for Appeal**

Appeals are accepted **only** on one or more of the following grounds:

| Ground | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Procedural error** | A defined P16 step was omitted, misordered, or violated |
| **Material factual error** | Evidence relied upon was demonstrably incorrect |
| **New evidence** | Evidence unavailable at enforcement time that materially alters outcome |
| **Misapplication of canon** | Enforcement applied a rule outside its defined scope |
| **Proportionality breach** | Remedy exceeds the maximum permitted under P16 |

Disagreement with outcomes alone is not a valid ground.

---

## **3. Evidence Standards**

### **3.1 Acceptable Evidence**
* System logs and audit records (P14)
* Verifiable documents, records, or communications
* Regulator correspondence
* Independent expert attestations (where permitted)

### **3.2 Evidence Integrity Rules**
* Evidence must be **unaltered, timestamped, and attributable**
* Retroactive fabrication invalidates the appeal
* Hearsay, conjecture, or narrative statements without proof are inadmissible

---

## **4. Appeal Timelines & SLAs**

| Stage | Time Limit |
|---|---|
| Filing window | ≤ 14 calendar days from enforcement notice |
| Initial admissibility check | ≤ 5 business days |
| Substantive review | ≤ 30 calendar days |
| Decision issuance | ≤ 7 business days after review close |

Late filings are automatically dismissed unless regulator override applies.

---

## **5. Review Authority & Independence**

### **5.1 Internal Review Tiers**
1. **Tier-1 Review Panel**  
   *Independent of original enforcement actor*
2. **Tier-2 Oversight Authority**  
   *Escalation only for high-impact or precedent-setting cases*

### **5.2 Authority Limits**
* Review bodies **cannot** weaken P16 enforcement standards
* No reviewer may assess a case they participated in
* Conflicts of interest mandate recusal

---

## **6. Permissible Outcomes**

A review may result in one of the following canonical outcomes only:

* **Uphold** — enforcement stands unchanged
* **Modify** — remedy adjusted within P16 bounds
* **Reverse** — enforcement voided due to invalidity
* **Remand** — returned for re-execution with binding instructions

No novel remedies may be created at appeal stage.

---

## **7. Finality & Exhaustion Rules**

* Tier-2 decisions are **final within VisaVerse**
* Exhaustion of P17 is mandatory before any external remedy
* Repeated or abusive appeals may trigger restriction under P16

---

## **8. Regulator Override & Supremacy**

* Regulator-mandated reviews supersede internal finality
* Judicial or statutory orders override all internal outcomes
* VisaVerse must preserve records to enable external inspection

---

## **9. Anti-Abuse & Integrity Safeguards**

* Frivolous or bad-faith appeals are logged as risk signals
* Pattern abuse escalates to enforcement review
* Due process may not be weaponized to delay compliance
