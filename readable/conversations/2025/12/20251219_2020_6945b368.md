## 1️⃣ 30-Second Elevator Pitch

**Jira Sentinel exists because Jira Cloud has become too powerful and too fragile.**  

Today, Jira admins make configuration changes every day—permissions, workflows, automations, apps—but **there is no built-in way to clearly see what changed, who changed it, and whether that change quietly broke governance or security**. Problems surface only after users complain, tickets fail, or audits flag issues.

**Jira Sentinel continuously watches Jira’s configuration layer** and surfaces risky, unexpected, or high-impact changes *as they happen*.  
It gives admins **early visibility, not late surprises**—so Jira stays trustworthy, stable, and compliant.

---

## 2️⃣ 2-Minute Judge Story (Why It Exists, Pain → Value → Outcome)

Imagine you’re a **Jira administrator** in a growing organisation.

Your Jira instance isn’t just a tool anymore—it’s the backbone of delivery.  
Multiple teams. Multiple projects. Marketplace apps. Automation rules. Permission schemes. Constant change.

Now here’s the problem:  
**Jira Cloud does not give you a single, clear picture of configuration risk.**

Changes happen silently:
- A permission scheme is modified  
- An automation rule is edited  
- A workflow is altered  
- An app changes its scope  

Nothing looks wrong—until suddenly:
- A team loses access  
- A workflow breaks production  
- An audit asks, *“Who changed this, and when?”*  
- Or worse, a security gap goes unnoticed  

Admins today are forced to **react after damage**, using manual checks, scattered logs, or gut feeling.

That is the pain.

---

**Jira Sentinel exists to shift admins from reaction to awareness.**

It continuously observes Jira’s **configuration state**, detects meaningful changes, and highlights **what matters**—not noise.

It answers the questions admins actually struggle with:
- *What changed since yesterday?*
- *Is this change risky or expected?*
- *Does this affect permissions, compliance, or stability?*

No guessing. No diff-hunting. No waiting for users to report failures.

---

**The outcome is simple but powerful:**
- Admins regain confidence in their Jira environment  
- Risky changes are seen early, not discovered late  
- Jira becomes governable at scale, not fragile at scale  

**Jira Sentinel doesn’t replace Jira.  
It protects it.**

That’s why it exists.
