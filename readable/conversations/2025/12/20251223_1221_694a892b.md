## 🧾 **1. Billing & Subscription Overview (Atlassian Cloud)**

Atlassian’s **billing hub** lets you manage all cloud subscriptions (Standard, Premium, Enterprise) for apps such as Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, and others.  
You can:  
- **View your active subscriptions & current bill**  
- **Switch between monthly vs annual billing**  
- **Add/remove users** (affects billing)  
- **Change payment method or update tax information**  
- **Cancel or renew apps**  
All of this is done via your billing account in **admin.atlassian.com**. citeturn0search15

The main documentation page grouped these billing topics (subscriptions, plans, billing profiles, Marketplace apps, usage charges, Rovo billing, and more). citeturn0view0

---

## 📊 **2. How Atlassian Charges You**

### **Seat-based Billing**

For cloud products like **Jira & Confluence**, pricing is **per user** — the more users you have, the higher the bill.  
- **Monthly** or **Annual** payment cycles available.  
- Price is based on the **maximum number of users in the billing period**.  
  That means if you add users mid-month, you pay for the highest user count that month (Maximum Quantity Billing). citeturn0search1turn0search16

### **Plan Levels**

Products often come in tiers:  
- **Free** — limited users/features  
- **Standard** — core functionality  
- **Premium** — more controls, SLAs, storage, analytics  
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing / advanced enterprise features citeturn0search2

---

## 🔹 **3. Jira (Core Product)**

**Jira Cloud** pricing is user-based and progressive — higher headcount often unlocks volume discounts, but billing always aligns to user seats within your plan.  
- Applies to **Jira Software**, **Jira Service Management**, and unified modern “Jira” offerings.  
- You’re charged monthly or annually per user seat.  
- Billing includes seat changes prorated across billing cycle. citeturn0search3turn0search16

**Note:** *Jira Work Management* has been merged into core Jira in recent changes, with its users consolidated — but billing remains under your Jira subscription. citeturn0search10

---

## 📘 **4. Confluence**

**Confluence Cloud** also uses a per-user pricing model with tiered plans: Free, Standard and Premium available.  
- Free plans are perpetual for small teams (up to 10 users).  
- For paid plans, you’re billed per user per month/year.  
- Higher tiers add more storage, admin controls, scale. citeturn0search2

---

## 🤖 **5. Rovo (Atlassian AI)**

Rovo is Atlassian’s integrated **AI layer** (search, chat, agents).  

**Current billing model:**
- Rovo *is included* with **Jira, Confluence, JSM or Teamwork Collection cloud plans** — no separate subscription line item for most users. citeturn0search19  
- Initially rolled out at no extra cost; usage charges may apply beyond quotas in future. citeturn0search11  
- If charges *do* occur (advanced usage beyond included), Rovo will appear on your bill as separate entries.

So **you don’t pay separately for Rovo** unless you exceed included usage; it’s tied to your main product subscription. citeturn0search19

---

## ⚙️ **6. Forge Platform**

**Forge** is a developer platform for building and running **cloud apps** within the Atlassian ecosystem:  
- Pricing is **consumption-based** — you pay based on actual usage of hosted functions, storage, APIs, etc. citeturn0search4  
- Many features are **free within generous limits**, and costs apply only when you exceed those. citeturn0search4  

This is different from seat-based billing for core products like Jira/Confluence — Forge costs are tied to **resource usage**, not number of users.

---

## 🧩 **7. Marketplace Add-ons**

Apps from the **Atlassian Marketplace** (e.g., portfolio boards, reporting extensions) are billed based on:  
- Number of users  
- Same billing cycle as your host product (Jira or Confluence).  
Installing an app *after* joining a collection may base billing on the collection’s total user count. citeturn0search17

---

## 📦 **8. Collections (Bundles)**

Atlassian offers **Collections** (e.g. Teamwork Collection, combining Jira + Confluence + other tools).  
- Instead of paying separately for each product, you receive a **single billable line item** for the bundle.  
- This simplifies invoicing and may reduce overall costs. citeturn0search8

---

## 🧠 **9. Summary: Product Billing Models**

| Product / Feature | Billing Model | Notes |
|------------------|---------------|-------|
| **Jira Cloud** | Seat-based subscription | Monthly/annual |
| **Confluence Cloud** | Seat-based subscription | Tiered plans |
| **Jira Service Management** | Seat/agent-based | Similar model |
| **Rovo** | Included + consumption | Often free unless over quota |
| **Forge** | Usage-based / consumption | Not tied to user seats |
| **Marketplace apps** | Seat-based | Matches host app billing |
| **Collections** | Single bundle billing | Consolidated invoices |
