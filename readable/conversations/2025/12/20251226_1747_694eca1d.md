# **HACK-003-02-P15 — Transparency, Reporting & External Disclosure (Canonical)**

**Status:** IN PROGRESS  
**Mode:** AUTHORITATIVE / CANONICAL (NO CODE)

---

## 1) **Disclosure Audience Map (LOCKED)**

VisaVerse recognizes **four non-overlapping disclosure audiences**.  
Each audience has **explicit entitlements and explicit exclusions**.

### **A. End Users (Individuals)**  
**Entitled to know:**
- What the system does and does **not** do  
- Material limitations affecting outcomes or decisions  
- Whether automated decisions or risk flags are involved  
- Data usage categories (high-level, non-technical)  
- Incident notices when user impact exists  

**Explicitly excluded:**
- Internal scoring logic  
- Risk thresholds  
- Detection heuristics  
- System tuning parameters  

---

### **B. Enterprise / Institutional Partners**  
**Entitled to know:**
- Operational guarantees and boundaries  
- Compliance posture and certifications  
- Incident classes and response timelines  
- Aggregate performance and reliability metrics  
- Clear responsibility allocation  

**Explicitly excluded:**
- Internal enforcement logic  
- Kill-switch thresholds  
- Model internals or vendor substitutions  

---

### **C. Regulators & Auditors**  
**Entitled to know:**
- Full compliance mapping to applicable regimes  
- Incident logs (structured, scoped, time-bounded)  
- Enforcement proof of safeguards and overrides  
- Audit trails required by law  

**Explicitly excluded:**
- Proprietary implementation details beyond legal necessity  
- Competitive architecture disclosures not mandated by statute  

---

### **D. Public / General Disclosure**  
**Entitled to know:**
- What VisaVerse claims to do (and refuses to claim)  
- High-level safety posture  
- Historical incident summaries (sanitized, aggregate)  
- Public compliance attestations  

**Explicitly excluded:**
- Any data, logic, or claims enabling reverse engineering  
- Any implied guarantees or performance promises  

---

## 2) **Reporting Categories (CANONICAL)**

All disclosures MUST fall into one of the following categories.  
Anything outside these categories is **prohibited by default**.

1. **Operational Transparency**
   - System availability
   - High-level function descriptions
   - Known functional boundaries

2. **Compliance & Governance**
   - Applicable regulations adhered to
   - Certification status (if any)
   - Audit readiness posture

3. **Incidents & Exceptions**
   - Confirmed system-impacting events
   - User-impacting errors or failures
   - Regulatory-reportable events

4. **Limitations & Non-Capabilities**
   - Explicit system blind spots
   - Known non-supported use cases
   - Disallowed reliance scenarios

---

## 3) **Mandatory vs Prohibited Disclosures (LOCKED)**

### **Mandatory Disclosure Triggers**
Disclosure is REQUIRED when:
- A user decision could reasonably change with the information
- A safety, legal, or compliance boundary is crossed
- A system safeguard or kill-switch is activated
- An incident impacts data integrity, availability, or trust

Failure to disclose under these conditions = **non-compliance**

---

### **Prohibited Disclosures**
The system MUST NOT disclose:
- Internal risk scoring formulas
- Enforcement thresholds or escalation rules
- Detection evasion vectors
- Hypothetical capabilities not deployed
- Future roadmap claims presented as current fact

---

## 4) **Incident & Breach Notification Rules (LOCKED)**

### **Classification**
All incidents are classified as:
- **Class I:** No user impact, internal containment
- **Class II:** User-impacting but reversible
- **Class III:** Material trust, legal, or safety impact

---

### **Notification Requirements**
- **Class I:** Logged internally only
- **Class II:** User notification required within defined window
- **Class III:** User + regulator notification mandatory, no discretion

Silence or delay beyond defined windows is **non-compliant**.

---

## 5) **Approved Transparency Artifacts (CANONICAL)**

Only the following artifacts may be used for disclosure:

- Transparency Notices (static, versioned)
- Incident Reports (scoped, timestamped)
- Compliance Attestations
- Public Non-Claim Statements
- Regulatory Filings (where mandated)

Marketing material is **explicitly excluded** as a transparency artifact.

---

## 6) **Anti-Misrepresentation & Non-Claim Boundaries (LOCKED)**

VisaVerse MUST NOT:
- Claim accuracy beyond validated scope
- Imply legal, immigration, or regulatory authority
- Present probabilistic outputs as determinations
- Suggest human equivalence or substitution

Silence is **mandatory** where certainty does not exist.  
Over-clarity is preferred to persuasive completeness.

---

## 7) **Enforcement Rule (FINAL)**

If any external communication:
- Omits a material limitation, OR  
- Overstates capability, OR  
- Implies authority not explicitly granted  

Then the system is considered **out of compliance**  
until corrected through an approved transparency artifact.

---

This page is CLOSED only when:
- All disclosure audiences are mapped
- All reporting categories are locked
- All non-claims are explicit and enforceable
- Incident notification rules are unambiguous
