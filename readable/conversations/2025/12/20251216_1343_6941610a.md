## Finger brushing — does it help?
**Yes, but with a role limit.**

**Benefits**
- Gentle **gum massage** → improves circulation  
- Better **pressure awareness** than a hard brush

- Cannot reach interdental spaces well  
- Removes **less plaque** than a soft toothbrush

**Best use**
- Morning: finger massage + light cleaning  
- Night: **soft toothbrush + fluoride**

  - Anti-inflammatory

## Can broken teeth grow back?
**No.**  
- Enamel and dentin **do not regenerate** once lost  
- Gums may tighten slightly, but structure won’t return  
- Only dental restoration (fillings, crowns, implants)

- **Calcium + phosphate:** supports tooth mineral balance  
- **Casein:** protective protein  
- **Milk sugar (lactose):** mild cavity risk if sipped frequently

**Best practice**
- Drink milk **with meals**, not constant sipping  
- Rinse mouth after bedtime milk (especially for kids)
