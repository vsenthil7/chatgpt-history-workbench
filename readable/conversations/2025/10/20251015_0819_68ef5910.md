### 🧠 **What are Tokens in LLMs?**  
Tokens are small pieces of text (words or subwords) that AI models read.  
Example: *“Learning AI is fun”* may be split as 5–6 tokens.  
LLM speed and cost are measured in **tokens per second**.

---

### 🗂️ **What is Context Window?**  
Context window is the memory space of an AI model – how much text it can “hold” during a conversation.  
Smaller context → AI forgets earlier parts.  
Larger context → AI can handle long chats or full documents.

---

### 🎭 **What is Hallucination in AI?**  
Hallucination happens when AI gives confident answers that are **incorrect or made-up**.  
It occurs when the model tries to fill missing facts with guesses.  
That’s why **verification** is important while using AI.

---

### 🎛️ **Temperature, Top-p, Top-k (Response Control)**  
| Parameter | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Temperature** | Controls creativity. Low = focused, High = creative/varied. |
| **Top-p (Nucleus Sampling)** | Filters answers by probability. Keeps meaningful tokens only. |
| **Top-k** | Limits the model to the *top k most probable* words. Lower value = safer answers. |

*(These parameters are not always visible in chat apps like ChatGPT, but developers use them to fine-tune behaviour.)*

---

### 🧪 Key Observation as a User  
Even if we don’t code, understanding these helps in writing **better prompts** and judging **AI reliability** (especially during hallucinations).
