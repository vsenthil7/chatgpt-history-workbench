3 · Context

OnCall Copilot is a Slack-native incident tool: an engineer types /incident declare, the app opens an incident channel, pages the on-call rotation, captures the timeline automatically, drafts a blameless postmortem on resolution and publishes to a status page — all without leaving Slack. Slack is the product surface; the HTTP API exists for settings and analytics, not as a web client.

The build has run for 30 releases under a 100% automated-coverage rule with discovery parked (decision D13). Two independent AI reviews have been commissioned: ChatGPT (13 September, 20 findings, 10 blocker) and Perplexity (16 September, 12 findings, 2 blocker). Both were dispositioned in full; the second is closed as of codebase v0.37.0. The enterprise-ready claim has been formally withdrawn until the remaining P0 set and live evidence close.

The single most important thing this document says: the difference between where the product is and a working product is not engineering time. It is a Slack workspace, a Stripe key, a KMS, a pen-test vendor and four decisions.

S49, S51, PX-S3.
The honest summary. Five of nine layers are finished and proven by 616 tests at 100% coverage over both the source and the composition scripts — including a boot gate that refuses to start production on anything fake, and a deferred-work queue that survives a deploy or a kill -9. What is missing is the last mile: the HTTP calls into Slack, the pager, Stripe and the status vendor. Those contracts are final, asynchronous, budgeted and probed, so writing the adapters is plumbing rather than redesign — which is exactly what the second review demanded be true before S38 started.

7 · Glossary of status words

WordMeansEvidence required to claim it
DONEBuilt, tested, and reachable by a user or an operator todayA named test that fails if the capability is removed, plus a caller audit proving a live surface reaches it
BLOCKEDBuilt and proven against fakes; cannot reach the real platformThe contract, the tests and the boot gate exist; a named credential, vendor or decision is missing
PARTIALSome of it exists; the remainder is a scheduled sprintThe shipped half is named and the missing half is on the roadmap with an owner
NOT BUILTNothing written yetA sprint id and a blocker; never a silent omission

These four words are the same vocabulary used in the sprint tracker (D-OC3) and in the review registers, so a status here can be traced to a row there.
