1. Where jobs are heading
Nobody can reliably name every future job. A more useful approach is to identify work that businesses will still need done—and what skills let someone take responsibility for it.

The evidence points toward changing jobs, not simply eliminating them. The ILO’s May 2025 assessment found that one in four workers globally is in an occupation with some generative-AI exposure, but transformation is more likely than complete replacement; clerical occupations have the highest exposure.

The World Economic Forum identifies AI and big data, networks and cybersecurity, and technological literacy as the fastest-growing skills through 2030, alongside analytical thinking, creativity, resilience, and continuous learning.
