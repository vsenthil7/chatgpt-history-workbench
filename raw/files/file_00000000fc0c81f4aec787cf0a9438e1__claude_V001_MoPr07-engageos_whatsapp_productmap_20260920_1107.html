2. Context

What EngageOS is. A WhatsApp-first customer-engagement suite for small businesses: a shared team inbox, campaign broadcasts, no-code chatbot flows, a catalog with UPI payment links, and analytics with a quality-rating guard, built in TypeScript on the WhatsApp Cloud API, in English, Hindi and Tamil, with four vertical template packs.

Where it stands. 80 of 100 backlog stories (312 of 399 points) are done in code. Thirteen stories wait for a real Meta account. Zero stories are Done, because Done means listed on the marketplace and paid for by a stranger, and no stranger has used the product. Two independent reviews have read it: ChatGPT (13 Sep) and Perplexity (16 Sep). Both say the engineering discipline is above average and the product is not yet something a stranger can sign up for, pay for, or keep their data in.

What this document is for. To let someone who has not followed the 46 releases see, in one picture, which parts of the product exist, which are only proven against a model of WhatsApp, and which are missing — and to see beside each gap the reason it is a gap, because in every case the reason is either something outside the code (Meta, infrastructure, a vendor) or a decision that only Senthil can make.

3. How to read this document

Green built, tested and wired into the running product. Amber built, but proven only against a fake Meta, or held in memory, or waiting on a decision to become useful. Red not built, or blocked by something outside the code. Italic text inside a box is the reason. Arrows are labelled with what moves along them.

Panel A is the mechanism: a customer's message enters at the top left, crosses Meta, is verified at the HTTP edge, is routed to its tenant, reaches a feature module, and is stored either in Postgres (green) or in process memory (red). The agent's browser sits on the right. Panel B is time. Section 6 is the same information as a table, with the finding IDs so each gap can be looked up in the tracker.

7. The decisions that move the most boxes

  1. Choose one target customer (ICP). Turns four amber vertical packs into one green workflow, decides the payment provider, and lets three commercial findings be dispositioned. Handover item 13.
  2. Meta Business verification and two test WABAs. Turns the Meta column from red to amber and lets thirteen Pending-live stories be verified. Handover items 1 and 11.
  3. Approve v0.47.0. Fixes the 1 October pricing change before it lands and adds the gate-matrix test both reviewers asked for. Plan presented 20 Sep 10:39.
  4. Pricing against the market ladder (handover 2) and the payment provider (handover 12) follow from the first two.
