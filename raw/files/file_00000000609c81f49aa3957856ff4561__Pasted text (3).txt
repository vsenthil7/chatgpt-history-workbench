The final review must answer:

> Can TalkProof progressively reduce its dependence on expensive general-purpose frontier intelligence while simultaneously increasing its company-specific intelligence, reliability, evidence quality and operational autonomy?

> Does every additional autonomous capability produce measurable economic value greater than its intelligence/compute/operational cost?

The objective is NOT maximum AI usage.

The objective is:

> **Minimum necessary expensive intelligence + maximum verified outcome.**

And ultimately:

> **TalkProof should become progressively more knowledgeable about itself, progressively cheaper to operate intelligently, progressively safer to change, and progressively harder to reproduce.**

One caution I deliberately put into the prompt: don't assume `CRAG → CLM → LLM` for every request. Sometimes **deterministic code → answer** should finish the request before any RAG/model is touched. That is cheaper *and* more reliable.
